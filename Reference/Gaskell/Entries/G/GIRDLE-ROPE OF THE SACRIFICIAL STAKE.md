A symbol of the higher emotions which encompass, as it were, the ray of the Divine Life (stake). ‘*He (the Sacrificer) yirds the stake with a triple rope for three-fold is food, and food means cattle; and there is the father and mother, and what is born is the third, therefore he girds it with . triple rope.'’—Sata. Brah, Ill. 7, 20 The Higher Self emanates the higher emotions as sustenance for the soul, for the buddhic emotions (cattle) nourish the soul. The triad of father, mother, and son, represents mind and emotion producing the indwelling Self born on the mental plane (son of mind). See BrrtH (second), CaTrie, CHIP, Foop, Sacrirtcern, STAKE, ToP-RING.
