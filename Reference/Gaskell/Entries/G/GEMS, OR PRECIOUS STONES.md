## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

Symbols of virtues and high qualities of mind and emotion.

> "In that city (of Kapila), shining with the splendour of gems, darkness like poverty could find no place."—Buddha-Karita, Bk. I. S.B. of E.

In the centred Self on the higher planes is perfect bliss, emanating the highest qualities of Love and Wisdom. Ignorance (darkness) or privation (poverty), could not possibly enter into manifestation at this stage of forthgoing towards involution.
