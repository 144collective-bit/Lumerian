In the cycle of involution this signifies the condition of the lower nature which is required in order that the Soul may take birth in it. ‘* Then one day by the king’s permission the queen, having a great longing in her mind, went with the inmates of “ gynseceum into the garden Lumbini.” Buddha-Karita, Bk.
