A symbol of the spiritual nature of love and wisdom. The ‘fruit ’’ of the ‘“‘vine,’’ or ‘‘ tree of life.’’ ** The common people like to mix water with the wine of their belief. They usually mix a great deal: sometimes so much as to drown the precious drop from the ‘calix inebrians,’ ‘the chalice of the grapes of God.’ But it is still there, potent in its divine virtue to slake the thirst of human nature for a good transcending sense; to lift eyes, dim with tears and dull with pain, towards the Beatifie Vision ; to heal and strengthen feet sore and weary from the rough ways of earth, for the steep ascent of Heaven."’—W. 8S. Luxzy, The Great Enigma, p. 288. See Cana, Cup, FRurr OF THE SPIRIT, Hanoina, INTOXICATION, VivE, WINE.
