```text
This signifies the process of Evolution whereby the Archetypal Man (Christ incarnate) becomes ‘‘ dismembered,’’ or ‘‘scattered,’’ for the sake of the incarnating egos (human monads), and so the One brings forth the many qualities for the use of each human soul according to its stage of development.—See Empedocles, verse 60 ; also see 1 Cor. xii. 12-31; Epu. i. 10. See Ano. MAN, DISMEMBEBMENT, EVOLUTION, GEOMETRY, INCARNATION.
```
