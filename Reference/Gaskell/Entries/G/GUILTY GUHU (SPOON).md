### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-guilty-guhu-spoon-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the causal-body on the higher mental plane. “The guhu (spoon) and upabhrit (spoon) are looked u as yoke-fellows, they being the two horses that are supposed to convey the sacrifice (and consequently the sacrificer himself) to the world of the Gods.”—J. Eacriina, S. B. of E., Vol. XII. p. 68. The causal-body, the seat of the individuality, and the mental-body the seat of the personality, are closely allied, and it is through the mental activities (horses) of both, working together for evolution, that the lower nature is sacrificed and the consciousness raised to the higher world wherein the ideals (Gods) are realised. The spiritual ego is conveyed heavenwards by means of the individuality and the personality operating upon the mental plane of the soul. See BUTTER, CAUSAL-BODY, DHRUVA, EVOLUTION, GHEE, Gops, HEAVEN, INCARNATION, INDIVIDUALITY, PERSONALITY, SACRIFICER, UPABHRIT.
```
