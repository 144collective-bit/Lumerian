Symbolic of opinions, prejudices, confining notions, narrowness of ideas, habits of low thought, literalism, and the ‘‘ higher criticism ’’ of scriptures. These limitations of mind pertain to the lower mental plane, and enclose the ego in shells of mental matter, often very difficult and painful to get rid of. They have to be broken up by the advent of life and truth from the Spirit within. ‘*And he (blind Bartimeus), casting mie A his garment, sprang up, and came to Jesus.’’—MAaARK x. 50. The aspiring mind rises and “ casts off his garment” because he has, first, to make the effort to rise of his own accord, and stand (as it would 302 GARODMAN appear); and second, he must needs cast away his old prejudices and confining opinions, symbolised by the garment, before he can go to the Christ within. ** Proclus has it : The soul, on descending into the body, forsakes unity, ‘ and around her, from all sides, there grow multiform kinds of existence and manifold garments ;' ‘love of honour is the last garment of souls’; and when, in mounting up, ‘we lay aside our passions and garments which, in coming down, we had put on, we must also strip off that last garment, in order that having become (entirely) naked, we may establish ourselves before God, having made ourselves like to the divine life.’ '°—From uae, Mystical Element, Vol. II. p. 98. See Buinp Man, CLOTHING, INSPIRATION, NAKEDNESS, PRIESTS AND ELDERS.
