### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-golden-soles-made-by-hephaestus-which-enable-the-gods-to-tread-earth-and-sea-and-ocr-body`

```text
Symbolic of the functionings of the causal-body, which the Divine Mind contrived to enable the Ideals (Gods) to operate upon the lower planes through the vehicles connected with the causal-body. These vehicles of the ego are upon the physical plane (earth), the astral plane (sea), and the mental plane (air). The ‘‘ soles ’’’ or ‘‘ shoes ’’ signify power to advance in progression. By means of the Ideals which arouse aspiration, the soul grows towards perfection. See Arn, CAUSAL-BODY, EARTH,GODS, Hepuaistos, Ska, SHOES.
```
