Symbolic of unbalanced mental conditions at a very early stage of evolution. These having had some glimpses of truth and higher emotion presented to them, had rejected the higher light, not being ready to receive DBOPNER, NANNA, GAGATI it. These low conditions were doomed to extinction in the soul, as fuller mentality evolved. See Grants, NEPHILIM.
