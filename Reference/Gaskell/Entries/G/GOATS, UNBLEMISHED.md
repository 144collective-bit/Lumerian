A symbol of sincere faith tending upwards (desire exalted). ‘* Whether haply Phoebus Apollo may be willing, having partaken of the savour of lambs and unblemished goats, to avert from us the pestilence.’’—Jliad, Bk. I. Whether it will be that the Higher Self, having accepted the contribution of the lower self in the thought of loving service (savour of lambs) and sincere faith (unblemished goats), will afford the lower self the means for overcoming the adverse conditions which surround it.
