Symbolic of a buddhic sub-plane to which the consciousness rises ere it becomes one with the Self. ** And my toga of brilliant colours I cast around me, in ite whole breadth. I 303 GATES clothed myself therewith, and ascended to the gate of salutation and homage ; I bowed my head and did obecisance to the majesty of my Father who had sent it to me.’”’-—Hymn of the Soul, GNoSsTic. And also my “toga,” the causalbody of variegated qualities, did I now assume to its complete extent of development. I was able to clothe myself in my bright vestures, so that I was not found naked ; and then did I ascend to the portal of reverence, filled with a sense of the majesty of God—the Creator and Sustainer of the Cosmic Scheme. I bowed my mind and did homage to his greatness ; and I reverenced my Father from whom had emanated such splendid endowments for the soul. See BUDDHIC PLANE, Door (higher), GARMENT, NAKEDNESS, MJRAIMENT, Rose, Toga.
