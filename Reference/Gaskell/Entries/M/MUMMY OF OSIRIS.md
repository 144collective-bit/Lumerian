A symbol of the archetypal astrophysical body containing the personality which is an expression of the Individuality in the cycle of evolution. ‘* The emblamed body of Osiris was the first mummy that was made in the world.” ~——~PLUTARCH, Isis and Osiris. The making of the mummy of 519 MUSCULAR the archetypal man is the fashioning of the astro-physical body, which is now to be perpetuated; the ideal physical vehicle having at length been built up as a generative pattern for heredity in the human race.
