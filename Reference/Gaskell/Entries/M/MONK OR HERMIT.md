```text
A symbol of an ego who “‘ retires alone into the deser~ to lead a religious life,’’ that is, who withdraws into the soul’s inner mental being, intent upon consciously developing his nature in accordance with the Divine law. To this end he ceases for a period from external activities and incarnation. See Ascetic, ARHAT, ASRAMAS, MOON Cuurnon (four), Hermit, MENDICANT, Nouns, Orpers, Sarnts, SUFFERING, TRI-RATNA.
```
