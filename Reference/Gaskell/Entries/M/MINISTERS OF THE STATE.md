A symbol of the cosmic agents through which the laws of the Supreme are carried out. “The Kingly office was not mere pageantry: nor were the functions of state ministers empty show. Good government was established by rulers, and correct instruction diffused among the people.”—Chinese Mythology. At this period of the Golden Age of Involution no _ self-consciousness has been arrived at,—that is, things could not be other than they are centrally decreed. Necessity is at this stage imperative. The “ Kingly office” is the Divine Will; and the ‘‘state ministers"? are those agents through whom the Divine Will is executed. During Involution all the principles or laws of the universe are, as it were, laid down and worked out in an ideal and perfect archetypal system, and these serve for the guiding of the Evolution which is to follow. ‘* First, the Prime Intelligence created He, through the which Four Ministers He made to be; He those Four the Columns Four of earth had dressed, whereby Fixture, Elevation, Traction, Rest, Water, Fire, Air and Earth He named them there, and He made of them this kingdom’s bases fair. Four domains unto the Four did God decree’’ (Ghart Name of Ashig).—E. J. W. Ginn, Hist. of Ottoman Poetry, Vol. I. p. 188. From the Absolute there emanated the Creative Mind, who established MINOS below atma the four principles of manifestation to be ‘ ministers” of the Divine Life outpoured upon the four planes formed for their activities.
