The rational and moral order in the conduct of life, under coercion of law and conscience. This order precedes the order of Wisdom and Love ,—the Christ. ‘Now viewing the institutions of the dispensation brought in by Moses as typical, we look at them in what may be called their secondary aspect, we consider then as prophetic symbols of the better things to come in the Gospel.’'—P. gta D.D., Typology, Vol. I. 5 See CONSCIENCE, COVENANT, DISPENSATIONS, JOHN BAPTIST, JUSTIFICATION, LAW OF MOSES, Moses, PROPHET, TESTAMENT, Woman (adultery).
