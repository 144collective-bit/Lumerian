A symbol of invariable law and justice applied to the activities of the lower planes. The law of Karma. ‘‘Maat was the wife of Thoth, and daughter of R&; she assisted at the work ofcreation. She is the goddess of absolute regularity and order, of moral rectitude, and of right and truth. Her emblem is a feather.’’ ‘‘ The gods live by madt, i.e, never-failing and unalterable regularity and order.”’—Bupaz, Book of the Dead, pp. 4, 8. The ideals (gods) have their raison d@’étre in the law of karma, for by it they are related to the lower planes, and as higher qualities they function in the growth of the lower.
