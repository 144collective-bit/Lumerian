This designates the active and positive aspect of the manifest Duality; that which acts upon the receptive, feminine side of nature, which is the form side. The Spirit or Life side of manifestation is masculine; the Matter or Form side is feminine. The highest manifest plane, Atma, is masculine, and is the plane of the Logos or Higher Self,—the ‘ Sungod’? under many names. The mental and astral planes are masculine; the buddhic and the physical are feminine. But it must be remembered that on all planes both aspects are present in greater or less degree. See FEMININE, MALE-FEMALE, MAN AND WIFE,
