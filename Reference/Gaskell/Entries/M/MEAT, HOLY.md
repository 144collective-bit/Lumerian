A symbol of intellectual food. Science and philosophy for the development of the mental faculties, and the acquirement of truth. **The man who fills himself with meat ai with the good spirit.’’— Vendidad, The mind (man) that assimilates knowledge is filled with that which is necessary for its growth. See ASHEMOGHA, EatTING, Mortar, Woop (holy).
