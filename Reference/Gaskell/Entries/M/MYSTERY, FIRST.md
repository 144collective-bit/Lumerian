A symbol of purity, gentleness, and obedience to the calls of duty and love. *‘The disciples thought that all had been revealed (by Jesus), and that the First Mystery—the Father in the likeness of a dove,—was the gnosis of all gnoses.”’ —Pistis Sophia. The disciplined qualities mistakenly thought that revelation and finality are synonymous. This is an error into which many more or leas enlightened people fall. The First Mystery as a dove is an emblem of simplicity, meekness, and willingness to descend to everyday duties of life. “The way to the eternal glory lies through a right relationship to the common things of life, not in despising and forsaking them. The man of faith is he who discerns the spirituality inherent in material existence, and _ lives accordingly. And to him who so lives God reveals the truth that everything earthly is more and other than it seems ; is, in fact, divine.’—R. J. CampsELt, Serm., Steadying Power of Faith. See Dove, Gnosis, Mrpst.
