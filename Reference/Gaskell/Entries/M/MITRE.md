An emblem of Divine rule over the lower planes. It has the same meaning as the ‘‘ crown of Lower Egypt.’’ The cleft across represents a mouth opening upwards, which signifies the expression of the Divine Will in the lower realms. ‘*The mitre, the symbol of authority of the Jewish high priest, is also the attribute of bishops and archbishops. Cardinals anciently wore them.”’—F. Hutme, Symboliem, etc., p. 151. **The mitre denotes Divine Wisdom.” —SwWEDENBORG, Are. Cel., n. 9999. See BrsHop, Crown, Moura.
