## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

A symbol of the inward stimuli of the desire-mind (kama-manas) urging the soul towards fresh experiences of the lower life.

> "When Zoroaster had attained his seventh year, the inimical Durasrobo and Bratrok-rish still continue to connive against him; to harass and assail him. By magic practices they endeavour to daunt his spirit, and they attempt to destroy his body by poison."—A. V. W. Jackson, *Zoroaster*, p. 32.

The "seventh year" signifies the growth of the soul upon the lower planes. The conspiracy is the means by which the soul is ensnared through the desire-nature and the lower mind which intensifies the strength of the desires. The "magic practices" are the untried routes of the lower nature over which the soul must needs pursue its way, and the way appears appalling and interminable. The "poisoning" signifies the immediate result of the severer experiences upon the lower planes whereon the effects of the coarser functionings of the lower self have all the appearance of effects of poison.

See ASTRAL-BODY, BLACK MAGICIANS, BRATROK-RISH, DURASROBO, [Fay?], KAMA, SERPENT (subtil), SEVEN, YEAR, ZOROASTER.
