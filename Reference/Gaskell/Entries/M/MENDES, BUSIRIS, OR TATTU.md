A symbol of a centre of activity on the lower planes. See Ciry, Tartu. MENDICANT, RELIGIOUS :-—~ A symbol of a mental quality which lives by love (alms) received from other qualities. ** A man is not & mendicant (Bhikshu) simply because he asks others for alms; he who adopts the whole law is a Bhikshu. not he who only begs. He who is above good and evil, who is chaste, who with knowledge passes through the world, he indeed is called a Bhikshu.’’— Dharmrnaapada, XIX. 266-7. A mental quality to live by love must have fulfilled the law of righteousness and be above the opposites. It must be pure and wise as it passes through the soul. See ALMSGIVING, BuiksHU, Monk, SANNYASIN.
