## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-manvantara-ocr-body`

```text
The cycle of life in the lower nature, or the phenomenal universe of the solar system. It includes both the period of involution and the period of evolution, in relation to the lunar and terrene chains of globes. The mahamanvantara is the cyclic period of the whole solar system with all its planetary chains. See ABRAXAS, CIRCLE OF EXISTENCE, Day (great), Hour, Leviarmax, GarmMerr, | MARA Nieut, PLANETARY CHAIN, PRALAYA, SaBBATHS, SaMSaRA, SERPENY (ananta), To«s, Tuat, YEAR, ZODIAC.
```
