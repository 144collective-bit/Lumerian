## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-manas-manasic-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of mind, mentality, or the mental plane, below the buddhic plane. ** Place thy manas in me (Krishna), into Me let thy understanding enter ; then without doubt thou shalt abide in Me hereafter.’’— Bhagavad Gita, XII. 8. Devote thy mind (manas) to the service of the Most High, and harmonise thy higher emotions with the inner Self, then, O soul! thou shalt be liberated from the life below, and be united unto God for ever. See BakHDHI, BREATIC WORLD, INTELLECT, LIBERATION, Manat, MaisHaNn, Sworps, THOTH.
```
