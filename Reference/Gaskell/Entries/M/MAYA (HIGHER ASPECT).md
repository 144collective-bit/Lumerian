The Truth is said to proceed from the Self, inasmuch as it bears witness to the Self-existent, and is the concrete expression of that which transcends the manifest. The *‘ splendour of the sun ”’ is referred to, since upon these high planes the Self is above temporalities which produce ** shadow,” matter. Truth or Wisdom upon this exalted plane, or condition of being, is the supreme fact of all facts, which testifies to the Selfexistent,—the Being of all beings.
