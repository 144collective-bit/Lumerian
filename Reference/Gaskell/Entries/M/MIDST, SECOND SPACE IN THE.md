A symbol of the buddhi-manasic plane. ** He is a God who hath found the words of the Mysteries of the second space in the midst.’’—Booke of the Saviour. He has attained Godhood in the germ who has arrived at the stage referred to, which is the higher emotion-mental plane. This _ illustrates the saying,—‘‘I said, ye are Gods ”’ (Ps. lxxxii. 6; JOHN x. 34). ‘*That is called the midst which is inmost.’’——-SwEDENBORG, Arc. Cel. to Gen. iii. 8. See Mystery.
