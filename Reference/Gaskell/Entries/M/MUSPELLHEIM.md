```text
A symbol of the buddhic plane, the heaven-world. ** First of all there was in the southern region (sphere) the world called Muspell. It is light and hot, insomuch so that it is flaming and burning, and it is impervious to those who are foreign, and not indigenous there (or who have no home or heritage therein) ’’ (Prose Hdda).— Mautet, North. Antig., p. 401. The highest plane of the quaternary is the buddhic (fire) plane, which is imperceptible to the lower consciousness. The lower qualities (foreigners) cannot reach it, for they have no heritage therein. See BupDHIC PLANE, EDEN, FIRE, GARODMAN, HEAVEN, Kinqapom, PARADISE, SOUTH.
```
