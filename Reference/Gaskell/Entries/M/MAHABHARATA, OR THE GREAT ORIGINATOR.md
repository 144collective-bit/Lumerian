A symbol of the Logos or Higher ‘‘The (Great) Bharata, doubtless is Pragapati, for he sustains (bhar) this entire universe ;—‘that his great light shineth brightly, as the sun,—he who overthrew Piru in battles.’ Piru, by name, was an Asura—Rakshas: him Agni overthrew in battles.’’— Sata. Brdéh., VI. 8, 1, 14. The Higher Self, under many names, is the Life and Light of the universe, and the sun is his most general symbol. The desire-nature (Piru) acts in opposition to the higher nature, and is eventually overcome by the incarnate Self (Agni) after much conflict. See AGNI, BHARATA, CONFLICT, PraGApati, RAKSHASAS.
