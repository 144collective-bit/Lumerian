This is the name given to the periods called Manvantera and Pralaya or Dis590 J PRANA solution; one referring to the Active Periods of the Universe; the other to ite times of relative and complete Rest, whether they occur at the end of s Day, or an Age, or Life, of BrahmA.”” ** The essence of Darkness being Absolute Light, Darkness is taken as the spproprier: allegorical representation of condition of the Universe during Pralaya, or the term of Absolute Rest, or Non-being, as it appears to our fimite minds." ‘Nature runs down and disappears from the objective plane, only to reemerge, after a time of rest, out of the subjective, and to re-ascend once more. Our Kosmos and Nature will run down only to re-appear on a more perfect plane after every Pralaya.”—-BLavaTskKyY, Secret Doctrine, Vol. I. pp. 98, 173, 395.
