A symbol of the plane of atma, the abode of the Self. *‘ Thetis at once plunged from splendid Olympus into the profound sea. But Zeus on the other hand returned to his palace.” —Iliad, Bk. I. Nature being submerged in the plane of desire, as sense and instinct, the Self as Truth remained on the plane of atma. See Oxympus, Pyramip, THETIS, ZEUS. PALACES, HIGH-SOARING :-— Symbolic of an exalted condition of bliss on high planes. See AMARAVATI, KAPILA.
