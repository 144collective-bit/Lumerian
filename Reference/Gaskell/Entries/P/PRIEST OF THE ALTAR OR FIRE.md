## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-priest-of-the-altar-or-fire-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the spiritual mind or deeper intelligence which serves to direct the will. ‘*The very name for ‘priest’ in the Zend-Avesta, dthrava, is to be recognised in the atharvan of the Vedas, by which term a priest of Fire and Soma is meant.”’ —Hava, Essays on Rel. of Parsis, p. 212. See AaRON, ADVARYA, AGNIDHRA, Baxzsom, CHRYSE, FIRE pRIEsTS, PoNnairy, Pore, PRIEST. ATHWYA ATHWYA, THE SECOND MAN + A symbol of the Second or manifest Logos. ‘‘Athwysa was the second man who pre me (Homa) for the material world’? (Homa Yasht).—Haua, Essays. This signifies the forth-pouring of the Second Logos upon the higher planes, which prepared the Worldsoul (Homa) for its expression upon the lower planes. See Atua, Homa, SEtr¥.
```
