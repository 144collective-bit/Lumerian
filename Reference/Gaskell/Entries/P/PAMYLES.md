### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-pamyles-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the personality. **A certain person, named Pamyles, as he was fetching water from the temple of Jupiter at Thebes, heard a voice commanding him to _ proclaim aloud that ‘The good and great King Osiris was then born.’’’—PLurarcH, Isis and Osiris, § 12. This signifies that as the soul searches for truth in its inner nature, the Divine voice from within announces to the lower self that the Lord Christ is born in the mind to be the soul’s ruler and Saviour. See BreTH OF OsIRis, Kina (great), Osrnis, Prrsonatiry, SON OF MAN, TEMPLE, THEBES.
```
