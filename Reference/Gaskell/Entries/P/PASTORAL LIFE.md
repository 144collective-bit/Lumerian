A symbol of the activities of the desire-mind, and the disciplining of the lower emotions. **Fuh-he recommended pastoral employmenta for the people, and the rearing of domesticated animals.’’—Chénese Mythology, Kipp, China. This is a reference to the dawn of the intelligence, and the cultivation of the lower faculties in such a way that they might become of use to the soul. See AN™ALS, FUH-HE, JABAL, PAN.
