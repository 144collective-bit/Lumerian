A symbol of the Higher Self. “The kingdom of heaven is like unto @ man that is a merchant seeking goodly pearls: and having found one pearl of great price, he went and sold all that he had, and bought it.”—-Mar. xiii. 45, 46. The “‘man” is the mind or the ego. The pearl is the Higher Self. The possessions with which the ego parted are the sense-nature and the things of the lower planes, in exchange for which he may inherit all things; —the real in substitute for the apparent and illusory. The ego surrenders the lower in order to secure the higher. See Krxapom, Man, ParasBrz.
