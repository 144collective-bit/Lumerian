A symbol of the soul’s experience of the lower nature, which in going through it, eventually proves the truth of that which is above. “The sea Puitika, whose waters are urified before going back to their fcavenly seat—the sea Vouru-kasha, the waters above (Mount) Alborj.”"— Vendidad, V. The ‘‘sea Puitika” is a symbol of experience by which the soul learns truth, and which ultimately through QUALITIES, PURGATORY purification in the higher mind proves to the soul the truth underlying existence, thereby passing into the ocean of Reality (Vouru-kasha) above the mount of aspiration (Alborj). ‘* As the air is the element in which man moves, and yet again the element of life which is present within the man: 80 the Pneuma-Christ is for St. Paul both the Ocean of the Divine Being, into which the Christian, since his reception of the Spirit, is plunged,” and in which he disports himself, ‘‘and a stream which, derived from that Ocean, is specially introduced within his individual hfe.’—H. J. Hotrzmann, Lehrbuch, etc., Vol. II. p. 79. See ALBORDJ, EXPERIENCE, OCEAN, PRAYER, River, Tistrya, VovURvKASHA, WATER.
