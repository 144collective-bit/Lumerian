A symbol of reliance upon the Higher Self within the soul. ** Prosperity, the pillow of the couch of Brahman.” —Kaush. Upantshad. This signifies the sense of security which is the result of complete trust in the Higher Self and a knowledge of its Divine nature,—a state of consciousness which implies complete mistrust of all that is less than Divine, or of the nature of illusion. See BraumMa, CUSHION, PROSPERITY.
