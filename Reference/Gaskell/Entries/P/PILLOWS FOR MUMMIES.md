```text
A symbol of the reliance of the purified soul upon the Supreme Spirit. ** Pillows of wood, alabaster and stone which are placed under the heads of mummies to ‘lift them up.’ ’’—Bupag, The Mummy, p. 261. As embalmment signifies purification of the personality, complete trust is placed in the Higher Self (Osiris) to raise the consciousness to the higher planes. These pillows often had figures inscribed upon them, such as two lotus flowers and an eye of Horus, which signify Divine wisdom, perception, and expression; or a figure of the god Bes,—a symbol of the outbreathing and inbreathing Absolute Spirit. See EmpaLMucene, Mummy, Osinis, Uscnar.
```
