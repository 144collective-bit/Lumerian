A symbol of the measures taken in the Divine scheme for the raising of the qualities and souls in response to their aspirations, so that when they are perfected they may have 697 PROPHETS eternal life. The raising of the qualities is through the ‘ Holy Spirit of promise,’’ that is, through the functioning of buddhi.—See EPH. i. 13, 14; Rom. iv. 13-16; Hes. vi. 12-17; 2 Peter i. 3, 4. See BuiaspHemy, Buiessine, Hory GHOST, TRANSMUTATION.
