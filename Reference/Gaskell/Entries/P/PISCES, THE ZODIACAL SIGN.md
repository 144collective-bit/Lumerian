A symbol of the twelfth period of the cycle of life, which is the final period of evolution on the buddhic plane. At the close of the cycle, the Christ-soul (Jesus the ‘* Fish °’) becomes one with the Christ (Higher Self the ‘‘ Fish ’’), or the redeemed souls become one with the Redeemer, —the lower consciousness unites with the higher (the two fishes). ** Possibly the ‘Fish’ symbol of early Christianity may be explained by Peeves. On the catacomb lamps there are two fish, one swallowing the other.’’—A. oly ea The Old Test., etc., Vol. L p. 76. ‘** Pisces, the last of the signs, is sented by two fishes bound ‘by a cond Love and deep compassion is the real nature of Pisces.’’-—J. H. VanstTonz, On the Zodiac. See AMENT, AUSHEDAR, F isu (great), Gemini, Iapraora, JEsus (fish), SunMOFIONLESS, ZODIAC.
