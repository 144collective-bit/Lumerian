Symbolic of five energies of conscious life in the soul, both higher and lower. “These fires of prina, which are on the watch in sleep, are themselves five in number, viz. prdna, apdna, vydna, samdna, uddna, and they are mentioned together both earlier and later numberless times, and employed in the most varied allegories, without its being possible to obtain a clear and consistent explanation of them.”—DerussEn, Phil. of Upanishads, p. 275. ‘The prina makes its exit upwards, the apfina downwards, and carries off the excrements.’’—Ibid., p. 279. The préna signifies the Divine life which flows upwards or inwards. The apdna, the “‘ wind of digestion ” and having an “evil odour,” signifies the lower life of desire which flows downwards or outwards. ‘‘ The prina dwells in the heart, the apdna in the bowels.” —Jbid. ** Vyana, ‘interspiration,’ is ‘the bond between prana and apfna.’ The conception of it is accommodated to that of apfna. ... Samdna, ‘all-breathing,’ bears the name because it ‘leads to union.’. . . Uddna, or ‘ up-breathing,’ conducts the soul from the body at death, while already in deep sleep it guides to Brahman.’’—Jbid., p. 280. Vyana is the life of the lower emotions, which is modifiable by both PRAYER the higher and the lower energies. PRAYERS — laws as definite as the laws of equilibrium | Saména is the life of the higher emotions, it ‘‘ assimilates the food” and ‘* dwells white as milk in the navel,”’ —that is, the higher emotions give sustenance to the causal-body, and purify the lower nature. Udéna is the life of aspiration, which leads the ego upward from its submersion in the lower nature, and raises it to union with the Higher Self. The five life energies of the soul are said to be ‘‘ organs of nutrition,” for they serve to give sustenance to the causal-body (manas), which grows from latency to actuality in the many human souls. The causal-body is potentially perfect, but in each human being it is actually defective and incomplete, and having to be nourished or built up by means of the life processes on the lower planes. See Arn, BREATH, CAUSAL-BODY, CHarnioT, Foop FOR sOUL, HEART, HIGHER AND LOWER NATURES, Lazakvus, LincamM, MILK, NAVEL, Sanu, SENSE ORGANS, UNION, VISCERA, VITAL AIRS.
