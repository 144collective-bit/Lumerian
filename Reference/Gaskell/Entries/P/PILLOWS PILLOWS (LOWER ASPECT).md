A symbol of reliance upon the lower nature, and contentment with the things of the worldly life. **Behold, I (the Lord) am against your pillows, wherewith ye there hunt the souls to make them fly, and I will tear them from your arms, and will let the souls go.’’—Ezex. xiii. 20. The Self condemns contentment with the things of the lower life, which is active in driving out the higher qualities (souls); and points out that trouble and suffering will supervene to destroy contentment, and so allow the higher qualities to expand. ‘**It is said by the Prophet, ‘Woe to those that sew pillows under every elbow of the hand, and make cushions under the head of every age’ (Ezxx. xiii. 18). For a ‘pillow’ is put for this, that we may read the easier. Therefore whoever flatters persons doing wrongly is putti a pillow under the head or the elbow o one lying, so that the man that should have been chidden on account of ain, being stayed up therein by commendations, should rest at his ease.”’— St. Grecory, Morals on the Book of Job, Vol. II. p. 321.
