A symbol of kingship—supremacy over the lower nature. ** Yellow is a good example of the fact the student in colour symbolism must by no means overlook, that a colour may be employed either in a good or a bad senso. ... It is the colour of jealousy and treason, and Judas is often represented in old glass painting in a yellow robe. Where, however, it is represented by gold, or is a substitute for it, it signifies love, constancy, dignity, wisdom.” —F. E. Hume, Symbolism, etc., p. 20. See Cortours, Goxip, Poison (yellow).
