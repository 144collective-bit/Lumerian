A symbol of the Higher Triad or Divine Ray—Atma-buddhi-manas. ‘* This cord, which is a coil of three t 8, is worn over the left shoulder and allowed to hang down diagonally across the body to the right hip, and the wearing of it by the three twice-born classes was the mark of their second birth.”’—Mon. WILLIAMS, Indian Wisdom, p. 246. The wearing of the triple cord signifies the acceptance by the ego of the means of progress in the spiritual life.
