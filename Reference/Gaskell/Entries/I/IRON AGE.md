A symbol of the present period of consciousness, wherein the lower mind is rendered active through the vibrations from the physical plane. ‘* And that branch (period) which was mixed with iron is the evil sovereignty of the demons with dishevelled hair of the race of Wrath (Aeshm).’’—Bahman Yast, Ch.
