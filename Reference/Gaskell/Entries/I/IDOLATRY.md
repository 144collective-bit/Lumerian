For there are idols of the mind which take the place of visible images: idols of tradition, of language, which come between us and God; idols of the temple too, in which d and evil seem to be inseparably lended, and the good is near and present, and the evil is only recogni in some fatal but distant consequences.'’— aoe Serm., Image of the Invisible See PRIESTS AND ELDERS, TRADITION.
