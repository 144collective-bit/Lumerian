A symbol of the higher mental plane which is below the buddhic (heaven). ‘*In the id Texta it is always assumed that the flat slab of iron which formed the sky, and therefore the floor of the abode of the gods, was rectangular, and that each corner of it rested upon a illar.... At a later period, the four quarters of heaven were believed to be under the direction of four gods, and the four pillars of the sky were poetically descri as the four sceptres which they held in their hands.”’—Bupcr, Gods of the Egyptians, Vol. I. pp. 156-7. 401 Isis See CEILING or sky, CHILDREN OF HORUS, FIRMAMENT, FOUR BEINGS, Gops, Hanp, Lapprr, PANTHER, PILLARS, QUARTERS, REN, SCEPTRE, SKY.
