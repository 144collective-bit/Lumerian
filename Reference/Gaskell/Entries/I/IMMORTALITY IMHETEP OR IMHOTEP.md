A symbol of the Higher Self manifesting upon the higher mental! plane. ‘‘The name of Imhetep signifies ‘ he who comes in peace’; the Greeks transcribed it Imfdthes, and likened him to Asklepios. .. . He cured men of their ases by means of medicine and of ic. He was generally regarded as a learned deity."-—WIEDEMANN, Rel. of Egyptians, p. 139. ** Imhotep was reputed the first hierophant; he it was who recited the liturgy of the dead, and the magic formulz which restored health to the sick and raised the dead to life.
