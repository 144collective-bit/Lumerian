Symbolic of the objects of sense regarded as an end in themselves. ‘‘Idols graven in stone’’ signify the ideals inverted,—the mistaking of the shadow for the substance, due to the sense of separateness, and supposing the part to be the whole. ‘‘Ashamed be all they that serve ven images, that boast themselves of idols.”—Ps. xcvii. 7.
