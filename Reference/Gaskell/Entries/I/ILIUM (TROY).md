```text
A symbol of the causal-body, the abode of the causal-Self, as a centre for the higher mental qualities and emotions. The personality (Achilles) is represented as seeking to capture the heavenly city by violence; that is,—through the striving of the personality for perfection, the consciousness is raised to the higher mind, which involves the death of the personality. ‘‘In the centre of the world was Turkland with its capital Troy. In this Turkland there were twelve kingdoms, and a chief king, who was Priam.”— Preface to Prose Edda. In the centre of the soul, or midway between the higher and lower planes, is the higher mental plane, with the causal-body, the seat of the causalSelf (Priam). On the higher mental plane are represented the twelve stages of the Life Cycle (Zodiac). See ACHILLES, CAUSAL-BODY, CrTy OF GOD, HELEN, MENIS, PERSONALITY, Priam, SHIPS OF GREEKS, TROJANS, Troy.
```
