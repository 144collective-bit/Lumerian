Symbolic of mental definitions or compartments in which the lower mind formulates its conceptions. ‘** Peter said unto Jesus, Lord, it ie good for us to be here: if thou wilt, I will make here three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah.’—Mar. xvii. 4. The unenlightened reason or lower mind (Peter) possesses the tendency to formulate, define, and _ confine, ideas in forms of thought. Therefore, when the lower mind becomes aware of the reason and ethical (Moses), the emotional (Elijah), and the spiritual (Christ), it wishes to keep each in a special ‘‘ water-tight " (truth-isolated) compartment (tabernacle) of its own devising.
