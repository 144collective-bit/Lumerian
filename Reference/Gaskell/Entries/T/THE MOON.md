```text
A symbol of the Personality on the lower mental plane of consciousness. **Geburah or Pachad is sometimes used in a good and sometimes in an evil sense; the world is based thereon —in the sense that severity is indisble—but it could not subsist without Mercy (Chesed). It is said also to be the repentance of God, and it seems even to connect with Samael. It was by Geburah that Jerusalem was destroyed.”—A. E. Waits, Secreés Doctrine of Israel, p. 40. The Personality acts well or il according as it chooses the good or evil course. The choice of evil entails suffering according to karmic law. The Individuality (Chesed) projects the Personality into conditions calculated to correct some of the defects of the Personality. The Divine Love aids the Personality by giving it improved means of expression. The Personality when ruled by desire P AIRIKA (Samael) obscured and rendered inoperative the higher nature (Jerusalem) of the soul. See Fatt, GoD REPENTING, HESED, JHOKuMAH, INDIVIDUALITY, JERUSALEM, JUDGMENT DAY, Karma, PanDoRA, PERSONALITY, PRODIGAL, SEPHImotTH, Svorrerinc, Sun, Moon, ‘T1IPsERETH.
```
