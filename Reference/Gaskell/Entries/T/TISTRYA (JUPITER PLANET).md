A symbol of the Ego who initiates, directs, and governs qualities on the mental plane, in order to awaken ideals. ‘** Tistrya is called the giver of wealth ; his lustre is red, and of great beauty.”’— Hava, Essays, p. 200. Tistrya is a symbol of the Ego or causal Self. He is the “giver of wealth,” because all that is gotten comes through the Self seated in the causal-body.
