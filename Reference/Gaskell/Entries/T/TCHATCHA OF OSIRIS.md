A symbol of the quaternary,— the four planes below atma. ‘* May there be no opposition to me from the Tchatcha.’’—Papyrus of Ani. (The Tchatcha of Osiris were Mestha, Haépi, Tuamautef and Qebhsennuf.}— Bupnae, Book bf the Dead, p. 25. The soul aspires to that which is beyond the opposition on the lower planes. See CHILDREN OF HORUS, OSIRIS, QUATERNARY.
