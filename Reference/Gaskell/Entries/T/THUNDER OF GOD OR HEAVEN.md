A symbol of the expression of the Divine Will in pain and terror on the lower planes. ‘““The Lord also thundered in the heavens, and the Most High uttered his voice; hailstorms and coals of fire.”’— Ps. xviii. 13. “The voice of the Lord is upon the waters; the God of glory thundereth, even the Lord upon many waters.”’— Ps. xxix, 3. Through the sufferings and terror on the lower planes (the waters), forms of thought, erroneous opinions, and unruly passions, are disrupted and made to give place to better states. ‘* But I (Thetis) will go mveself to snowclad Olympus to tell this thy saying to OF THUNDERBOLT Zeus, whose joy is in the thunder, if eat he may hearken to me.’’— liad, Bk. I. Nature, however, contemplates soaring to the heights to impart the knowledge which has been gathered to the Higher Self, who rejoices in the terrors which must beneficially befall the lower Self ia its progress.
