A symbol of the lower nature in which the souls are confined as they pass through the cycle of life. ‘** Poeta describe Tartarus as the place in the lower world in which the spirits of wicked men are punished for their crimes; and sometimes they use the name as synonymous with Hades, or the lower world in general.’’—Smith’s Clase. Dict. The “spirits of wicked men” are the unperfected souls which, after each incarnation and subsequent interval, must descend again to the lower life to undergo development and purification amid conditions of strife and suffering. See Bonpace, Capriviry, Dusaku, Eros, Hapres, HELL, Svurreria, Tarrvu, Tuat, UNDERWORLD.
