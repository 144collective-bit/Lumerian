A symbol of illusion which dominates the lower mind. “The man that can strive against tho wicked tyrant and smite him on the head.”’ —Vendidad, IV. The “‘man” is the mind or ego _ that assimilates knowledge and thereby dispels illusion. The “ smiting on the head ”’ is the effort made to overcome illusion under its most subtle forms, such as pride. See AsHEMOGHA, ATREUS, HiIPPoPOTAMUS, ILLUSION.
