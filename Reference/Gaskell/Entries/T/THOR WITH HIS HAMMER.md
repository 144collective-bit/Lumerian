A symbol of the higher mind wielding the will (Mjolner). ‘* Thor, as mediator between heaven and earth, had the general command of this terrestrial atmosphere.’’—MAaLuet, Hist. de Danemarc, Intro. The higher mind is the medium of communication between the higher and lower natures in the soul, and organises the mental plane (air). ** Thor stood beside the funeral pile of Balder, and consecrated it with hi hammer Mjéliner. Before his feet sprung up a dwarf called Lit; Thor kicked him with his foot into the fire, so that he also was burned.”’—Story of Balder, Edda. And from the mental plane with which the ego is identified the Self beholds the sacrifice and sanctifies it with self-conscious experience. And now rises a ‘‘dwarf,’’ the symbol of error, which the mind consigns to the purifying process which is to yield spiritual fruition. See CREMATION, Hermes, MJOLNER, HORNE, THOTH.
