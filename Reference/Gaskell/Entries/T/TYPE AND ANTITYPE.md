The symbol and the truth symbolised. The figure in the lower illusion of what exists in the higher reality. ‘‘For, as the typical is Divine truth on a lower stage, exhibited by means of outward relations and terrestrial interesta, so when making the transition from this to the antitypical, we must expect the truth to appear on a loftier stage, and, if we may so speak, with a more heavenly aspect. What in the one bore immediate t to the bodily life, must in the other be found to bear immediate respect to the spiritual life."—P. Farrparrn, Typology of Scripture, Vol. I. p. 194. See CREATION, IMAGE, PROTOTYPES.
