A symbol of the critical faculty of the lower mind. ‘*This Talus had but one vein in his body, which ran from his neck to his heels, and was filled with ichor: a brass pin was fastened in this vein.”—Argonautic Expedition.—KrIcuHT.Ley, Mythology. This “‘ vein signifies the sutra or Divine ray. It runs from the higher mind plane downwards. By ‘“‘ichor™ is meant the consciousness—the reflection of the Divine spark. The “brass pin” is a symbol which may be taken as comparable to the content of consciousness, therefore its removal would indicate the dissolution of the lower nature.
