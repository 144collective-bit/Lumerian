An emblem of Divine rule over the higher mental and buddhic planes, exercised from the summit of Power and Truth. CROWN “Thou (Osiris) art stablished as king, the white crown is u thy head, and thou hast grasped in hy snide the crook and the whip.” The “crook” and the “flail” are symbols of shepherding and agriculture, over which, in their inner sense, the Divine Shepherd and Husbandman, the Higher Self (Osiris), presides. The tall white crown of Osiris symbolises the higher nature, and the mount of aspiration which has to be climbed in order to attain perfection. See CoME INTO THEE, FiarL, HvusBANDMAN, Kina, Mountain, OsInis, SERPEent (south), Smam, WHITE.
