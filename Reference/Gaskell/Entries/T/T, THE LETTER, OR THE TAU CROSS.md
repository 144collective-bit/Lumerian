A symbol of manifestation of the Divine Life. Tree of life. “Amo the earliest and simplest ideographic symbols in the Chines TAAROA language is one whch resembles our capital letter TT, signifying that which is ‘above,’ and the converse of this, the JT resting on its base ({) signifies that which is ‘below.’ In both cases ® point or a comma, as if a tongue of fire, is placed occasionally over an angel or divine messenger, to signify his more than human character. ... Hence the symbol Fi means to come down from above, where the dot or fiery tongue denotes a spark or flame descending from the upper world, which is signified by T. Hence again {| means the lower world, and the symbol je means to go up from below, or to ascend.”—BUNSEN, Angel Messiah, pp. 57, 58. The first process is that of Involution, wherein Spirit (fiery tongue) descends into Matter and supplies it with all potencies and qualities. The second process is that of Evolution wherein Spirit ascends from Matter, making manifest in multiplicity and diversity that which had been placed within it. In these two processes the the Divine Life finds expression in the invisible and the visible universes of a solar system.
