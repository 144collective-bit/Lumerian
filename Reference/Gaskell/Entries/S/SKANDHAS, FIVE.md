Symbols of the five elemental bodies or vestures of the soul, as instruments for the manifestation of spirit. “Every being is com of five constituent elements called Skandhas, which have their source in Upddana, and are continually combining, dissolving, and recombining, viz. (1) Form (rupa), i.e. the organised body. (2) Sensation (wedand) of pain or pleasure, or of neither, arising from contact of eye, ear, ete. with external objects. (3) Perception (sanna) of ideas through tho same sixfold contact. (14) Aggregate of formations (samkhGra), i.e. combination of properties or faculties or mental tendencies, fiftytwo in number, forming individual character and derived from previous existences, (5) Consciousness (tinndna) or thought. This fifth is the most important. It is the only soul recognised by Buddhists. Theoretically it perishes with the other Skandhas, but practically is continued, since its exact counterpart is reproduced in a new body.”—Mon. WILLIAMS, Buddhiem, p. 109. The bodies are taken in_ their receptive aspects towards the energies upon the several planes. Placing them in their proper order, the five bodies are,— Samkhéra = Buddhic ; Vinnéina = Causal; Sanna = Mental ; Vedan&é = Astral; Ripa = Physical. The Samkhdra is described as the potential higher causal-body containing the prototypes of all things and qualities that have to be evolved on the planes below. These prototypes are derived from the experience of previous life-cycles. They are perfect and complete (5 + 2 = 7), and guide the formation of the character of the units in the phenomenal world. The Vinndna represents the causal-body on the higher mental plane, the seat of the higher consciousness of humanity. The causal-body persiste throughout the cycle, and periodically reproduces the transitory lower bodies 696 SKINS at each incarnation. The Senna is the mental body which gives perception of ideas formed through vibretions from the lower activities. The Vedand is the astral body of the desires, feelings, and lower emotions which are aroused by the senses. The Ripa is the physical body by means of which action is effected. See Castres, CavusaL-Bopy, ConSCIOUSNESS, ELEMENTS (five), FIVE, IGNORANCE, PRANAS, PROTOTYPES, SAMKHARA, SENSE ORGANS, VESTURES.
