Symbols of the archetypal forms of the vehicles of mind, emotion, and higher desires. ‘** Thithor bring the seeds of all men, ~vomen, and cattle, which are the greatest aand best and finest on this earth.’’— Venslidad, II. The ‘‘ seeds,’’ which are the ideal type-forms brought over from the Previous cycle of manifestation,— the Lunar,—are now introduced on the terrene globe. These type-forms are on the mental, astral, and physical planes. See Beasts (sorts), CATTLE, DwELLINGS, KARSHVARES, LUNAR CHAIN, PLANETARY CHAIN, REGIONS, SHIP OF MANU.
