A symbol of the higher planes of buddhi and atma, while North is a symbol of the lower planes. ‘For upon the ‘south wind’ coming, the ‘north wind’ arising departs (Cant. iv. 16) when our old enemy, who had bound up our soul in inactivity, being expelled by the coming of the Holy Spirit, takes himself away. ...
