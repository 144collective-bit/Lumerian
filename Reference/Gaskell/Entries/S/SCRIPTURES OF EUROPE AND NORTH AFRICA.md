A symbol of the higher planes of buddhi and atma, while North is a symbol of the lower planes. ‘For upon the ‘south wind’ coming, the ‘north wind’ arising departs (Cant. iv. 16) when our old enemy, who had bound up our soul in inactivity, being expelled by the coming of the Holy Spirit, takes himself away. ... And thus the chambers of the South’ are those unseen orders of the Angels, and those unfathomed depths of the heavenly Country, which are filled with the heat of the Holy Spirit.”—Sr. Gnrecory, Morals on the Book of Job, Vol. I. p. 507. See Eayrt (higher), MusPELLHEIM, PHANG.
