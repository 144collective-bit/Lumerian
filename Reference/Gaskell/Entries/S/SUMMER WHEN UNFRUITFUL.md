A symbol of latency on the buddhic and higher mental planes. ‘“Ten months of winter are there; two of summer; and these latter are cold as to water, earth and plants; then as frost continues there is disaster.”’ —Vendidad, I. The long winter signifies that there occurs a period of manifestation on the lower planes before there is awakenment of the true Divine life upon them. The “two months of summer ”’ symbolise the state on the higher planes, described as_ being unfruitful, which indicates periods of latency when there is no manifesta| | | | 730 SUN tion of spiritual life on a globe of the planetary chain.
