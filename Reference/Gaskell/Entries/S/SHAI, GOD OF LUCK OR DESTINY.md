### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-shai-god-of-luck-or-destiny-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the material relation of the individuality, and refers to the personality. The physical life being to a great extent independent of the control of the soul itself, and under the divine guidance of the Lords of Karma, so the happenings which beset the physical body are not to be conceived of as anything but illusory, and, as it were, in the nature of chance, since they are but temporary combinations of conditions which are conducive to such growth as will afford the Divine life within the means for its expansion and development. See IMMORTALITY, JUDGMENT HALL, Kama, Karma, Morar, PERSONALITY, SHENIT, THEMIS.
```
