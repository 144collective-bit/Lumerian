This highest egal is served by all the faculties of the soul, which aasist it to reach the source of the soul, by raising the latter out of the sphere of inferior things. The spark aspires to the Absolute, to that unity outside of which there remains nothing ”’ (Eckhart).—Ursrrweea, Hist. of Philos., Eng. trans., Vol. I. p. 472. 712 SPARROWS ‘*The freedom from law and from all activity belongs, according to Eckhart, only to the ‘ little spark,’ but not to the faculties of the soul.
