Then the conscience speaks within the mind:—The degsire-nature has done amiss from the relative point of view, in that it has become the means of drawing the soul (Balder) downwards to the karmic planes (Hel), and that fall now serves to prevent it from rising to union with the Self. But under all the circumstances which were incidental to such @ fall,_-was not cause and effect, or compensation on higher planes, made to yield such results as were just and right withal? And the answer comes,—that throughout the soul’s evolution, perfect equilibrium between the higher and lower natures was maintained, and that the outcome of the whole process is stored away in the soul’s inner being.
