A symbol of the physical body, the external sheath of the soul. “All the (Indian) systems assign to each person two bodies: (a) an exterior or body (sthéla-sarira); (6) an interior or subtle body (sikshma-sarira or linga-sarira). The last is necessary as a vehicle for the soul when the gross body is dissolved. ... The Vedanta affirms the existence of a third body, called karana-sarira or causal body.’’—Mon, Wiuiams, Indian Wisdom, p. 64. These three bodies are the physical, the astral, and the causal bodies, functioning on the physical, the astral zz STIFFNECKED and the higher mental planes respectively.
