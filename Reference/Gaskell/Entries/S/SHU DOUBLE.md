A symbol of the Divine Will as outbreathing and inbreathing the Divine Life. ** Ka-Shu, i.e. the ‘double of Shu,’ is present in the Boat (of Afu-Ra) in order to supply the god with air.” —Bunag, Egypt. Heaven and Hell, Vol. III. p. 106. The Divine Will has the double capacity of outpouring and indrawing the Life of the lower planes, in order that the Incarnate Self (Afu-Ré) shall experience alternate periods of activity and of rest. See Arvu-RA, Boat, BREATH, Horizons (two), Lion-cop (double), Mosvsl. SHUN, CHINESE EMPEROR :-— A symbol of the Self operating on the buddhic plane during the cycle of Involution.
