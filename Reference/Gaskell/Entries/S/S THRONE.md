This symbolises the sublimated lower self, or ‘‘natural man,’’ through which the Higher Ego has attained its final liberation. Although this condition is ‘‘ unreal,’’ yet it has been absorbed into the Self; for the germ of this state exists and will persist through eternity ; it is transformed, but is still visible to the Self,—though now seen to be derived of It. Nothing in manifestation, or experience, is ever lost: there is an atom or centre on each plane which, when communicated with, will respond to vibrations which answer to the varied aspects of the Self. See AmMITAUGAS, ATOMS, ATOMS (permanent), BraHMa, GOLD (spot), Man (natural), MoNAaD OF LIFE, PILLow.
