This symbol connotes a cessation from activity. It implies limitation (Saturn) at the end of a sixth period; such as the sixth ‘‘ day ’’ of the period of involution, which closes that cycle and ushers in a pralaya (sabbath), after which state of latency (rest), a new manvantara of activity opens on the first ‘‘ day ”’ of the cycle of evolution. See Day, EVOLUTION, MANVANTARA, PRALAYA, SABBATH, Srx, SUNDAY.
