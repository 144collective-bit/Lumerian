A symbol of the discarding of the lower nature. “* And God said, Draw not nigh hither : put off thy shoes from off thy feet, for the place whereon thou standest is holy ground.”"—Exop. iii. 5. It is pointed out by the Spirit within that the moral nature (Moses) is incapable of furthering the growth of the soul beyond a certain level, when the love-nature (Christ) is evolved to bring the soul to perfection. See Law or moses, Mosaic, Moses.
