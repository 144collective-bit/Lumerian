## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

A symbol of the vibrations arising within the soul, in response to the activities of desire and sensation.

> "Then Apollo sat down apart from the ships, and sent among them an arrow, and terrible arose the clang of the silver bow."

Then the indwelling Self, seated in the lower vehicles of the soul, first of all receives, through the impacts from without, the means for the commencement of its evolution. The "clang" is the thrill of response from within.

See APOLLO, ARROWS, BOW (silver).
