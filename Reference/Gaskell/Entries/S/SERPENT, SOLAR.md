A symbol of the Higher Self, or manifesting Logos, as arising from the darkness of potentiality. “The symbol, then, of Agathodaimon as Logos was the Serpent of Wisdom, . .. in his sun-aspect, symbolised as a serpent with a lion’s head.”—G. R. S. Meap, T. G. Hermes, Vol. I. p. 480. “O dazzling Sun, who shed’st thy beams on all the world! Thou art tho mighty Serpent, the chief of all the Goda, O thou who dost possess Egypt’s beginning and the end of all the world"? (A prayer, Hermetic).—C. WrssELy, Papyrt | Grae. The Higher Self (sun) is adored as the Divine Life manifesting through the cycle of existence (the serpent) ; outpouring in the beginning on the mental plane (Egypt), and at the end indrawing from the lower nature (world). ** As there is a serpent below which is still at work in the world, so there is a sacred serpent above which watches over mankind in all the roads and pathways and restrains the power of the impure serpent’ (Zohar).—A. E. Warre, Secret Doctrine in Israel, p. 87. See AGATHADAIMON, LION, SERPENT (ananta), SuN, Wisposs (masc.).
