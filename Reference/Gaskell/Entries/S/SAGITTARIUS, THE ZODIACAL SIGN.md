## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

A symbol of the ninth period of the cycle of life, in which the lower mind is perfected through evolution, and therefore "dies." The Higher Self seated on the intelligence (horse) destroys with the "arrow" of the Spirit the lower qualities, thus enabling the lower consciousness to rise to the higher mind.
