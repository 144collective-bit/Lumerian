A symbol of the lower desires, instincts and astral forces; signifying an evil state,—depraved and unclean. ‘When Jesus the Son of Mary shall descend from the heavens upon your people as a just king, he will break the cross and will kill all the swine.’’—The Mishkat, Bk. XXIII. 6. When Christ, born in the soul of the purified lower nature, shall irradiate and raise the purified qualities (people), he will be victorious in the crossingover from the lower to the higher, and will put an end to all evil conditions and desires (swine). ‘‘ That the swine should be connected with the underground world of the dead is not surprising. We find the same connection in Keltic mythology (as in Babylonian). There, too, the swine are the cattle of Hades.’’—Sayce, Rel. of Egypt and Babyl., p. 358.
