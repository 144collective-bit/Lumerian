```text
A symbol of the buddhic or buddhimental plane, including a devachanic or nirvanic, state of consciousness. ‘* Few there are among men who arrive at the other shore (become Arhats) ; the other people here run up and down the shore. But those who, when the Law has been well preached to them, follow the Law, will pass across the dominion of death, however difficult to overcome.”’ —Dhammapada, Ch. VI. 85, 86. (Footnote by Max Miller: ‘‘ ‘ The other shore’ is meant for Nirvana, ‘this shore’ for common life.’’) Few of the mental qualities or souls have passed on to the higher 688 SHRINE planes; most are engaged in the activities of the lower planes, going to, and returning from the physical life. But when the Divine Law is understood and followed, then the souls are purified and they pass from death to life everlasting. See ARHATS, DEVACHAN, DHARMA, HrkososHi, Nirvana, Rart.
```
