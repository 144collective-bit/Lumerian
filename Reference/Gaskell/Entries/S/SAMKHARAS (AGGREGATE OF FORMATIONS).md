```text
A symbol associated with Dhamma (karma). It signifies usually the transitory mental type-forms which change with every personality. “‘Impermanent truly are the Sankharas, liable to origination and decease ; as they arose so they pass away; their disappearance is happiness.”’—Ibid., p. 251. See Consciousnsss, Forms, IenorANCE, Karma, SKANDHAS.
```
