A symbol of the causal-body perfected at the end of the cycle. ‘“‘ It rises in the air from the ground, in the midst of the assembly: it is made of seven precious substances. It has thousands of balconies strewn with flowers, etc.’’—Lotus of the Good Law. The causal-body is on the higher mental (air) plane, above the lower nature (ground), and centralised amid the higher qualities. It is complete in all virtues. See Cone, HovusE (frequented), KaaBa, PALAce, PyRAMID, TABERNACLE, TEMPLE, TONGUE. STYx
