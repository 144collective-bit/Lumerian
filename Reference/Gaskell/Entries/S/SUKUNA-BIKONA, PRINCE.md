A symbol of the personality as a ruler of the lower mind. LITTLE SUMMER “‘ A dwarf deity who wore garments of bird skins and came over the sea in a tiny boat.”,—W. G. Aston, Shinto, p. 107. The personality is a much-limited (dwarf) manifestation of the soul. The “ bird skins ”’ signify the capacity of the personality to aspire to higher conditions; and the ‘coming over the sea in a boat” is an allusion to the development of the personality on the astral plane (sea) in animal forms which have been evolved first on globes of the lunar chain and afterwards on the physical earth. See Brrp, Boat, Dwarr, LUNAR CHAIN, OHONAMOCHI, PERSONALITY, Pitris (lunar), PrrrmryvANA, PLANETARY CHAIN, SKINS OF ANIMALS.
