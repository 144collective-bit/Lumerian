A symbol of the buddhic forces acting through the mind, which raise and heal the soul. ** As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up.” —JonN ii. 14. As the moral law (Moses) points to and exalts wisdom (serpent) in the arena of the soul’s lower nature, so the Son of mind,—the Christ-soul, —must be exalted when tho soul is sufficiently evolved, or perfected. See Brass, Mretayts, Moses, Son or MAN, WILDERNESS.
