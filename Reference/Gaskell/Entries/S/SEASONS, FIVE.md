A symbol of the five planes of manifestation within the cycle af life. ‘‘And that Pragdpati who became relaxed is the year; and those five bodily parts of his (i.e. hair, skin, flesh, bone, and marrow) which became relaxed, are the seasons; for there are five seasons, and five are those layers.”’"— Sata. Bréh., VI. 1, 2, 18. The Archetypal Man, being perfected in the cycle of involution, thereafter suffered a reversed process and became segregated for the cycle of evolution. The five planes (physical, astral, mental, buddhic, and atmici, which had received their qualities and potencies in involution, were now to evolve the same in multiplicity of forms. The five planes are the five layers or regions of the universe and soul. “And those five bodily parts of his, the seasons which became relaxed, are the regions; for five in number are the regions, and five those layers; when he builds up the five layers, he builds hm up with the regions.”—ZJbid., VI. 1, 2, 1% The inner Self is built up as mastery over the lower planes is achieved. The lower conditions are the mean: by which the Self is manifested iz the soul. See ALTAR (fire), ARC. MAN, ASTRAL PLANE, ATMA, BONES, BUDDHIC PLANE DISMEMBERMENT, ELEMENTS (fivel.
