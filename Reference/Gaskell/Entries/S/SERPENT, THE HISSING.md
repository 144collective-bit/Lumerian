A symbol of the desire-mind, with its knowledge of good and evil, or the distinctions of relativity. It assumes the attitude of tempter,— subtle and deceitful, that is, illusory (miyaivic). The ‘ hissing ’’ signifies the activity of the senses, which dig channels for themselves in the lower vehicles. ** After this, Set changed himself into a serpent which hissed loudly, and he sought out a hole for himself in the ground wherein he hid himself and lived” (Legend of the Winged Sun-disk).— Bunek, Gods of the Egyptians. And following this stage of evolution, ‘‘evil’’ (Set) acquired the capability of tempter (serpent) from having the knowledge of good and _ evil. The “hole,’”’ or ‘‘ bottomless pit,’’ is the sense-nature, which is never satisfied and has no ending inthe lower nature (ground). The ‘hiding’ and ‘“‘living’’ signify the latent power which the desire-mental nature acquires in the process of the evolution of buddhi in the soul. See Beast, BOTTOMLESS PIT, BupDDHI, DHRITA-RASHTRA, EVIL, Frenp, GROUND, HEAD OF HORUS, Inuusion, Pir, Poe, SEtT. 677
