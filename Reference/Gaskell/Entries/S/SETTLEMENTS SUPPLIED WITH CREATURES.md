Symbolic of manifested worlds primordially containing the monads of life and form. In this ‘‘ golden age,’’ the egos functioning through the quaternary dwell prosperously and are at peace. There is no strife. Time passes unheeded in this condition of peaceful involution on the Lunar and Terrene globes. See GOLDEN AGE, INVOLUTION, KaRSHVARES, LUNAR CHAIN, PLANETARY CHAIN, REGIONS, VARA.
