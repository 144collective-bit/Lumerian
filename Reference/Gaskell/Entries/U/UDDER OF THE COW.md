A symbol of the means through which the higher qualities (milk) are derived for the soul's sustenance. “* Ita body (the sky) is the cow, ita udder the cow’s udder (the cloud), ite teat the cow’s teat (lightning), its shower (of wealth) the shower of milk: from the cow it comes to the Sacrificer.”— Sata.
