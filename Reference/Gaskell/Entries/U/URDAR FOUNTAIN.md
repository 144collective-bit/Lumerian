A symbol of the fountain of Truth expressed in the outpouring of the activities in the Cycle of Life. “The third root of the great Tree of Life runs under the region of the Agar, or gods. Beneath it is the Urdar or rimeval fountain. Twelve swans swim In this fountain, and are the parents of the race of swans. There, too, by the Urder fountain, dwell the three chief Nornor, or Fates.’’—Howrrt, Literature of the North, Vol. I. p. 465. Behind the Unity which precedes the Duality of Time and Space is the Absolute Source of all. From It proceeds the Cycle of Life in twelve stages of progress, or states of the Self producing all other states. These are subject to natural laws. See Assotuts, Apiryas, ASAR, CIRCLE OF EXISTENCE, FATE-SPHERE, 780 URIM Founrarn, Morar, Nornoz, OCEAN, Swan, TREE OF LiFe, TUAT, TWELVE, Water, YoapRasIL, ZODIAC.
