A symbol of a sheath of the soul, or body of mental, astral, or physical matter on the respective planes. The causal-body is usually signified as being the abode of the Self or Divine Spark. In the case of Loke’s ‘‘ house with four doors,’’ all the four bodies are meant. See CiaAssEs, DWELLINGS, Hovuss, PALACE, VESTURES.
