A symbol of the indwelling Self, or Saviour, active on the higher planes (Abtu). “*May I see the Abtu fish at his season.'—Hymn to Ra. The name of 8& mythological fish which, on coffins, etc., is seen swimming at the bows of the boat of the Sun-god.”"—Bupcr, Book of the Dead, p. 6. The soul when perfected anticipates union with the Higher Self at the end of the cycle. The Higher Self symbolised as a Great Fish in the Ocean of Truth, leads the soul (boat) onwards in its course through the cycle of life on the lower planes. See BENNU BIRD, DELUGE, Fisx (great), JEsus (fish), Ocean, ReEsTau, Sarp (Manu).
