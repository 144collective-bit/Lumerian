A symbol of the Higher Self incarnate on the lower planes of the soul. “ As Amen-Ré he was worshipped as the supremo *‘ King of the Gods,’ a creator and soul of the universe, inscrutable and eternal. He is usually figured in human form, with two long upright plumes rising above his head, holding a sceptre and the symbol of life. The ram was sacred to him, and in this animal he was believed to be incarnate.’’—Non-classical Mythology, p. 9. The incarnate Self is symbolised by the ram of sacrifice,—the ‘‘ Lamb of God.”’ See Aru-RA, Crown (sekhet), Derry (hidden), Mut, PLumes, SCEPTRE.
