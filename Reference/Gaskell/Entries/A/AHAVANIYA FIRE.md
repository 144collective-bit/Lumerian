A symbol! of the centre of outpouring spiritual activity on the buddhic plane. ‘*Now Indra, in truth is the same as the Ahavanfya; and king Yama is the rer : - Aa Garhapatya.”’—Sata. Brih., ‘“‘Indra”’ is a symbol of Divine energy (atma-buddhic), and ‘“ King Yama’ is the causal self in the causal-body. ‘*That Ahavanfya fire is the Sacrificer’s divine body, and this body of his is his human one.”—Sate. Bréh., VI. 6, 4, 5. The buddhic outpouring is_ the Divine vehicle of the Self, and the causal-body is his mental one. See AGNiHOTRA, GARHAPATYA, Yama.
