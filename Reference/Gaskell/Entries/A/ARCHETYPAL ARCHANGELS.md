### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-archetypal-archangels-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of primordial Divine Powers on the highest planes of manifestation. These have their part in the construction of the spiritual universe which underlies the perceptible and visible phenomenal universe. ‘*Archangels”’ also signify messengers of the Logos on high planes; and exalted Intelligences who introduce the spiritual Lifeelement into the vehicles, or forms, fully prepared and awaiting its reception. See AMSHASPANDS, ANGELS, APSARAS, BAHMAN, BUILDERS, DEVAS, GABRIEL, GUARDIANS, MESSENGERS, MICHAEL, VOHUMANO.
```
