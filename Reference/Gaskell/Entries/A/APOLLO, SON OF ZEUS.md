A symbol of the Second Logos proceeding from the First. God manifest, or the Higher Self. ‘‘Nine days through the army went the arrows of the God (Phebus Apollo) ; but on the tenth, Achilles called the people to an assembly; for to his mind the white-armed Goddess Juno had suggested it; for she was anxious concerning the Greeks, because she saw them perishing.”’-—Iliad, Book I. trans. BUKKLEY. For nine periods the life-giving force of the Logos ensouled the egos, or monads of form; but at the tenth period, the personality (Achilles) is evolved sufficiently to enable the lower mental qualities to assemble to it as a centre. For at this period of soul-growth there was some glimmer of such higher perception as is essential to the further development of the individuality. And the Wisdom APOSTATES aspect is directly concerned with the evolution of the mental qualities which had begun to languish. “Dionysius proclaims: ‘The Divine Goodness, this our great Sun, enlightens, nourishes, perfects, renews.’ Even the pure can thus be made purer still. ‘ He, the Good, is called spiritual light; he cleanses the mental vision of the very angels: they taste, as it were, the light.’ All this imagery goes back, in the first instance, to Proclus. For Proclus puts in parallel ‘sun’ and ‘God,’ and ‘to be enlightened’ and ‘to be deified’; makes all purifying forces to coalesce in the activity of the Sun-God, Apollo Katharsios, the Purifier, who ‘everywhere unifies multiplicity .. . purifying the entire heaven and all living things throughout the world’; and describes how ‘ from above, from his super-heavenly pot Apollo scatters the arrows of Zeus,— is raya upon all the world’”’ (In Par. IV. 34).—F. von Huarn, Mystical Element, Vol. II. p. 93. “Nothing can be more profound, more entire, than the reverence of Homer’s mortals for Apollo and Athené.” myer E. GLADSTONE, Juventue Mundi, p. 177. See ACHILLES, ANAPHE, ARGIVES, AROUERIS, ARROWS, DELOS, EXILE, Gate, HecatomsB, Hers, Hymn, Latona, NINE, NINE DAYS, PESTILENCE, PRIEST OF APOLLO, WRATH OF APOLLO.
