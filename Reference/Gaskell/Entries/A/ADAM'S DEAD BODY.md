```text
A symbol of the old self, devoid of spiritual life, and through discarding which the soul rises. ** Noah carries the body of Adam into the Ark, his three sons following with the sacred tokens.’’—Book of the Conflicte of Adam. ** Noah,” the individuality or sublimated ‘‘ Adam,” conveys his old self with him, as it were, in the causal. body (ark), as it is only through it, and by surmounting it, that he rises. His ‘‘sons’’ stand here for the fruit of the affectionate nature, and these willingly follow with the tokens of the essence of his aspiration,—Faith, Love, Wisdom, Power. See ARK (Noah), Ham, Noag, Noau’s sons, Rock, ToKENs.
```
