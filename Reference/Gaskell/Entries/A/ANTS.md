A symbol of the monads of life and form. ‘Ye divine ante—for it was they that produced this (ant-hill),—‘the first-born of the world,’—the first-born of the world, doubtless, is this earth.’’—Saza. Bréh., XIV. 1, 2, 10. The spiritual monads (atmabuddhic) build up the forms and ANUBIS endow with life all the atoms and beings of the lower nature, which manifest first,—prior to spiritual qualities. The first to manifest in the cycle of evolution is the lower nature (earth). See Devas, EARTH, FORM, MONAD OF LIFE.
