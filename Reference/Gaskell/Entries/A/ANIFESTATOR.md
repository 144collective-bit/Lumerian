## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

A symbol of the Second Logos, or Higher Self, who proceeds from the Absolute as a Divine germ of universal life.

> "The Triple God born from the Egg was called [word unclear] and also Metis and Ericapeus, the three being aspects of [word unclear], Power."—G. R. S. Mead, *Orpheus*, p. 162.

> "Of the three aspects, Phanes is said to be the 'father,' Ericapæus the 'power,' and Metis the 'intellect' in Platonic terms. . . . Phanes was also called Love (Eros) . . . also the Limit or [word unclear] intelligible Light."—*Ibid.*, pp. [4, 0?].

The Higher Self comes forth in three aspects,—Will (Phanes), Wisdom (Metis), and Action (Ericapezus). The Higher Self is the Divine attraction or Love; also Atma, the highest state of the manifest, and the Light of Truth.

See ABSOLUTE, CAVE, EGG, ERICAPAUS, EROS, HIRANYAGARBHA, LAO, METIS, MONAD, NOAH'S SONS, SEED, TRIAD.
