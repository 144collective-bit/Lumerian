A symbol of the Second Logos, or Higher Self, who proceeds from the Absolute as a Divine germ of universal life. **The Triple God born from the Egg was called epee and also Metis and Ericapeus, the three being aspects of on, Power.’’—G. R. 8. Mrap, Orpheus, p- 162. ** Of the three aspects, Phanes is said to be the ‘ father,’ _Ericapaus the ‘ power,’ and Metis the ‘intellect’ in Platonic terms. . . . Phanes was also called Love (Eros) ... also the Limit or ata qe7. intelligible Light.” —Jbid., pp 4, 0 The Higher Self comes forth in three aspects,—Will (Phanes), Wisdom (Metis), and Action (Ericapezus). The Higher Self is the Divine attraction or Love; also Atma, the highest state of the manifest, and the Light of Truth. See Anso.turs, Cave, Eac, ErRicaPaus, Eros, HIrRANYAGARBHA, Lao, Metis, Monap, Noan’s sons, SEED, TRIAD.
