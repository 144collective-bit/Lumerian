```text
A symbol of the physical plane, the lowest plane of the quaternary. See Apam (lower), COUNTENANCE (greater), WORLDS (five). ASEKA ADEPTSHIP :~— A symbol of the state of consciousness of the ego when raised to the highest possible point it can attain in the present cycle of twelve stages through which growth is accomplished. This would mean an atmabuddhic level, whereas the Arhat consciousness is buddhi-manasic. The next globe period will be capabie of carrying the advanced ego up to the Aseka stage of being ; so it may be imagined this stage will probably be achieved in the future. See Annats, ASCETIOC, INITIATIONS, MasrEeR OF wISDOM, WALES.
```
