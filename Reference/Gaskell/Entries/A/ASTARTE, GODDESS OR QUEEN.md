```text
A symbol of wisdom on the buddhic plane. “The king Melcarthus. The queen Astarte, or Saosis, or Nemanoun, the same as the Greek Athenais.’’—PLUTARCH, Isis and Osirie, § 15. The ‘‘king” is a symbol of the ego as governor of the astral activities. The names of the queen signify aspects of wisdom, i.e. Wisdom-love, Wisdompower, and Wisdom-activity. ‘‘The goddess Astarte, or Asthertet, is in a chariot drawn by four horses which trample on the foes of Rd.’’-—Bupaz, Book of the Dead, Vol. I. p. 478. Wisdom is seated in the causalbody (chariot), directing the lower quaternary (horses), which through the mind overcomes the desires. See Free or HELL, IstTar, MELCARTHUS.
```
