## Quoted definition / text (OCR exact; short)

> A symbol of the spiritual mind, that is, the inner mind which is moved by the higher emotions.

## Attributed paraphrase (editorial)

Gaskell presents Aaron the High Priest as a symbol of the spiritual mind (inner mind moved by higher emotions), operating subjectively on the higher-mind plane and serving the causal-body's development; he also equates Aaron with Abel on a higher mental sub-plane.
