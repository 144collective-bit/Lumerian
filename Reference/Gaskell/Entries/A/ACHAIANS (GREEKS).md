A symbol of the lower mental qualities. “Of a truth (said Achilles) Agamemnon raveth with baleful mind, and hath not knowledge to look before and after, that so his Achaians might battle in safety beside their ships.’”’-—TJliad, Bk. I. Assuredly the Desire-mind has strange fancies, and has little knowledge to perceive before and after, i.e. it has little steadiness of mental vision, which steadiness is needed in order that the mental qualities (Achaians) might successfully carry out their evolution. See DaNnaans, GREEKS.
