These symbols signify the Divine principles of the Higher Triad— Will, Wisdom, and Action. ** Minos, who is stated by Thucydides to have been the first known founder of & maritime empire, appears in Homer as the greatest and most important of bis archaic personages.... The name of Minos, whether mythical or not, is a symbol of political power, of the administration of justice, in a word, of civiligation. . . . His brother Rhadamanthus, hardly less distinguished, has the custody of the Elysian Plain.”— W. E. GLapDsTone, Juv. Mundt, pp. 119, 120. The Divine Will rules over the empire of Truth (water).
