A symbol of a high state of Truth and Bliss upon the buddhic plane. “He (the departed) approaches the Ara, and crosses it by the mind, while those who come to it without knowing the truth are drowned in it.””— Kaush. Upanishad, I. 2. He, the liberated ego, who has advanced beyond the conditioned, now hath known and realised his true nature to be Bliss. This he has discovered by means of the higher mind—buddhi. But those egos who, having acquired some powers of the mind, would pass beyond the conditioned without having the Truth and without purifying the lower nature, work destruction to themselves, as far as that state is concerned. See BLacK MaGicians, Briss, Rart, Vieara, YATUS.
