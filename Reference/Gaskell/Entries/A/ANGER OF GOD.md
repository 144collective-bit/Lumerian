For I was afraid of the anger and hot displeasure, wherewith the Lord was wroth against you to destroy you. But the Lord hearkened unto me that time also. The rational and ethical nature has been unable, in this backward state of soul, to partake of goodness and truth, because the lower mind has been so full of the activities of desire and sensation.
