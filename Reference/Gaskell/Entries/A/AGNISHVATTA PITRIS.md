## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-agnishvatta-pitris-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the monads of life; the spiritual egos central in every soul, and of atma-buddhic nature. These are the ‘‘Sparks from the Flame,’’—the Divine egos which render human souls immortal. ‘As from a blazing fire consubstantial sparks proceed in a thousand ways, 80 from the imperishable Spirit various living souls are produced, and_ they return to Him again.’’—Mundaka Upansshad, II. 1, 1. “The spirit of man contains a spark from the power and light of God.”— Benen. “Only the ‘little spark’ of the soul is to be at all times with God and united with God.”’—EcKHART. ““What is clear to my mind is that every individual human soul is a detached, or seemingly detached, portion of the AHANKARA Universal Substance, and that all the porous of divinity are in it waiting to revealed. ... We have come from the One; unto the One shall we return, but not empty-handed.”—R. J. CampBELL, Serm., The Son of Man Ascending. See Fre, FRAVASHIS, MANASAPUTRaS, Maruts, Monap (life), MUSPELL (sons), Prrris (solar), Sons OF GOD, SPARES.
```
