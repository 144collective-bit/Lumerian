```text
This is the love-element on the different planes, which draws forces, atoms, or qualities, towards centres. “For unto every one that hath shall be given, and he shall have abundance : but from him that hath not, even that which he hath shall be taken away.” — Mart. xxv. 29. This indicates that the strength of attraction is in proportion to the number of grouped forces or faculties attracting to a common centre. Where a force is unattached to a group, there is no balance about a centre, therefore it flies off to join a group, or it is dissipated. The teaching is, that higher qualities must not be isolated, but must be brought into harmonious relation with kindred qualities, so that all may work together for good. ‘“‘ Where two or three are gathered together, there am lin the midst of them.” See APHRODITE, ASSEMBLY, CITY, Love (uniter), Pmcrms (shrine), SrRirs.
```
