Symbols of Divine Will and Divine Wisdom, i.e. Atma-buddhi. “Light (Atea) having been evolved tom darkness (Tanaoa), the god Ono or ‘Sound’ was evolved from Atea, and he destroyed ‘Mutuhei'’ (silence).
