A symbol of the third initiation of the personality, implying attainment by the soul of a resolve to go through to the bitter end of experience and finish the course. In the Gospel Drama it is the equivalent of the three years’ ministry of Jesus which ushers in the fourth initiation at the Crucifixion (the Arhat), when the lower nature is discarded and liberation is effected.
