May Asha-vanuhi grant happiness here, in this sacred abode of Homa, the transmitter of righteousness. May Wisdom be the means of securing the bliss which is to flourish in the kingdom of Love, the dispenser of Righteousness ! ** Ashish vanuhi makes the wisdom of all the prophets continue, and inspires them in their turn with the original wisdom.’’—M.
