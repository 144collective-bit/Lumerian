Heaven and Hell, Vol. I. p. 19. ** Life to thee, O governor of Amentet, Osiris, who art over the beings of Amentet.”’—Ibid., p. 276. *“O R& of the beautiful Ament.’”’— Ibid., p. 86. ‘Isis of Amentet.”—Jbid., p. 85.
