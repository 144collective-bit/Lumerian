## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

Symbols of the desire-nature and the instinct nature, fully developed through the astral evolution.

> "The most perfect objects of vegetation are two trees Askr and Embla, upon the sea-shore. The Gods change these trees into man and woman by giving them the human soul and body" (*Voluspa*, V. 33-40).—Ida Pfeiffer, *Iceland*, p. 314.

This refers to the perfecting of the desire and instinct natures in the forms on the astro-physical plane (sea-shore), and the conferring by the higher nature, of mind and spirit on the lower personalities. This development requires the union of mind (man) with emotion (woman) in a fitting human body.

See ASAR, MAN, [Matro?], RIMTHURSAR, SHORE, TREE, WOMAN, YMER.
