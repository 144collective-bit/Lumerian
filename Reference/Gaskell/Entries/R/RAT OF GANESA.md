A symbol of the lower quaternary. **Ganesa is frequently attended by, or is riding upon, a rat.’’—WILKINS, Hindu Mythology, p. 324. The Higher Self, operating upon the higher mental plane, presides over the lower mental, the astral, the etheric, and the physical planes, symbolised by the four limbs of a rat.
