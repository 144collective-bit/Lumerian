```text
A symbol of the differentiated and regenerate individuality, it having acquired through aspiration and effort the higher qualities wherewith it is now endowed. ‘The ren, or name, to preserve which the Egyptians took the most extraordinary precautions, for the belief was widespread that unless the name of a man was preserved he ceased to exist. Already in the time of King Pepi the RENDING name was regarded as a most important peer of & man’s economy, and in the ollowing pasaage it ranks equally with the ka :—‘ The iron which is the ceiling of heaven openeth itself before Pepi, and he passeth through it with his panther skin upon him, and his etaff and whip in his hand ; Pepi passeth with his flesh, and he is happy with his name, and he liveth with his double.’ Already in the Pyramid Texts we find the deceased making supplication that his name may ‘grow’ or ‘shoot forth’ and endure as long as the names of Tem, Shu, Seb, and other gods.”—BuncE, Book of the Dead, p. \xiii. The individuality (ren) is seated in the causal-body (ka) and is indistinguishable from it spiritually. See CEILING OF SKY, IRON PLATE, Ka, NAME, PANTHER SKIN, PyRamID.
```
