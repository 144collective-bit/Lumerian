A symbol of instability of purpose and weakness of volition. “‘ Tropologically, ‘a reed shaken with the wind’ is a light man, inconstant, tossed to and fro: at one time impelled by the words of flatterers, he asserts something: again being driven by detractors, he denies it, as a reed is blown in different directions by different winds. A reed is one who is devoid of truth, virtue, and consistency, as a reed has no strength, or stamina.”"—C. A LapipE, Great Comm., Vol. IT. p. 53.
