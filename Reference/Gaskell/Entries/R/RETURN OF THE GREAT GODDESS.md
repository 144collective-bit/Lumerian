A symbol of the Wisdom-nature evolving in the soul after its period of obscuration or latency. Since the fall of mind (man) into the lower mature, the buddhic consciousness (Wisdom) has been absent from the human soul. This spiritual consciousness returns to the ego when the soul is perfected at the end of the cycle, and reveals the ‘‘ treasures in heaven.’’ See AMATERASU, Fatt, Goppsss, WITNESSES (two).
