A symbol of the buddhic plane on which is manifested a buddhic globe of the lunar planetary chain. “The twelfth good region was Raghs of the three races. Thereupon Angra Mainyu created the sin of utter unbelief.” — Vendidad, I. The three root races of incipient humanity were upon the plane of buddhi (Ragha). These races were upon one of the globes of the lunar chain in matter of the wisdom plane, awaiting the period when they shall descend and solidify on the mental and astral planes. At this early period they had no vehicles in which to behave as humanity does under later circumstances. The consequence is they have reference entirely to the Archetypal Man,—the potential pattern of humanity. In opposition to this condition of manifestation, the division is hinted at between the lower and higher principles, since their union has not yet been effected. See ADAM (lower), AROHETYPAL MAN, — —_ = . _ =n = = —=—_— = a RAHU RAINBOW Brasts (three sorts), GOLDEN AGE, upon the egos immersed in the lower LUNAR CHAIN, PLANETARY CHAIN, nature (hell) and subject to suffering Pyius, Races, Root-Races, Rounp. (hell-fire) ; and they will be purified of their imperfections and enter into bliss.
