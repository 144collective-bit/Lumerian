## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-respectful-manners-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of harmony in human conditions during the period of involution. ‘‘ Human beings, among whom respectful manners and pure customs prevailed, occupied one territory.”,—Chinese Mythology, Kipp, China. And now is developed the humanity of the fabled Golden Age,—the early type of man in which the absence of all relative experience of opposites appears as Edenic perfection. The ‘territory ’® is a symbol of the limitation imposed upon the race by the Absolute. See CIVILISING, EDEN, GOLDEN AGE, Opposites, Races, RELATIVE, Yao.
```
