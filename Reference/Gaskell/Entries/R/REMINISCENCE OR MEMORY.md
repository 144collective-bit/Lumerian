This, in its deeper signification, is identified with the causal Self, which is a direct offshoot or ray of the Self emanating from a centre in the higher mind. The personality is the means whereby intercommunication between the inner memory and the outer nature is eventually established, for through this memory the lower mind is awakened on its higher side and made receptive of ideals which are implanted by the causal Self.
