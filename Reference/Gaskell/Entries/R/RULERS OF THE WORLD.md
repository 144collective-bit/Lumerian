Symbolic of the five natures which constitute the Soul. “The five shoota (of the tree of Life) are the five rulers,—the House ruler. the Village-ruler, the Tribe-ruler, the Province-ruler, and the supreme Zarstust.”"—Sikand-Gumanik Vigar, §&.B.E. Vol. XXIV. p. 118. These are the physical, astral, lower mental, causal, and Atmabuddhic natures. See Castes, CHILDREN OF HORUS, CuassEs, Days (five), PELEMeEnts (five) GaTHa pays (five), Hovsz (clan), Province, TREE OF LIFE, TrrBE (ruler), VESTURES, WILLAGE, WorLps (five), ZOROASTER.
