## Full OCR entry body (exact)

```text
Symbolic of the spiritual egos or individualities. These are said to be ‘‘spirits moving in mid-air on their vim4Anas or chariots,’’ that is, they are the human egos active on the higher mental plane (mid-air) seated in their causal vehicles. See AIR, CAUSAL-BODY, CHARIOT, INDIVIDUALITY, Marnuts, Monap (life), PHAEACIANS, SONS OF GOD.
```
