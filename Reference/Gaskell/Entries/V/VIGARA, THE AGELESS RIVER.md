A symbol of infinity,—the river of life eternal. ‘He comes to the river Vigara, and crosses it by the mind alone, and then shakes off his good and evil deeds.’’— Kaush: Upanishad, I. 2. The perfected soul arrives at the border of Eternity, and crosses it by pure buddhi, the ‘raft of wisdom,” and so shakes off all relativity,—the pairs of opposites. See Ana, LIBERATION, OPPOSITES, RaFtT OF WISDOM.
