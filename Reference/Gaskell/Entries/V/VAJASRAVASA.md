A symbol of the Supreme Manifesting Spirit, or the Higher Self. ** Vajasravasa, with the desire of recompense, offered sacrifice, and gave all that he possessed to the priests. He had a son named Nachiketas.’’—Katha. Upanishad, I. The Higher Self, desiring the results of experience, limits Himself in the manifestation of his nature, and bestows all his powers and qualities upon the spiritual egos or minds. The son of the Highest is the Soul or incarnate Self. See EXPERIENCE, NacHIKETAS, Poverty, Priest, SACRIFICER, SELF. | VALHALLA VALE, LOKE’
