A symbol of the lower principle. ‘In the Rig-Veda (see VI. 20, 2) the demon of cloud and darkness, called Vritra, is either identified or associated with the serpent Ahi; and the god Soma is described as delivering over all evil speakers and slanderers into the power of this serpent.”—Mon. Wriiams, Religious Thought tn India, p. 13. VULTURE The Divine Life (Soma) requires those qualities of the lower mind which express falsity and perversion to be re-embodied and thereby placed in the power of the lower principle, in order to gain experience and development. See Arrows (divine), DARKNESS, Ravana, SERPENT, SoMA.
