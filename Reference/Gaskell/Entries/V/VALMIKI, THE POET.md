A symbol of the Divine Self, the inspirer of truth in the soul. “The voice of Valmiki bap its try which the great seer Kyavana sould not compose; and that medicine DDD VAPOUR which Atri never invented, the wise son of Atri proclaimed after him.’’—BuddhaKarita, Bk. I. 48. The efforts of the Divine Self to communicate itself to the Soul failed in the immature forerunners of the Soul. The means whereby the Divine nature was to deliver, heal, or redeem the Soul, the Eternal is said ‘“‘ not to have invented’ and applied; since deliverance is to be achieved through the Son of the Eternal, that is, the Soul itself is to be its own deliverer. ‘* For the purpose of realising the nature of the Self, we have had to come out from our eternal home in God that we might strive and suffer amid the illusions of time and sense. We have to overcome before we can enter into the eternal truth that lies beyond all seeming. In that overcoming we have to master the fiesh and magnify the spirit, despise the world to save it, lose the life to find it.” R. J. CamMPBELL, Serm., Winning the Divine Name. See ALcHEMy, ArTRI, ImMHOTEP, Kyavana, MEDICINE, PHYSICIAN, Prrris (lunar), SERPENT ASCULAPIUS.
