A symbol of the principle of Buddhi, Wisdom, the primal manifestation of life. ** Vach, the melodious Cow who milked forth sustenance and water.’’—Rtg- Veda. The Holy Spirit, Wisdom (Cow), the source of Divine inspiration, who bestows higher emotion (milk) and truth (water). VAEKEREYA “* There is also with the Maruts, Vak (the voice of thunder) like unto a courtly, eloquent woman.’’—Rig- Veda, I. 167, 3. There is allied with the spiritual egos the buddhic principle (v4k), which expresses the higher emotions in peace and harmony within the soul. The ** voice of thunder ”’ signifies the manifestation of the higher life through wisdom, truth, and love. *““The Great Voice, ‘the voice out of the midst of the darkness.’ It is interior, imperceptible, without cessation or interruption. It is the house of the Eternal Wisdom and is female, as a house always is."—A. E. Warts, Secret ne in Israel, p. 50.
