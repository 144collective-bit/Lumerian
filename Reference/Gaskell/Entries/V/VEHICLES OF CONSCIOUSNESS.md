### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-vehicles-of-consciousness-ocr-body`

```text
The organised bodies on the different planes, in which the soul abides and expresses itself. The five vehicles are,—the Buddhic, the Causal, the Mental, the Astral, and the Physical. Sometimes a sixth is VEIL mentioned, namely, the Etheric, which is only to a small extent a vehicle. See CasTES, CHILDREN OF HORUS, CrassEs, MARRIAGE (castes), VESTURES.
```
