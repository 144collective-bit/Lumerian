A symbol of a homogeneous buddhic condition. ‘* When Oro emerged from the vapour which, like a cloud, had encircled the rainbow, he discovered the dwelling of Vairaumati, the fair mistress of the cottage, who became his wife’? (The Tahitian ‘‘ Legend of Oro’’).—ELus, Polynesian Researches, Vol. I. p. 231.
