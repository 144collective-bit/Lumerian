## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-vinegar-to-drink-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the experience of suffering and sorrow. “And straightway one of them ran, and took a sponge, and filled it with vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink.”—Mar. xxvii. 48. Through the action of a quality there was given to the Christ-soul the cup of sorrow which was to be drunk to the dregs ; this was willingly (reed) accepted under the rule of Divine law. *‘And the vinegar which was handed to Him seems to me to have been a symbolical thing. . . . the reed signified the royal sceptre and the divine law.’’— Dionysius, Br. or ALEXANDRIA, An Interpretation of Luke. See Cow’s URINE, CRUCIFIXION OF JESUS, GALL, GETHSEMANE, PASSION, REED, Sorrow, SuFFERING.
```
