A symbol of channels for the expression of wisdom or buddhic emotion. “And Mary said to Jesus,—I found thee in the vineyard ; Joseph was putting up the vine-poles.’"—The Story of the Infancy in the Pistis Sophia. The purified lower nature perceives the Christ-soul in the place of spiritual wisdom, and observes the reason exercised in directing channels through which that wisdom shall find expression in the soul.
