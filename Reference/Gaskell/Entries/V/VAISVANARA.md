## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-vaisvanara-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the Divine Mind, or the Higher Self on the mental plane. Thus Adyta, the sun, rises, as Vaisvénara (belonging to all men) assuming all forms, as spirit, as fire. . .. the golden, who knows all things, who ascends highest, alone in his splendour, and warms us ; the thousand rayed, who abides in a hundred places, the spirit of all creatures, the Sun, rises.”’—Prasna Upanishad, I.7,8. This refers to the Higher Self manifesting (rising) on the mental plane, and producing and energising the ideal forms of all things which actualise on the astral and physical planes. The Self is atma-buddhi (golden), and is the informing Spirit within all creatures; knowing all things, and ascending through evolution, to the highest from which he had descended. He gives life to all (warms up), and is always and everywhere present within his creatures. See Apirya, Dawn, East, HrranYAGARBHA, POTSHERD, SLEEPING (waking), SuN, SUN-RISING, SURYA.
```
