Symbolic of transcending the sense of separateness, and the illusions and attractions presented to the ego by the lower mind during the life cycle. ‘Buddha was born to destroy the evils of the world.’”—Buddha-Karita, Bk. I. 20. The Soul is born in the womb of matter (mental, astral, and physical), with the object of overcoming the things of the world,—that is, conquering the falsities which are mindcreated, and which are to be destroyed through the knowledge of the Real.
