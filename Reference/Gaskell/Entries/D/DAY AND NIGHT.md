Symbolic of periods of spiritual and material activity, in major or in minor cycles. ‘Day and Night sre Pragapati, its day is spirit, its night matter.” —Prasna Upanishad, I. 13. ‘“‘Learning through this treatise, by trouble and day and night painstaking.” —Preface to the Bundahis. Learning from the Book of Life is possible only by effort and “day and night painstaking,” that is, through experience in a successive series of physical incarnations.
