Symbolic of the disciplined qualities freed from reliance upon objects of the external life, and directed to purify the life within. “‘Go not into any way of the Gentiles, and enter not into any city of the Samaritans: but go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.’—Mar. x. 5, 6.
