Hecateeus of Abdera says that the he who is .  DELOS Egyptians invoke the Supreme God as being hidden and invisible, and implore Him to make himself visible and apparent, and therefore call him ‘ Amun’ great was the piety of the Egyptiana in their teaching about the Gods.’’— PLUTARCH, Ists and Ostris, § 9. So far as God is unmanifest in the soul and its qualities, he is, as it were, hidden, or in hiding, from the lower consciousness. The aspiration, therefore, of the soul is that God should manifest his nature in the higher qualities, and appear as a centralising Ideal to the consciousness. When God is born in the soul, he becomes, as it were, “visible and apparent” in the religious consciousness, and receives the adoration of the higher qualities of mind and emotion.
