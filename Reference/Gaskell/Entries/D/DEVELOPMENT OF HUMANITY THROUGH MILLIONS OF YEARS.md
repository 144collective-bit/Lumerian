```text
Development cannot be measured from the point of view of the lower . mind, and there is no evidence to show that it proceeds appreciably on this physical plane. It is God the Highest who looks down ; not we who look up, who can judge upon the growth of the soul. Eternal verities cannot be put into terms of space and time, for these illusions must be eliminated from the mind ere it can discern the celestial significance of the sublime teaching of the sacred scriptures of the world. See AMBAS, INSPIRATION, REVELATION, SCRIPTURES.
```
