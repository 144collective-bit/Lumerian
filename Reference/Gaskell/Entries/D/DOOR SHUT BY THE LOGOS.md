```text
A symbol of the termination of the cycle of life on the lower planes. ‘* When once the master of the house is risen up, and hath shut to the door, | and ye begin to stand without, and DOOR to knock at the door, saying, Lord open to us; and he shall answer and aay to you, I know you not whence ye are.’ —LUKE xiii. 25. This signifies the manvantaric evolution brought to a conclusion, and describes the inability of those who have not kept pace with the scheme, to secure a place in its fulfilment. Excuses are useless: for unless the lower avails itself of the advantages offered by the Influence from On High, no grace is obtained, and nothing is achieved. The “door shut’ implies that the distinction between the material and spiritual is realised. The insufficiently progressed qualities or souls shut out have to await further opportunities of improvement. See Justice, Karma, MANVANTARA, Vimers (parable).
```
