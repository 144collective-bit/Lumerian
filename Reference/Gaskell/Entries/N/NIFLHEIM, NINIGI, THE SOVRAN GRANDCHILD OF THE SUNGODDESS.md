A symbol of the Higher Self born in the soul’s lower nature purified by buddhi. 637 NIRVANA ** Ninigi on his descent to earth took to wife Konohana-sakuyahime (ladyblooming - like - the - flowers -of- the- trees). Her father Ohoyamatsu-mi (great-mountain-person) had offered him both his daughters, but the elder was rejected by Ninigi as being too ugly. Her name was Iha-naga-hime (rock-long-lady). She in her shame and resentment, uttered a curse and said: ‘ The race of visible men shall change swiftly like the flowers of the trees, and shal] decay and pass away.’ This is the reason why the life of man ie so short.” —W. G. Astron, Shinto, p. 112. The Self born in the lower nature became allied with Faith-in-the Divine-within,—a product of Aspiration which at first produces Trust-intradition-and-authority, with which the Self cannot ally itself. But the teaching of Tradition is self-destructive, for opinions (visible men) change swiftly, and authorities lose hold and pass away.
