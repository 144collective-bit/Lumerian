A symbol of negation, secrecy, or the forgetting of all past experience on entering a higher level of consciousness. This implies the expunging of sorrow and pleasure, the wiping out of pain and sensehappiness, the killing out of desire, in passing from the ‘‘ night ’’ of the soul,—the delusion of the lower planes,—to the ‘‘ day ’’ of the higher planes. The symbol ‘night’? may thus come to stand for inaction, or subjectivity. See CHARON’S reRRY, Nectar, Nox, PrRanayvA, RECOLLECTION, Sryx.
