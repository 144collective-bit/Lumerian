A symbol of nine cycles or periods. ‘‘Nine days through the army went the arrows of the god (Apollo); but on the tenth, Achilles called the people to an assembly.”’—ZIliad, Bk. I. For nine periods the Life of the Logos ensouled the manifesting egos on the mental plane; but at the tenth period the personality is evolved sufficiently to enable the lower qualities to assemble. See AcHILLES, APOLLO, ARROWS, ASSEMBLY, PESTILENCE, TEN.
