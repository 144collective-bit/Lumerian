Symbolic of a co-ordination of the astral mechanism in relation to the formation of the Soul. ** Pwan-koo taught navigation, made passages through the mountains, and reigned as the first king of the human race.”’—K wp, China, p. 100. The Logos, or Divine Mind, was the originator and first ruler of the human soul. Teaching ‘‘navigation’’ is emblematic of the management of the astral mechanism, and is preparatory to experiences on the plane of desire. The “ passages’ refer to the establishment of tracts upon the mental plane. And from the astral plane through the action of manas,—the mind principle,—evolves the human soul. 627 NECESSITY See ASTRAL PLANE, Net, PwAN-KOO, Sea, VALLEYS, WATER (lower).
