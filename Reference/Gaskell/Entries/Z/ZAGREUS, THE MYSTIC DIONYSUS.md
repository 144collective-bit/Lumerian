The buddhic emotion-nature (Persephone) fructified by the Supreme Spirit (Zeus) brought forth the Soul or Self on the buddhic plane to be the ruler of the lower nature. The Self being subjected to the primal laws and conditions of manifestation (the Titans) which ruled and limited him on every side, passed through the various stages of involution until he was perfected as the matrix of all forms and qualities (the bull). The primal laws then brought about his death and dismemberment of qualities, in preparation for the new cyele, that of evolution. Through the potential conditions of nature (ashes of Titans) were then gradually evolved the astral, physical, and mental bodies of the races of mankind. The human being is made up of both natural and spiritual elements; the _ spiritual springing from the life (blood) of the incarnate Self within.
