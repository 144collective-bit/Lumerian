A symbol of the Absolute, the Eternal unmanifest Being, the Emanator of the Universe. ‘In the beginning, the Eternal or Absolute Being, Zerfna-Akerana, produced two beings: Ahura-Mazda ( of Light) and Ahriman (King of Darkness).
