A symbol of the lower quaternary, or underworld. “Yomi is spoken of as the abode of Susa-no-wo, who, according to one myth, was appointed to rule this region. We also hear of the deities of Yomi, the armies of Yomi, the ugly females of Yomi, and the Road-wardens of Yomi.” —W. G. Asron, Shinto, p. 54. The lower planes constitute the realm over which the desire-nature (Susa-no-wo) presides. The “ deities and armies of Yomi”’ are the desires, the ‘ugly females’’ are the lower emotions or passions, and the ‘‘ Roadwardens”’ are aspirations on the upward path, or moral regulations in the lower mind to ‘make straight the way of the Lord.” See Coma, Curip, DarEnsss (lower), PEACHES, QUATERNARY, SUSA-NO-WO, TARTARUS, WOMEN’S LIFE.
