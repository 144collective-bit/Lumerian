SON JUBAL, OF LAMECH AND AD A symbol of the germ of higher emotions aroused through reflection (Lamech) and experience (Adah). *‘ And his brother’s name was Jubal: he was the father of all such as handle the harp and pipe.’""-—Gen. iv. 21.
