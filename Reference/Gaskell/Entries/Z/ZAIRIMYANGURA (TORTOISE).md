A symbol of the lower desire will. “The daéva Zairimy is an evil creature that destroys from midnight to dawn thousands of creatures of the good Spirit.’”-—Vendidad, XIII. This signifies the opposite of the true will, that is, the negative will which has no discrimination, and allows the higher impulses to pass unrecognised. See Creatures (small), Darvas, TORTOISE.
