Significant of the omniscience of the Higher Self on the mental plane. “‘The eyes of the White Head are diverse from all other eyes. Above the eye is no eyelid, neither is there an eyebrow over it. ... This eye is never closed; and there are two converted into one. All is right; there is no left. He slumbereth not, and he requireth not rotection.’’—Kabbalah, ‘*The Greater oly Assembly,’ Ch. IX. There is a reference here to the personality being merged into the Individuality, when the two become one, and there remains no lower self. See COUNTENANCE, Face, Heap, WHITE, YMIR.
