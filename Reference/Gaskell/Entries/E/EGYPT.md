Out of mental experiences, or learning, is the Divine Child, the Son of mind (man), educated as a means to tho end of the soul’s salvation. “This Egypt, that is our present life which oppressed us ; when flattering may aid when pressing us: and that which, when cherishing, crushed us with the yoke of bondage, may show the way of liberty, while it tortures.’’—ST.
