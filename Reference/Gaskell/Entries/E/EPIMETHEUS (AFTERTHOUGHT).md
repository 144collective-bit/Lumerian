A symbol of the personality, or lower mental nature. *“‘For Prometheus and Epimetheus are one Man, according to the system of allegory,—that is, Soul and Body.” ** But the Greeks call the earthy Adam, Epimetheus, who is counselled by his own Mind, that is, his brother (Prometheus).”—Zosrmus, Fragments, 26, 27, EQUINOX The ‘‘earthy Adam" is the lower mind or personality in its mentalbody, and this is emanated by the higher mind or individuality (Prometheus) in its causal-body or immortal soul. ** Hermes took Pandora to Epimetheus, ese made her his wife.’’—Smith’s Class. The mental principle (Hermes) conveyed the emotion-nature (Pandora or Eve) to the lower mind (Epimetheus or Adam), and these became allied in the human soul. See Apam (lower), Droscuri, Eve, GosHURUN, HERMES, INDIVIDUALITY, Marriacr, PaNpORA, PERSONALITY, PROMETHEUS, WIFE, WOMAN.
