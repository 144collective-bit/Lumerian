A symbol of entrance into a new state or condition of the soul, the number seven signifying completion of the former state. Or the symbol may refer to duality of aspects on the four lower planes. **The ‘eighth day ‘is always typical of resurrection. The day, after the seventh, or Sabbath, answers to ‘ the first day of the week’ on which Christ rose... . ‘Seven days’ include the periods proper to the first creation The eighth day brings us in type into a new order of things.”’—A.
