Anru, ANUBIS, MAN, A symbol of the First Logos, or. the Higher Self. ** The name of the first celestial Emperor of China was Tae-haou,—excessive splendour,—because his perfect holiness and virtue were resplendent as the luminaries of heaven.”’—Ktpp, China, p. 103. The First Logos is the Supreme Ruler, and being equal to go forth in his universe and to accomplish his work below, he is said to have heen perfectly endowed with every quality, and holy as the Light of Truth which lighteth the Heaven above.
