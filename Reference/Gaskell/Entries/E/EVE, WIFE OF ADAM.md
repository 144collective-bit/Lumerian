A symbol of the emotion-nature united tu the mental-nature of the lower mind. ‘* And the man called his wife’s name Eve; because she was the mother of all living.’—GeEn. iii. 20. And the mind recognises the lifeprinciple within the soul to be the emotion-nature, for it is the originator or former of all qualities that subsist, that is, of all qualities that have in them the germ of the higher life. **Eve is the right magical child, for she is the matrix in which the Love-desire stood in Adam. She was Adam's Para. disical Rose-garden in peculiar love wherein he loved himself.”—J. BozHme, Mysterium Magnum, p. 83.
