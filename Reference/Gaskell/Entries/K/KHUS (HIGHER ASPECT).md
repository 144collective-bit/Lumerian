A symbol of the monads of Life, or the Individualities. ‘* Khu,—a shining or translucent of the spiritual economy of a man which dwelt with his soul in the edhu or spiritual body.’"—Bunpaes, Book of the Dead, Vol. I. lxii. ‘*May I become a Khu therein (thy Belds of Sekket Aaru).”"—Jbid., Ch. CX. Flay thisbe: eatvannisen ot! ie id the form, I, the Self, realise Myself. ** And behold, the Khus, each of whom therein (Sekhet-Aaru) is nine cubita in height, reap the wheat and the barley side by side with Hera-khuti (young Horus).’’—Bup@r, Book of the Dead, Ch. CXLIX. p. 486. The monads of Life, when perfected through experience in the “ field’’ or arena of the lower life, inherit the treasures of Wisdom and Love, together with the indwelling Christ. The “nine cubits in height” signifies perfection attained on the lower planes. The perfected ‘‘ Khus” are the individual souls (the saints), who rise with Christ to the higher planes of consciousness at the end of the cycle. See AKESI, ANGELS, ARENA, BopHISATVAS, Ka, MONAD OF LIFE, QAHU, Reapine, Sanu, Sarmnts, Toms.
