CXLIX. p. 486. The monads of Life, when perfected through experience in the “ field’’ or arena of the lower life, inherit the treasures of Wisdom and Love, together with the indwelling Christ. The “nine cubits in height” signifies perfection attained on the lower planes. The perfected ‘‘ Khus” are the individual souls (the saints), who rise with Christ to the higher planes of consciousness at the end of the cycle.
