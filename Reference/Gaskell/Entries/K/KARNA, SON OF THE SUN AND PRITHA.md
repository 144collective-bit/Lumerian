A symbol of self-esteem and personal dignity, produced by the Spirit (sun) acting through the natural order of emotion (Pritha). **Karna exhibited in a high degree fortitude, chivalrous honour, self-sacrifice, and devotion. Especially remarkable for a liberal and generous disposition, he never stooped to ignoble practices like his friends the Kurus, who were intrinsically bad men.”’—Mon. Wuiiuiams, Jndtan Wisdom, p. 384. ‘*‘Karna’’ was said to have been born ‘clothed in armour,” and that ‘* Indra conferred upon him enormous strength.” This means that selfesteem is able from the first to withstand the assaults of the lower selfinterests and is powerful to maintain an ideal of devotion (Indra). Nevertheless, self-esteem fights on the side of the desires (Kurus), and is finally slain by the personality (Arjuna) as it attains to selflessness and perfection. See Ansuna, InDRA, Kurvs, PRITHA, Quaitizs, Sun.
