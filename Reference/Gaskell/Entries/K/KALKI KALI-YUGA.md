A symbol of the present period and process of evolution in which Wisdom and Love are gradually developed from ignorance and desire. **The Kali-yuga or fourth age of the world was supposed to commence at the death of Krishna. The Hindu idea of a succession of four Yugas or ages, in which & gradual deterioration of the human race es place, has its counterpart among the Romans in the Golden, Silver, Brazen, and Iron Ages, as described in Ovid's Metamorphoses (I. 89, etc).’°—Mon. Wriiiams, Indian Wisdom, p. 333. The period of evolution of Spirit from matter commences from the death of the Archetypal Man (Krishna). Prior to that is a triple period of involution, wherein Spirit descends into matter through the buddhic (Golden), mental (Silvern), and astral (Brazen) planes, and completes itself in the physical plane (Iron). This gradual obscuration of Spirit has the appearance of deterioration of the soul. See ARO. MAN, GOLDEN AGE, [RON AGE, Kaki, Scorpio.
