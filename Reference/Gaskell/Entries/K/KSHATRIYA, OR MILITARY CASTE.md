A symbol of the mental body centralising the faculties of the lower mind which contend against ignorance and emotion. *“The Kshatriya, he (Manu) commanded to protect the people, to bestow ifte, to offer sacrifices, to study the eda, and to abstain from attaching himself to sensual pleasures.'"'"—Laws of Manu, Ch.
