A symbol of the soul’s inner memory of experiences, the child of the Self, which is the means of directing the higher mental qualities to the causal self, for it is of the higher mind. “To Kalchas first spake Agamemnon with look of ill: ‘ Thou seer of evil, never yet hast thou told me the thing that is leasant. Evil is ever the joy of thy eart to prophesy, but never yet didst thou tell any good matter nor bring it to pass.” ’’'—IJitad, Bk. I. The lusts and desires of the lower self having become intensified, the Desire-mind (Agamemnon) experiences @ sense of dissatisfaction which proceeds from the dawn of the higher consciousness within the soul. The conscience, an expression of the inner memory of experience, decides that certain things are evil, and this being distasteful to the Desire-mind, the sense of rebellion is stirred. The Desire-mind realises that the higher mind always foresees evil, since indeed it is through the apprehension of folly that wisdom is awakened. “‘The higher and lower powers of the soul meet in the imaginative faculty, which is the psychical organ of ang | and self-consciousness’’ (Plotinus).— WHITTAKER, The Neo-Platonists, p. 52. See AGAMEMNON, CONSCIENCE, EXPERIENCES, GaTHAS (chanting), INTUITION, REMINISCENCE, SHIPS OF GREEKS, WorD (living). IN
