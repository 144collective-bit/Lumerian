A symbol of the lower quaternary as energised by kama-manas. ‘** Ares in the form of a boar sete all evils in commotion.’—PLurarcn, Of Love, § 13. ‘Ares in the form of a boar with savage teeth, bringing death, came to weave the web of fate about Adonis.” —Nonnvs, Dionysiaca. It is through the activity of kamamanas, the desire-mind or “devil,” that evolution at first takes placa, and that all the evils in the world exist. The “‘ web of fate”’ entangling the Higher Self is the desire-mentality 117 BOAR which imprisons the Self, but which is the means, through Self-conquest, of the attainment of bliss and the results of experience. See ADONIS, ARES, Mans.
