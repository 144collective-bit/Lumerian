Symbols of monads of form which become the centres of consciousness for the personalities. They are the ‘‘pitris of the demons,’’ that is, the astral centres of the desires, passions, appetites, and instincts of the lower nature. They have been developed in animal-man on the moon in the lunar cycle. See LuNaR OYCLE, MoNnapD oF FORM, Moon (waxing), Prrris (lunar).
