```text
A symbol of the causal-body during the involution of the desire-mental qualities. In the fourth division (kamamanas) of the Tuat (Life-cycle) according to the Book Am-Tuat, Afu-Ra (Higher Self) has to leave his Boat in order to pass through waterless deserts (illusion and _ error). He and his company (ideals) therefore proceed on the back of a two-headed serpent (desire-mind). Tho ‘hidden gods” (experiences to develop the soul), who march in front of the boat to help it along, are all under the control of Anubis (physical body of sensation and action).—See Buper, Egypt. Heav. and Hell, Vol. III. p. 133. See Aru-ni, ANUBIS, DESERTS, Szxer, SERPENT, Tvart.
```
