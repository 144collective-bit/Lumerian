A symbol of the comparative valuation of the forces (spirits) for good and evil in the soul, between incarnations. “The weighing of Rashnfi the just, with the balanoe of spirits which renders no favour on any side, neither for the 2 pager nor yet for the wicked.’”’— The Minok-hired, Hava, Hesays. The “‘ weighing ”’ is the point where the balance in favour of the Higher Self may be estimated. The righteous and the wicked are symbolic of the individuality and the personality.
