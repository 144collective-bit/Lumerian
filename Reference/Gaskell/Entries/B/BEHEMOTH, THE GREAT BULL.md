A symbol of the astro-physical matrix of all living things in the lower nature. It proceeds from its prototype upon the buddhic plane, and is fertilised by the mental seedgerms of all forms. “‘The word behemoth literally means beasts, from the singular noun behemah, from the word baham, to be dumb.” *““By Behemoth and Leviathan are symbolised the two great hindrances of human salvation, viz. the Flesh and the Devil. . To speak more precisely, it seems ‘probable that Behemoth represents the Evil One acting in the animal and carnal elements of man’s own constitution, and that Leviathan symbo. lises the Evil One energising as his external enem Behemoth is the Enemy within Us, eviathan ia the Enemy sar us."’—WorpswortH, Bible, Job, . 95. Pre Behold now behemoth, which I made with thee; he eateth grass as an ox, etc.’’—JoB xl. 15-24. The astro-physical nature is said to be made in alliance with the mental nature, and to have ite life awakened BEHUDET by the faint aspirations or desires (grass) on the lower planes. Ite manifestation (strength) is in the lower nature (loins, belly, etc.). It is the means whereby the Divine scheme of soul-probation is to be carried out (chief of the ways of God), the “ sword of the spirit,” or Divine will, being exercised upon it. “Hail, holy Bull, beneficent, who makest increase. Who dost bestow thy gifts upon the faithful. Hail to thee whom : Ahriman kills.”’— Vendidad, The prototype of the lower quaternary being on the buddhic plane, it is said to die on its descent to relativity (Ahriman) on the lower planes. As it contains the promise and potencies of perfection, it bestows the higher qualities upon the faithful in proportion to their efforts. “In the names Behemut and Levitiya, sometimes given respectively to this Bull and Fish (of the Ottoman Sufi cosmogony) we seem to recognise the Behemoth and Leviathan of the book of Job.”°—Grss, Hist. of Ottoman Poetry, Vol. I. p. 39. See AnRIMAN, Bort, Grass, Jos, LxviaTHAN, Ox (sarsack), VISCERA, Vira arms, Woms (cosmic). BEHODET, GOD OF :~— A symbol of the Higher Self seated upon the buddhic plane. **Thoth spake to Horbehfidti: ‘Thou art a great protector.’ From that day the sacred bark of Horus is called Great of Protection.’’ (God of Behfdet is the same as Horus, or Horbehddti.)}—Legend of Winged Sun-disk. The higher mind (Thoth) uttered iteelf to the Self: ‘“‘ Thou art indeed @ great protector of myself.” And from that period of development the causal-body (bark) is known to be a haven of protection for the qualities ; for through its functionings the soul is brought to a state of stability, centralised on the higher mind plane. See Boat, Cavusat-BoDy, DwELLING (palace), Honsenfp11, Horus, THors.
