The perfected soul (Teta) subsists upon the Truth which comes to it in the course of its further development, and its activities are in accordance with the Divine will (Shu). It taketh part in the life of the higher qualities ). ‘* By bread is meant all that is spiritual and celestial, which is the food of angels, ‘the bread which came down from heaven.’ ’? — SWEDENBORG, Arc.
