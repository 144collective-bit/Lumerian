### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-breastplate-of-judgment-ocr-body`

```text
This symbol signifies an assembiage of twelve disciplined qualities, or aspects of the Divine life in the evolution of mental qualities, in close affectional relation with the spiritual mind (Aaron), and connected with the buddhic and astral centres of activity (shoulderpieces). These qualities are illumined by the Divine Ray (the girdle). The breastplate was ‘“‘foursquare,”’ that is, the fleld of the qualities is in the quaternary ,— the four planes below atma. The double thickness relates to active and passive conditions of qualities.—Sce Exon, xxxix. See AARON, CoLouRs, DISCIPLES, Erxop, Germs, GIRDLE, JEWELS, Kust1, Precious, QUATERNARY, SHEWBREAD, TRIBES, TWELVE, Uni AND THUMMIN.
```
