```text
A symbol of the spiritual nature to which the higher and lower qualities of the soul are attached. ‘“‘By bones in holy Scripture we understand virtues; as it is written, ‘The Lord keepeth all his bones; not one of them shall be broken’ (Ps. 121 BOOK xxxiv. 20). ‘ His flesh shall waste away, and his bones which were covered be laid bare’ (Jon xxxiii. 21). For when every outward peat is worn away by the pressure of the rod, the bones of inward firmness are laid bare. For what is meant in this place by the word flesh but fleshly peere itself ? Or what by bones but the virtues of the soul? The flesh therefore wastes away, and the bones are laid bare, because while carnal pleasure is brought to nought by the reproof of scourges, those steady virtues are laid open, which had long been concealed, as it were, beneath the fiesh.’’— Grecory THE GREAT, Morals on the Book of Job, Vol. III. pp. 40, 46. See CORPSE DEVOURED BY BIRDS.
```
