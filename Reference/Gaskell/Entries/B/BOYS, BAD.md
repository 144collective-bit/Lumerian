Symbolic of uninstructed qualities of the lower mind, which need to be transmuted and raised. *‘A boy, a worker of iniquity, ran up and came against the shoulder of Jesus, ing to make sport of Him, or to hurt Him, if he could.’ '—Goepel of Pseudo- Mathew.
