Symbolic of ambitions, or elevated desires of a selfish nature. ** Separated by evil strife, the human members wander each in different directions along the breakers of the sea of life. Just so it is with plants and with fishes dwelling in watery halls, and beasts whose lair is in the mountains, and birds borne on wings.’’—Empedocles, FAIRBANKS, 247. When evolution commenced, and the struggle for separate existence began, the functions of the Oversoul are said to be scattered, inasmuch as through desire and opinion a continual surging and unrest serve to stir the latent emotions by means of which growth is accomplished. So it is with the dawn of the emotions (plants), the astro-mental nature, the ambitions (beasts), the ideals and aspirations (birds). See ARO. MAN, Bmps, Driseoex. BERMENT, EVOLUTION, FisH, Muxm.. BERS, Mounrain (lower), Prawnrs, Sea, SEPARATION, Staire, WINGS. |
