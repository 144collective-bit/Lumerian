Symbolic of a system of vortices in matter, by which vibrations are set up. See Eppy. WHITE, OR WHITENESS. A symbol of perfect purity,—the external nature as spotiess as the internal. ‘*‘And his head and his hair were white as white wool, white as snow.”’—ReEv. i. 14. And the mental and higher aspects of the Self were of immaculate purity. ** Since by the head is understood love and also wisdom in their first principles, it follows that by hair is to be understood love and wisdom in their ultimates.”— SwEDENBORG, Apoc.
