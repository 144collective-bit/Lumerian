A symbol of Wisdom on the buddhic plane, pure and free from the lower nature. WICKED ‘* Then Hephaistos the famed crafteman to make harangue among them, to do kindness to his dear mother, whitearmed Hera.’’—Iliad, Bk. I. Then the Creative Mind, or Christ triumphant, commenced to co-ordinate the higher qualities and so induced them to focus their activities upon Wisdom seated above. See ARMS OF BODY, HEPHAISTOS, Hera, Muse.
