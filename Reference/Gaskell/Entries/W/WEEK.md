A symbol of the six periods of Divine activity in the cycle of involution, together with the pralaya of rest, passivity, or balance, which precedes the opening of the present cycle of evolution. Otherwise, a week signifies a period of completion. ‘“‘“A week in Daniel and elsewhere, signifies an entire period, from beginning to end, and is predicated of the church.
