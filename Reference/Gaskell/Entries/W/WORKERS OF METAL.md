Symbolic of qualities upon the mental plane, attached to high ideals which will endure and consolidate and overcome the lower desires. ‘But Horbehudti was behind the enemies in the bark of Ra, a lance of iron and a chain were in his hand. With him were the raised soul-qualities who wero working tugether with him. See Arms, BLACKsMITHS, CUTTING, Inrr1ations, IRON, METALS, QUALITIES, TUBALCAIN, WALKS, WEAPONS.
