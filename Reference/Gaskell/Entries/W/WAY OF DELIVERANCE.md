```text
A symbol of the Self whose manifestation in the soul brings about ite liberation from the lower nature. ‘* Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, and the truth and the life: no one cometh unto the Father but by me.’’— JOHN xiv. 6. The indwelling and partially manifest Self explains that ita further manifestation in the soul is the path to the unmanifest Self (the Father), and that it is only through the manifest that the unmanifest can be approached by the evolving souls of humanity. See ABSOLUTE, DEVAYANA FATHER, HIGHER AND LOWER SELVES, INCARNATIoN, Lost (forest), PatH (twofold), SELF, Sout (middle), Works (path).
```
