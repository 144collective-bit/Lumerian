A symbol of the flowing out of Divine compassion towards the monads or centres of consciousness suffering on the lower planes. ‘* When at length Isis was alone, she opened the chest, laid her face to that of the dead Osiris and kissed him and wept.” —Pucrarcn, Isis and Osiris, § 17. When at length the Wisdom principle reaches the plane of action whereon it is supreme, the time arrives when the lower experiences are reviewed through introspection, and then arises the spirit of infinite compassion for the sufferings of the lower nature. See CHEST, DEATH OF OSIRIS, EXPERIENCES, Faces, Isis, Monap, Osrzis, WIsDoM.
