Symbolic of the way of the soul to liberation and bliss, by means of effort and learning. So far is it from being true that works cease when sanctification is attained, that it is not until after one’s sanctification that right activity, love to all creatures, and most of all to one’s enemies, and peace with all, begin. Ecstasies are soon over, but union with God becomes an abiding possession of the soul, even when, in the midst of the soul’s outward activity, that union seems to be withdrawn’”’ (Eckhart). —UEBERWEG, Hist of Philos., Vol. 1. p. 480. See Hymns, LIBERATION, PatTH (twofold), Way.
