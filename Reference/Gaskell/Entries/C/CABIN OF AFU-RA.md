## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-cabin-of-afu-ra-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the innermost shrine of the causal-body, or World-soul, on the buddhic plane. It signifies 135 CAIAPHAS the confinement or involution of Spirit in Matter, and stands for the Incarnation of R&A (Afu meaning flesh), or the sacrifice of the ‘‘Lamb of God’’ symbolised by the ramheaded R&, the Divine Ram sacrificed in matter, for the redemption of mankind. See ApytTum, Aru-RA, CANOPY, CAUSAL-BODY, CRUCIFIXION OF CHRIST, Heart, INCARNATION, Lams, Ram, SARINE.
```
