A symbol of the Archetypal Formuniverse, or the Monad of Form. It is the spiritual universe which is the formative pattern of the mental, astral and physical universes. Cosmos is the Macrocosm. ** Cosmos is second God, a life (living thing) that cannot die; it cannot be that rive per of this immortal life should die. things in Cosmos are parte of Cosmos, and most of all is man the rational animal . . . after the image of the Cosmos made and having mind beyond all earthly lives ”’ (Corpus Hermeticum, VII1).—MeEap, T. G. Hermes, Vol. II. p. 125. The Archetypal Pattern, or Life and Form Scheme of the solar universe, represents the Second Logos, or second Divine outpouring of Life on the higher planes. This spiritual Pattern is fixed and permanent and not subject to successive changes of state; consequently it is free of time. (Time only enters into manifestation when the Pattern is reproduced bit by bit on the lower planes. This is very plainly taught in the same treatise,—‘‘ God, makcth 42on ; AZon, Cosmos ; Cosmus, Time ; and Time, Becoming.” —Jbid., p. 176.) All things manifest are in degrees of representation of the Divine Pattern above. In humanity thero is the greatest number of degrees, for mind is present in man. The human soul is the microcosmic unit of the Macrocosm, and possesses the Divine Spark which is beyond all lower lives. “The basic element of most of the ancient, and to this day of many of the modern, religions of the world is, the idea of a perfect invisible universe above, which is the real and true paradigm or ideal model, of the visible universe below, the latter being the reflection, simulacrum or shadow, of the invisible perfect ideal above. . . . The idea of the Upper ideal but real and true, and the Lower apparently real, but in truth changeable and untrue, goes through the entire Apocalypse of St. John, is in St. Paul, and in the Epistle to the Hebrews.’”—I.
