A symbol of the passage of the spiritual egos, or monads of life through the cycle of manifestation. 158 CIRCLE “4s Humanity is the crown of the wimal kingdom, so is the ‘ Perfect Man’ te crown of Humanity. It is to this sage of the Perfect Man, who by contmplation and by virtue can enter into the pure thought of the First Intelligence, that all things consciously or unconsciously rive; for when the soul has reached this punt it is ready to pass back into the beom of that glorious Being whence it issued on its journey ages ago. This joumey is called the ‘ Circle of Existence.’ The spark of Divine Light or effluent Beng, descends through the Intelligences, the Souls, the Spheres, and the Elements tll it reaches Earth which is the lowest point on its downward course; and this i the ‘Outward Track’ or the ‘Arc of Descent.” The upward journey is then begun through the Mineral, the Vegetable, the Brute, and Humanity, till the stage of the Perfect Man is reached, when the Soul pesses back into the embrace of the First Inteligence whence it set forth ; and this is the ‘Homeward Track,’ or the ‘ Aro of Ascent.” And when it is achieved the journey is accomplished’ (Ottoman Sufi’s “Doctrine of the Soul ’’).—Grss, History of Ottoman Poetry, Vol. I. p. 52. The ‘‘ Perfect Man ™ is the Personality perfected at the end of the cycle, and ready to be merged into the Individuality, or in other terms, the lower Self about to become one with the Higher Self (First Intelligence). The spiritual ego (Divine spark) descends through the higher planes, to be embodied on the lower planes (the earth), and from thence begins its upward course from low forms of life to higher. This represents the evolution of the personality until the human stage is reached: then there is @ fresh spiritual outpouring and junetion with the Individuality is effected. From this point the human personality starts on its long course upwards to the stage of perfection, when the spiritual ego is liberated from the lower nature and is born into the higher. ‘* Ye must be born anew (or from above).'’—JouN iii. 7.
