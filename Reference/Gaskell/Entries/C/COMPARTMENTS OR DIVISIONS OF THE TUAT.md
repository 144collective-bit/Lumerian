Symbolic of twelve periods or stages of the manvantara or great Cycle of Life. They correspond with the twelve ‘‘tribes,’’ ‘* disciples,°’ and ‘‘signs’’ of the Zodiac. They are, as it were, sections of a spiral through which Life progresses in the order of time. See ‘Eon (thirteenth), DIscrezes, MANVANTARA, TRIBES (twelve), Tuat, TWELVE, ZODIAC.
