A symbol of a change in an upward direction of the ruling energies in the personality. ‘Except ye be converted and become as little children, ye shall not enter the kingdom of heaven.’’—Mar. xviii. 3. Distaste EXPAN173 CONVICTION for the lower is effected through contrasting the lower with an object of a higher character, and turning attention to the latter. See CONVICTION, HEAVEN, LITTLE ONES.
