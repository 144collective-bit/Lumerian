A symbol of the Divine Artificer. That aspect of the One Life which constructs and puts together. ** Tvashtar is the clever-handed carpenter-god, who, in particular, manufactured with his hatchet the thunderbolt of Indra, but also, in general, ‘ adorned heaven and earth—the parents, and all things with their forms,’ whence heaven and earth are called ‘ the artificer’s pair.’ ‘Tvashtar produced thee (Brihaspati) from all existing things, from hymn after hymn, the skilled artificer.’’°—H.
