Symbolic of new mental forms begotten of mind and emotion. These are opinions, ideas, and impressions formed in the lower mind; or they are aspects of mental qualities seeking expression in various ways. ‘That children signify truths, and in an opposite sense falses, is because in the spiritual sense of the Word, by ‘ generations’ are meant spiritual generations, - neither does spiritual generation give birth to any other sons and daughters, than truths and goods.’—SwEpDENBORG, Apoc. Rev., n. 139. ***In sorrow shalt thou bring forth children,’ points to the efforts ne for attaining knowledge.” —Joun Scotus. See BEGETTING, DAUGHTERS, CONCEPTION, GENERA
