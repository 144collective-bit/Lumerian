A symbol of the principles of Love and Wisdom which are united in Truth. ** So speaking he (Hephestus) rose up and set in his dear Mother's (Hera's) hand the Double Cup, and spake to her.” —Jliad, Bk. I, trans. Bucxriey. Thus having manifested Its powers, the Creative Mind rises to Buddhi, where it delivers to the Wisdomprinciple (Hera) the dual aspects of the Higher Self,—intuition and love,— and then addresses Itself to the Wisdom within itself.
