A symbol of supremacy over the lower nature. In its highest aspect it stands for the power of atma. ** Kether (the Crown) is the principle of all the principles, the Secret Wisdom, the most exalted Crown, with which all crowns and diadems are adorned.’’—Zohar, ILI. fol. 288 b. The highest plane (Kether) is the most interior of all. It is atma, the hidden Truth and Life which confer immortality upon all victorious souls. 188 CROWN ‘In that day shall the Lord of hosts be for a crown of glory, and for a diadem of beauty, unto the residue of his people.”’ —Is. xxviii. 5. When the cycle ends, the Higher Self shall be supreme in beauty and harmony within the higher qualities which have been evolved from below. See CournsE, Keturr, LION (man), TIARA.
