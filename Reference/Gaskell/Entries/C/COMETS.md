These are symbols of elemental disruptive forces at the close of a manvantara. They are liberated primarily on the mental plane, and produce feelings of umrest and disquietude which are reflected upon the astral and physical planes, and precede physical dissolution. The 167 COMPARTMENTS mind and will are able to control them until the decree of the Absolute is to be fulfilled. See AatT (waters), CONFLAGRATION, KALKI, RENOVATION.
