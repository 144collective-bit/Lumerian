## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

A symbol of a leading mental faculty which directs other mental faculties and qualities of the soul.

> "Mystically, the centurion is everyone who rules over his members, senses and faculties, so that they, as it were soldiers, may fight for and serve God."—St. Gregory, *Morals on the Book of Job*, Vol. I. p. 332.

See RULERS, SOLDIERS.
