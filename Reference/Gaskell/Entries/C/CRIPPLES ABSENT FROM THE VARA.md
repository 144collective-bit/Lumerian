Signifying that in the involutionary cycle all monads are perfect, and CROCODILE capable of carrying out the work which is set for them. ‘‘And God saw everything that he had made, and behold, i¢ was very good.’’°— Gen. i. 31. ‘‘There should be no cripples, or diseased ; no poverty, no meanness there.” —Vendidad, II.
