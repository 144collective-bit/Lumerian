A symbol referring to the seven CONSTELLATIONS planes, or the seven root-races of bumanity. See Races, RisHes, RoOorT-RACES, Rounpbs. CONSTELLATIONS, ISMS -— Groups of stars (mental faculties) which stand for the various mental states and experiences of the souls. ** In the present universe divine wisdom is, as it were, codified in the constellations. The stars are called in Babylonian, ‘ writing of the heavens.’ The moving stars of the zodiac in their constellations are especially interpreters of the divine will. . . . In the Babylonian religion the symbols of the gods are the same as those of the constellations.’’— A. JEREmias, Old Test., ete., Vol. I. p. 49. See Dracon (red) Stars (fixed), ZODIAC.
