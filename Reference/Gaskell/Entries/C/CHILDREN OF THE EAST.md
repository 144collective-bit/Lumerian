A symbol of the sun, moon, planets, and stars which have their birth, as it were, in the east where they first become apparent. These again are the symbols of the Higher Self (sun), lower Self (moon), etc. Thus, the ‘‘ greatest of all the children of the east '’ is the Higher Self, the Sun of righteousness.
