A symbol of the weakness of the lower nature in the presence of difficulties which the higher nature must surmount in raising and.purifying the soul. ** And when he drew nigh, he saw the city and wept over it, saying, If thou hadst known in this day, even thou, the things which belong unto peace! but now they are hid from thine eyes.’’— LUKE xix. 41, 42. The Christ-soul had come to take the citadel of the lower activities, or capacity of mind wherein the qualities have been wont to be used. He had come to his own from afar, and when he saw the task which lay before him, the humanity in ‘‘ Jesus ”’ was stirred, hence the statement is made that ‘“‘He wept.’’ The Christ, however, did not weep. It was the distress and distrust of the lower nature to accomplish the task which lay before the Higher that appalled ; and it was only after the lower was able to see that it was not the Doer but only the illusory agent, that the triumph was ultimately achieved.
