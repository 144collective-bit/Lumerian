A symbol of the aspiration, or spirit, of Truth in the moral nature. ** Jesus comes to Johanna to be baptieed. Jesus comes as a simple ‘ap* gseoki initiation into t. Oe eal of Johanna. But Johanna is not to be deceived, and immediately recognises him as the Master, Manda d’Hajje, the Gnosis of Life.’’—Codex Rewoe: Tractate XI. of the Right Hand This signifies the approach of the Christ-soul to the spirit of Truth in the moral nature, to which it is content to appeal so that it may unfold and realise the things of eternal Life which are contained within the soul. The Christ-soul comes as a little child, and is ready to take the Truth first hand,—to acquire it through the experiences which are to follow his “ initiation,” or deeper realization of some established fact of the spiritual life. But the spirit of Truth fails not to perceive the Life, Light, and Love which now illumine it and cognise it. Bo it follows that the Truth-nature realises that the Christ is above it, and it does homage. The meaning of ‘* Manda d’Hajje”’ is Truth-Goodness in union, and ‘“‘the Master” is the Higher Self,—the Divine [luminator of those qualities which have sought 415 JOHN the way to Peace and to union with the Self.
