## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

"Eridu" is a symbol of the higher planes—atma-buddhi-manas, and its "gates" stand for the higher mental plane through which there is access from the higher planes to the lower, and from the lower to the higher.

> "Eridu. This ancient city, the older name of which was Eri-dugga ('The Holy City'), was the Jerusalem, the Umritza, the Mecca of Chaldea."—G. Rawlinson, *Rel. Systems*, p. 16.

> "Men-nofer (Memphis), 'the good place,' is the equivalent of the name of the ancient seaport of Babylonia, Eridu."—A. H. Sayce, *Gifford Lectures*, p. 138.
