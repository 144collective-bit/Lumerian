A symbol of the buddhic plane, the first to possess relative truth.

> "Aryana-Vaêjo was created a region of delight by Ahura-Mazda."—*Vendidad*, I.

The buddhic plane exists in the innermost remembrance of the ego as it descends in the period of involution, to the lower planes.
