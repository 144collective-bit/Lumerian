## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-quadruped-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the Cosmos as an animal. The head, neck, and body signify the upper planes of atma, buddhi, and higher manas, while the four legs stand for the lower quaternary, that is, the lower manas, astral, etheric, and physical planes of manifestation. If an elephant is given as a symbol of the Cosmos, then the trunk has the same meaning as the sutratma, for through it flows the breath of life, corresponding to the Ray of life from the Supreme. See ANIMAL, BULL (man), ELEPHANT, GRIFFIN, Rat, SPHINX, SuUTRATMA.
```
