A symbol of perfection; or of purification of the qualities on a high buddhic sub-plane. “Hail, thou city of the gods Qahu, who take possession of Khus and gain mastery over the shades (khaibit), who devour vigorous strength and consume filth when their eyes see, and who not the earth.’”"—BupGE, Book of the Ch. CXLIX. The high ideals of perfection are said to take possession of the monads of life (Khus),—the returning Egos,—and also dominate the _ causal-bodies (khaibit), which have overcome the lower desires through spiritual perception, and which conserve not the lower nature (earth).
