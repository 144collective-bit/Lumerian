A symbol of the Spirit of Truth, produced primordially from Spirit and Matter. “Of the divine Titanic hebdomads, Ocean both abides and proceeds, uniting himself to his father [Heaven], and not departing from his kingdom. But all the rest of the Titans, rejoicing tn progression, are said to ‘have given completion to the will of Earth, but to have assaulted their father, dividing themselves from his kingdom, and proceeding et alana order.’’—PRoo.Lus, Tsmous, Of the essential elements of life, Spirit both abides above and proceeds to action below, and is one with the Supreme, not departing from the higher planes. But the other primal conditions, rejoicing in the new cycle of evolution, are said to have given completion to the process of involution into matter, repulsing the direct action of the Supreme Spirit, in separating themselves from the higher nature, and proceeding into the new process and order of evolution. See EarnrH (great) Evo.urion, GE, HEAVEN AND EARTH, INVOLUTION, PosEiwon, Srirnit, Trrans, TLazoo, Uranus, VARUNA.
