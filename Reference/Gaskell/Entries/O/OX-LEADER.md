A symbol of a ruling spirit of reverence for truth. “‘An endeavour is made by the sorcerers to have the babe trampled to death by a herd of oxen; the leading ox, however, stands over the child and prevents it from perishi beneath the feet of the herd.”—A. V. W. JAcKSON, Zoroaster, p. 29. The endeavour of the “ sorcerers,” signifies the instinctive and blind trust to habit and authority, which is calculated to crush out’ the early effort which the Self (the babe) makes to seek and know for iteelf. The highest aspiration, however, which reverences the spirit of truth and reason, more than mere tradition, is the means of protecting the Self. See BIRTH OF KRISHNA, DURASROBO, Herop, Nrnic1, PHARISEES, PRIESTS AND ELDERS, SPARROWS (twelve), TRADITION, ZOROASTER.
