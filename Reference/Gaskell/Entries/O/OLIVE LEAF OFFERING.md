A symbol of a tribute of love. **And the dove came in to him at eventide ; and lo, in her mouth an olive leaf pluckt off: so Noah knew that the waters were abated from off the earth.’”’— GEN. viii. 11. And now, at the conclusion of the re-incarnating period of that stage of the soul’s development, an “olive leaf’? or love tribute offered by the lower self,—as the result of the previous action of the Higher Self,—is brought to the individual Self. Then it is the higher consciousness realises that the power of recognising the Truth is contained within the soul. *““When we have felt the tossings cease, and received the olive leaf, the earnest of the inheritance, from the mouth of the gentle dove, which thus assures us of a world beyond the water-floods, then our freedom is near.’’—A.
