A symbol of four successive states of consciousness on the mental and ORIGINAL buddhic planes in the course of the growth of the soul. **The Student, the Householder, the Hermit, and the Ascetic, these constitute four separate orders which all spring from the order of Householders.”—Laws of Manu, VI. 87. The ‘‘Student’”’ represente an ego manifesting in the mental-body as a centre of consciousness on the four lower mental sub-planes. This is the personality either incarnate or excarmate. The ‘“Householder’’ is the ego seated in the causal-body on the fifth mental sub-plane. Thisis the individuality from which proceed the other three phases of consciousness. The ‘“ Hermit" is the ego manifesting in a centre of consciousness on the sixth mental sub-plane. And _ the ** Ascetic" is the ego manifesting in a centre of consciousness on the buddhic plane. The two last ‘“ orders" are without lower vehicles and receive no vibrations from the lower planes. See ASCETICS, ASRAMAS, CONSCIOUSwess, Hermit, HOUSEHOLDER, MONK, SaNNYASIN, SNATAKA, SPRING-TIME, STUDENT, YELLOW DRESS.
