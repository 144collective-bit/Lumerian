```text
These are the same as the twelve signs of the Zodiac, the twelve Adityas, etc. The signs, or constellations, stand for every department of human experience. ons are portions of great cycles, and 29 AESHM each on is distinguished by special features corresponding with the **twelve tribes,’’ the ‘‘ twelve apostles,’’ ‘‘twelve loaves of shewbread,’’ etc. “Twelve ons,—six being ruled by Adamas, and six by Iabradth.”— Booka of the Saviour. See Pistis Sophia. The AXons here referred to are the twelve divisions of the cycle of life,— six of which are ruled by the lower nature, and six by the Divine nature. The six under Adamas are those containing the souls attached to the things of the lower self,—those in whom the sense of separateness is not killed, and who are of the “old dispensation.”” The six under Iabradth are the six signs above the *earth’’ or lower nature, and which correspond with the triumph and ascent of the Christ in the soul. These are of the “ new dispensation.” “Jesus soared to the space of the Twelve Afons; and all the Rulers and Orders were amazed."’—Pistia Sophia. The Christ-soul now completes ita spiral ascent through the _ twelve departments of human _ experience, whose cyclic progression corresponds with the Twelve signs of the Zodiac, or fields of the Tuat. The ‘“ amazement '’ among the activities of nature, is simply the process of awakenment of the qualities and potencies (evolution) which takes place in the spiritual ascent. See Apamas, ADITyas, ANTHROPOS, APOSTLES, DISCIPLES, JABRAOTH, Saviourns, SEXUAL, TRIBES, TuaT TWELVE, ZODIAC.
```
