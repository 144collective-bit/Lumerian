A symbol of the Spirit of goodness and truth to which the soul aspires. ‘“‘ Homa grants a good and rich husband to those who have long been maidens, as soon as he (Homa), the wise, is entreated.’-—Homa Yash. The Supreme grants the fulfilment of those dearest wishes for ‘the fullness of him that filleth all in all," which are to be satisfied only through the abstinence and self-denial which are the means of rendering the soul responsive to higher joys. See Homa, NALI-KUVERA, RICHES (higher), WEALTH.
