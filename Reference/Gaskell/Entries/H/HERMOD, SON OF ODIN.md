```text
A symbol of the Higher Self, son of the Logos, born in the soul to become its ruler and redeemer. **Hermod the Lively, Odin’s son took upon himself this mission from Frigg, to find Balder in the underworld. Odin’s horse, Sleipner, was prepared for the journey; Hermod moun him, and rode hastily away.”—The Story of Balder, Prose Edda. owitt, Litera. And now it is that the Christ-soul is born upon the higher planes of experience, and it is this indwelling HEROD spirit which becomes the Saviour of the soul. On the lower planes (underworld) the Christ-soul is deputed by Wisdom (Frigg) to seek the Higher Self (Balder), the Christ within. Then the higher mind (Odin’s horse) is equipped for the expedition : and so the Self within, under the aspect of mind goes forth in the soul conquering and to conquer. The “ liveliness of Hermod ” is expressive of the outgoing energy of the Self. See BALDER, BRIDGE OF GJALL, DEATH OF BALDER, Frica, Hapss, Horssz (QOdin’s), Mopcunn, NogrTs, Opr.
```
