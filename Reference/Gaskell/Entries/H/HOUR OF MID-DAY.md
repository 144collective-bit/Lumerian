A symbol of the middle period of the cycle of involution, or of evolution. If the cycle is divided into seven periods, then the middle period is the fourth. Whether there are six or seven periods depends upon the 873 HOUSE manner of reckoning. The seventh counting downward is the turning point, or ‘‘ day of rest,’’ leaving six on each side: these are the twelve, as in the Zodiac. See ALons (twelve), ITaBRAOTH, SaBBaTH, SEVEN, TWELVE.
