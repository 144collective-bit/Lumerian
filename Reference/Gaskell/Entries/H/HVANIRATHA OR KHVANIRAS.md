A symbol of the Earth,—the physical globe in the planetary chain of seven globes. I (Zaratusht) call upon the region of Qaniratha the splendid; this they (the GAathas) assert as they are stationed in this one (of the seven regions).”— ee XIX. 39 (129); Have, Kesaya, 7 The incarnate Self acclaims the Earth planet as presenting conditions of life and experience very conducive to the growth of the soul. The inner spiritual vibrations (Gathas) from the buddhic plane to raise the soul are operative in the minds of those who are living the physical life on earth. See KARSHVARES, PLANETARY CHAIN, ReEGions, RENOVATION, SOSHYANS.
