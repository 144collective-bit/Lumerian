### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-hanuman-son-of-vayu-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the intellect which proceeds from the higher mind (Vayu). **From the south came the welcome tidings of the discovery of Sita by Hanuman, one of the chief captains of the Vanar host, a son of the a itr by a nymph of paradise.’’—J. OMAN, Great Indian Epics, p. 65. The lower, or astral (south) emotions, when transmuted, bring about the evolution of the intuition (Sita) which the intellect recognises and saves from extermination. The “Vanar host’? are the mental qualities. A ‘‘nymph of paradise”’ is a lower aspect of buddhi. 337 HARLOT See Invectecr, Lanka, Rama, Ravana, Srra, SovurnH, TRANSMUTATION, VIBHISHANA.
```
