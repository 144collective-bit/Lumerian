I then entered the condition of thought wherein mind must needs limit and define everything. I became immersed in self-seeking, fallen low, and in Egyptian darkness. And my companions, Truth and Hope, departed from me. I betook me headlong to the realm of the senses,— Nigh whose centres of astral activity did my consciousness have its abode, SOUTH, SERPENT Waiting until the cycle of the dominance of the senses was ended, And I could capture the experience that through it should come to me, Taking unto me my Pearl,—the treasure laid up for me in Heaven.
