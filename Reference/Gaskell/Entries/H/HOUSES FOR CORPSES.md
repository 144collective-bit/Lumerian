The three houses for the dead bodies of Parsis are symbolical of the physical, astral, and mental bodies which have contained the personality (corpse) and which afterwards gradually disintegrate on their respective planes. The flesh-eating birds of the Dakhma stand as symbols of the rulers of the planes.— See Ezex. xxxix. 4. See Brrps (lower), Corpse, Doa (white), TEMPLE (fire), VULTURE.
