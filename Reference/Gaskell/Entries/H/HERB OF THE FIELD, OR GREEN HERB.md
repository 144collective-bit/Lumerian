```text
A symbol of the primitive desires and affections which are aroused by the activities of the senses. ** And no plant of the field was yet in the earth, and no herb of the field had yet sprung up: for the Lord God had not caused it to rain upon the earth, and there was not a man to till the ground.’’—GEn., ii. 5. And at this early period of the soul's evolution, no desire had as yet appeared upon the lower plane (earth); and no sign of awakening life had yet thrilled the matter from which unfoldment was to take place. For the Divine Truth-Love had not yet caused the forthcoming of stimulative ideas to proceed from buddhi. And no mental faculty was up to the present actively functioning in the lower nature. See AGRICULTURE, Eartu, FI£.p, Grass, GREEN, MAN, PLants, RaIn, THORNS. HERB YIELDING SEED AFTER ITS KIND -— Symbolic of the natural laws of growth and increase in the development of the desires, affections, and faculties of the soul. CRONUS, MUSE, 354 ‘‘And the earth brought forth grass, herb yielding seed after ite kind, and tree bearing fruit, wherein is the seed thereof, after its kind: and God saw that it was good.’’—Grn. i. 12. And the physical or natural law of growth was set in operation, and the laws which worked upon the lower planes of being were established and commenced to act. And all was perfect in the period of involution. In other words, there was an awakenment of the divine life in the lower nature (earth) which then brought forth the desires and affections which give rise to higher states. Also there appeared the aspirations which are to bear fruit on higher planes. And all was made perfect in preparation for forthcoming evolution. See EartTuH, Fruit, GOLDEN AGE, Goop, Grass, GREEN, INVOLUTION, PLANTS, SEEDS OF FOODS.
```
