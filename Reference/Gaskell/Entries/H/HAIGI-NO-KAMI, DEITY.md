## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

A symbol of the physical plane.

> "Haigi-no-kami is said to be the God of the space between the door of the house and the outer gate."—W. G. Aston, *Shinto*, p. 146.

The extremity; the outermost plane of manifestation is the physical. "House" means causal-body. See HESTIA, NEPHTHYS.
