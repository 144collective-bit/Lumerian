A symbol of aspiration which is the expression of the Divine life within the soul. **But hear now the motive for my coming and rejoice thereat; a heavenly voice has been heard by me (Asita) in the heavenly path, that thy son (Buddha) has been born for the sake of supreme knowledge.’’—Buddha-Karita, Bk. I. 62. The lower self (buddhi-manas) in involution, now realises the nature of the higher law whereby ite development is to be quickened; and within it joy is aroused. The Divine nature, —aspiration,—has awakened within it, THE HEBDOMAD and now it recognises that the Son of Righteousness (moral law and conscience) has been born upon the higher planes. See Astra, BrmTreH OF BUDDHA, BuppuHa, SIMEON.
