```text
A symbol of the matter and space side of manifestation which eventuates in the lower nature. ** There were two grand orders of gods : the first and most ancient were the gods of the night, as night preceded light ; end then followed the gods of the light. Of the former, the chief was Hine-nuite-po, the great mother Night, the grandparent of the rest’’—R. Taytor, New Zealand, p. 16. The potential (darkness) precedes the manifest (light). Space-matter OR HIGH HIRANYAGARBHA gives birth to both higher and lower qualities. ** Po, or night, was the great name for Hades.""—Ibid., p. 41. The manifested lower nature in ignorance (darkness) is the underworld, —the lower planes. See Haprs, LEVIATHAN, MATTER, MAYA (lower), MotHrer, Niaut, Po, Woms.
```
