A symbol of the indwelling Self working upwards through the personality towards union with Buddhi, Wisdom. ** Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth. . Thy name is as ointment ured forth ; ‘therefore do the virgins ove thee. Draw me; ; we will run after thee. ...I am black but comely.”’— Sone OF Sonas, i. 2-5. The incarnate Self whose differentiation is Love (ointment) is adjured to express his true nature, for all the 462 LUNAR virtues (virgins) are in harmony with it. Let Love aspire, and Wisdom with the virtues will respond with joy. Wisdom is unapparent, latent (black), but beautiful when expressed. See Buiack, Cupm, Htxkososu, INCARNATION, Marpen, Mout, Name, VIRGIN.
