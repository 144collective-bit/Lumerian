## Full OCR entry body (exact)

### Block `gaskell-1960-entry-lunatic-mind-ocr-body`

```text
A symbol of the astral principle which has been developed in the lunar cycle. It is through the stimulus of the astral or desire-nature that the mind is eventually awakened. When the desires are extremely active in combination with the lower mind there results an unruled and undirected state of soul, very dangerous morally. See ASTRAL PLANE, CHURNING, KaBANDHA, MOON-WAXING.
```
