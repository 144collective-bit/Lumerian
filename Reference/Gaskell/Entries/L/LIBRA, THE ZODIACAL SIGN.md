A symbol of the seventh period of the cycle of life, which is the first period of evolution on the astral plane. The first point of Libra represents the balance between involution and evolution, and the commencement of the evolution from homogeneity to heterogeneity of the many forms and qualities. See Anc, MAN, Equinox, EvotuLIGHT TION, INVOLUTION, Lowss, SABBATH, SEVENTH, Vimco, ZODIAC.
