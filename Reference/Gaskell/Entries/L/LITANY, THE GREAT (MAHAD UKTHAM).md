A symbol of the evolving Divine life proceeding from below upwards. ‘* And that vital essence of his (the relaxed Pragapati’s) which flowed upwards became the Great Litany; it is in quest of that vital fluid that the priests go by means of the Rik and Saman.’’—Saia. Bréh., X. 1, 1, 4. The Archetypal Man (relaxed Prag&pati) has given up his life for humanity. His ‘vital essence ” (blood of Christ) ascends in supplication or aspiration in the human souls. The higher emotions (priests) are allied to the Divine life through the vibrations from above (the Vedas). ‘*The Great Litany is the body or LITTLE Self, &tman.” ‘A bird-like body.”— id., KX. 1, 2, 1 and 5. The Divine life is the Self below when meeting the Self abore (the &tman). The “ body,” or Self below, is aspiring (bird-like). See Arc. MAN, ATMAN, BLOOD, BLoop or LAMB, GarTrHas (chanting), HoMA-JUICE, HYMN SINGING, PRiaaPATI, SINGERS, SOMA-JUICE, VEDA. LITTLE ONES;
