A symbol of the causal-body as containing the means of Selfmanifestation,—all the qualities and powers of the soul. ‘The eighteen first producte of prakriti, vic mane (buddhs), ahankdra, manas, indryas, and tanmétrae form the 454 LION subtile body, which surrounds the soul and accompanies it in all its wanderings. It is termed lingam. ... Every lifehistory is a new self-unfolding of the rakriti before the purusha concerned y means of the lingam.’’—DeErvusseEx, Phil. of Upanishads, p. 242. ‘‘L[inga-sarira comprising the five organs of sense, the five organs of action, the five vital airs, with buddhi and manas."— Mon. WiiiiaMs, Indian Wisdom, p. 119. **The lingasarfram is described in Sarvopanishats. 16, as the vehicle of the organs the prfinas, the gunas, and the ethical qualification, and accordingly is identified with the bands of the heart.” DrvussEn, Phil. of Upanishads, p. 282. The causal-body is first involved complete in potentiality ; afterwards it grows to completion in actuality. See CaUSAL-BODY, KaRANA, Pranas, SENSE-onGaNS, STHULA, VITAL AIRS.
