A symbol of facts of experience for the development of the mind and the moral nature.

> "And his (John's) food was locusts and wild honey."—Matt. iii. 4.

This is the food which signifies the natural means of nourishment, subsistence upon uncooked, simple fare, signifying plain facts.

> "Locusts signify the qualities of the sensual man, or the sensual principle which is the ultimate of the life of man's mind."—Swedenborg, *Apoc. Rev.*, n. 424.
