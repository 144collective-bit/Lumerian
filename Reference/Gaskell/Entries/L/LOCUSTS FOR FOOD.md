A symbol of facts of experience for the development of the mind and the moral nature. ‘* And his (John's) food was locusts and wild honey.’’-—Mar. iii. 4. This is the food which signifies the natural means of nourishment, subsistence upon uncooked, simple fare, signifying plain facts. ‘* Locusts signify the qualities of the sensual man, or the sensual principle which is the ultimate of the life of man’s mind.’’—SwEDENBORG, .4poc. Rer., n. 424. See JOHN BAPTIST.
