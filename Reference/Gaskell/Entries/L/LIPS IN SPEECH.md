A symbol of the expression outwardly of that which is within, either truth or error, good or evil. ** The lip of truth shall be established for ever: but a lying tongue is but for a moment.”—PRov. xii. 19. The expression of truth in the soul brings immortality to life; but the expression of error is evanescent. ‘** Mankind went forth from Ra's two eyes, the gods were created on his lips.” a mei Rel. of Anc. The individuality and the personality proceeded forth from the higher and lower Selves on the mental plane ; the ideals or higher qualities were the expression of the Divine Will and Wisdom on the higher planes.
