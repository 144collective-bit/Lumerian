The cycle of life on the lunar chain. In the lunar cycle the astral life and the desire nature have been developed, culminating in the personality. The personalities are the lunar pitris, or ‘* fathers ’’ of humanity on its form side. The individualities, or solar pitris, are the ‘‘ fathers ’’ on the life side, and bring with them the spiritual element, the Divine ‘‘ sparks ’’ or monads of life. See Days (five), Goat (milch), Moow (lower), Moon (solar), MoonWAXING, PERSONALITY, Prrris (lunar), PITRIYANA.
