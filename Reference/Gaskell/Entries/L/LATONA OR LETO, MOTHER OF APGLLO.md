A symbol of Wisdom, or the buddhic emotion-nature which gives birth to the Self within the soul. ** Leto would therefore (from her name) signify ‘the obscure’ or ‘ concealed,’ not as a physical power, but as a divinity yet quiescent and invisible, from whom issued the visible divinity (Apollo) with all his splendour and _ bnilliancy.”— Smith’s Clase.
