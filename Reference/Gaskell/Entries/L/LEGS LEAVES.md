## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

A symbol of true ideas, or known facts.

> "And the leaves of the tree (of life) were for the healing of the nations."—Rev. xxii. 2.

The acquirement of true ideas perfects the qualities.

> "Christ's Eye pierces through the thick leaves of our secret [thoughts?]"—Wordsworth, *Bible*, John, p. 276.

> "The reason why leaves signify rational truths is because by a tree is signified man."—Swedenborg, *Apoc. Rev.*, n. 936.

See HEALING, MULTITUDES, TREE, TREE OF LIFE.

### Also in this note: LEES; DREGS

A symbol of the lower instincts and desires which remain unpurified.

> "Moab hath been at ease from his youth, and he hath settled on his lees, and hath not been emptied from vessel to vessel."—Jer. xlviii. 11.

The lower nature at first is at ease in the soul; there is little effort towards improvement, and habits of thought bind down to things below (lees). The lower nature has not been purified by being passed into mental forms (vessels) of better thought, so as to leave the lowest forms (the lees) behind.

See TAMAS, VESSEL.
