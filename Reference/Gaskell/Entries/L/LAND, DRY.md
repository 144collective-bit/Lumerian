A symbol of the lower quaternary, —the lower planes of the soul, or ‘* natural man.”’ ‘** And God called the dry land Earth : and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas: and God saw that it was good.”’—GENn. i. 10. And the ‘‘dry land” is named ‘* Karth,”? which term also stands for the lower nature of the Soul, which is the natural man; and the “ gathering together of the waters’ signifies the formation of the astral ‘sea ’’ of the desires. And all is pronounced ‘* good,” that is, perfect in involution for purposes of forthcoming evolution. ‘“These knowledges are the ‘ waters gathered together into one place,’ and are called ‘seas’; but the external man is called ‘dry land,’ and presently *earth.’’’—Swrpenpnore, Arc. Cel. to Gen. i. 10. See EantH, Grounp, Man (natural), QUATERNARY, SEA, WATER.
