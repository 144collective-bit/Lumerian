Emblem of dependence upon the Self; and indicates the ego gifted with spiritual graces of Wisdom and Love.

> "I go about among you an immortal [god], no longer a mortal, honoured by all, as is fitting, crowned with fillets and luxuriant garlands."—Empedocles, Fairbanks, 352.

The Immortal Self moves amid the qualities which are transmuted and no longer perishable, and It is distinguished from its modes of action, as is essential to right perception, for it is crowned with Self-dependence and the "fruits of the Spirit."
