Symbols of astro-mentality, wherein truth (water) lodges ; which is soft and pliable—easily subordinated to truth ; or which easily yields truth. “The ushshu-plant, the dittu-plant of the marsh, the reed and the forest he (Marduk) created, the lands, and the marshes and the swamps"’ (Babylonian Tablet).—L.
