A symbol of the Divine life in the scheme of things. The Tree of Life, which grows up from a small seed and forms a stem, branches, twigs, leaves, and finally fruit and seeds, all typifying the spiritual life which permeates in every part the structure of the universe and the soul. “This everlasting, holy fig-tree stands with roots above, with branches downwards. Its root is that pure Self, that aaa principle. "— Katha. Upanishad, See Breps (two), Oak TREE, SEED, Soma PLANT, TREE OF LIFE, Ye@apRaSIL.
