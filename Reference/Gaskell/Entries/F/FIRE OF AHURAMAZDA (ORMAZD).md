## Reading text (AI-corrected from the OCR, 12 November 2026)

Symbolic of Divine love energy.

> "Then thou, O man, wouldst sit down, chanting the Gathas, and consecrating the good waters and the fire of Ahuramazda."—*Hadokht Nask*, Hava.

Then the Ego by the raising of the consciousness through meditation would be able to recover the past experiences collected in the causal sheath, and to dedicate himself to the pursuit of Truth and to the Love of God to do His will.

> "The principal ideas in the emblem of fire seem to be swift energy and penetrating power, which cleanses and transforms. It is fire as the source of light and heat; it is fire, not so much as burning up what it seizes into ashes, but rather as laying hold upon cold, dead matter, making it sparkle and blaze, and turning it into the likeness of its own leaping brightness; it is a fire as springing heavenwards, and bearing up earthly particles in its shooting spire; it is fire, as least gross of visible [things]; in a word, it is fire as life, and not as death, that is the symbol of God. It speaks the might of His transforming power, the melting, cleansing, vitalising influence of His communicated grace, the warmth of His conquering love. It hath, indeed, an underside of possible judgment, punishment, and destruction, but it hath a face of blessing, of life-giving, of sanctifying power."—A. Maclaren, *Sermons*, 2nd Series, p. 232.

See CAUSAL-BODY, EXPERIENCES, GATHAS (chanting), KATCHAS, LOVE OF GOD, RECOGNITION, RECOLLECTION, REMINISCENCE, SHIPS (Greeks).
