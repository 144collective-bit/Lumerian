A symbol of the desire-energy on the astral plane. . ** Ahriman passed even into Fire, the visible symbol of Ormazd, defiling it with smoke and vapour” (Zoroastrian System).—F. J. The passing of the desire nature into “fire” is the symbol of energising the matter of the lower planes. ‘‘Smoke ” and “‘ vapour” signify the ignorance and illusion accompanying the process. ‘“Fire’’ is a symbol of Atma-buddhi (Ormazqa).
