```text
A designation signifying the Divine Monad or spiritual Prototype in its dual aspect. ‘“Even Ormazd has his Fravashi in relation to Zerdna-Akerana.’’— Zoroastrian System, J. F. CLARKE. On the highest plane the Divine Being is said to bear under its dual aspects of Spirit and Matter a similar relationship to Itself (Absolute) as the lower manifestations bear to each other. The relationship of life and form. See ABSOLUTE, HEAVEN AND EARTH, MonaD, SPARK, ZERANA.
```
