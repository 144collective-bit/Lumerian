A symbol of the functioning of buddhi in the purifying of the qualities. This is reflected on the lower planes in the suffering and sorrow which accompany the process. ““The Lord hath taken you, and brought you forth out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be unto him a people of inheritance, as at this day.”—Dervurt. iv. 20. ‘“The Lord, whose fire is in Zion, and his furnace in Jerusalem.”’—Is. xxxi. 9. ‘And shall cast them (the wicked) into the furnace of fire: there shall be the weeping and gnashing of teeth.”— Mart. xili. 50. The qualities on the astro-mental plane of conflict (Egypt) have undergone the discipline necessary to prepare them for an influx of spirit which ghall raise them. The puriWoop FURROWS fying buddhic functioning is in the higher nature (Zion and Jerusalem). The lower qualities (the wicked) shall be subjected to the purifying discipline of the Spirit, and their lower astral conditions shall be dissolved. ‘* St. Catherine of Genoa, ‘as she unged in the divine furnace of puriying love, was united to the Object of her love, and satisfied with all he wrought in her, so she understood it to be with the souls in Purgatory? This ‘divine furnace of purifying love’ demands from the ardent soul not only a complete self-surrender and voluntary turning from all impurity, a humility of the most far-reaching kind, but also a deliberate active suffering, a self-discipline in dreadful tasks. As gold in the refiner’s fire, so ‘burning of love into a soul truly taken all vices purgeth.’ '’—EVELYN UNDERHILL, Mysticism, p. 266. See BupDHI, Ecypt, FIRE oF HELL, FrmE (destroying), Haprs, HEtL, JERUSALEM, MAN (bad), PURGATORY, TrxrH (gnashing), ZION.
