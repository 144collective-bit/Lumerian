```text
A complete series of re-incarnations in a stage or period of the soul’s evolution. These re-incarnations are transitory conditions on the lower planes. ‘* For yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights; and every living thing that I have made will I destroy from off the face of the ground.’’—GEN. vil. 4. ‘‘Yet seven days,’—a numerical period which signifies completeness of @ stage of activity. A flood of truth (rain) is to descend and envelop the lower nature. This outpouring from above is to last ‘forty days and nights,” meaning a period which corresponds to a term of the reincarnating cycle. The lower nature at this stage is to be transformed, that is, raised and purified ; hence it will no longer exist as heretofore, but pass away. See FAcE OF GROUND, Ran (deluge), RE-INCABNATION, WILDERNESS.
```
