Further, whatever doth descend from the above, begetteth ; what floweth upwards, nourisheth '’ (The Perfect Sermon).—IJbid., Vol. II. p. 310. The buddhic vibrations bear the 286 FOOD qualities upwards and are the Divine life of the soul. The lower qualities subserve the higher. Truth flowing downwards increases knowledge. Aspiration for Love and Wisdom brings food to the soul. See AMBROSIA, FIRE, Gops, HERMES, SACRIFICE, USERT.
