A symbol indicative of what is shown, manifested, demonstrated or done. The forefinger of Horus, which he places upon his mouth, signifies the expression, or manifestation, of the indwelling Self in the soul ; the evolution of the Self from small to great. ‘*‘ But if I by the finger of God cast out devils, then is the kingdom of God come upon you.”—LUKE xi. 20.
