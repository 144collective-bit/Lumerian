A symbol of the physical foundation 270 FETTERS of the lower activities which are necessary to advance the evolution ofthe soul. Activity or Will exercised on the lower planes. “*I thought on my ways, and turned rng feet unto thy testimonies.”—Ps. cxix. The soul meditates on its condition and regulates the lower activities, so that it may advance towards the ideals set before it. “By standing upon their feet is signified the external of man. The external man is in iteelf natural.’’°— SwEDENBORG, Apoc. Rev., n. 510. See Foot, Foorrrinrs, Ricut roor, SANDALs, Sky, STRIDES OF SOUL, WaLKING, WASHED FEET.
