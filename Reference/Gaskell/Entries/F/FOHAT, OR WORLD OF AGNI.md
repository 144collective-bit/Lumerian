A symbol of the force-aspect of the Logos, operative only on the mental plane of the cosmos or in the causalbody. **Evolution is commenced by the intellectual energy of the Logos, ... not merely on account of the potentialities locked up in Mulaprakriti. This Light of the Logos is the link between objective matter and the subjective thought of Ishvara (or Logos). It is called in several Buddhist books Fohat. It is the one instrument with which the Logos works.”—T. Sussa Row, quoted in Secret Doctrine, Vol. I. p. 161. See LIGHTNING, POLYPHEMUS.
