This fig-tree stands for the lower mature which can never of itself bring forth the fruits of the Spirit. The lower nature withers away at the word of the perfected Christsoul. The emblem here is taken as the lower part, or obverse, of the Tree of Life.—See Mar. xxi. 19-21. See Man (natural), Worp.
