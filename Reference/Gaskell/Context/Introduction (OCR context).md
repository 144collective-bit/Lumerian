# Introduction (OCR context)

Context note for Gaskell’s **Introduction** across EPUB `page_16`–`page_22`.

## Editorial summary of Gaskell’s own framework (as OCR presents it)

Gaskell’s introduction (OCR) argues that popular religious systems are unstable; that neither religionists nor rationalist scientists have solved the nature of scriptures; that myth-making theories fail to explain sacred speech; that scriptures resemble inspired/ecstatic utterance more than cunning invention; and that a sacred symbolic language can be learned by open-minded study. On [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_22]] he lists symbols he believes sometimes carry dual meanings, and names selected higher-mind figures across systems. Those lists are **Gaskell’s own comparative statements**—not vault-established equivalences or influence claims.
