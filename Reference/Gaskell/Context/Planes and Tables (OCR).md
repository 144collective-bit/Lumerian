# Planes and Tables (OCR)

Gaskell’s “Five Planes” comparative tables and related diagrams, as found in this OCR EPUB.

## Important limit

> [!warning] Table reconstruction withheld
> EPUB `page_13` and `page_14` are clearly tabular in intent, but OCR line-ordering is **not clear enough** to rebuild a faithful cell grid without inventing cells. This note preserves OCR via page links and offers only a cautious editorial reading of what Gaskell claims the tables are doing.

## Editorial reading (supported by OCR wording)

On [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_13]], Gaskell states that the tables indicate Five Planes of Existence via symbols in many religious systems; correspondences run horizontally; and he asserts religions are essentially the same in referring to the same facts of being—**author statement in the book**, not vault doctrine.

On [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_14]], he maps names from several systems onto five planes and states that bracketed names symbolize the Absolute / God-unmanifest while the highest plane indicates God-manifest / Higher Self.

On [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_23]], a five-plane schema appears as OCR text.

On [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_849]], a “Life-cycle of twelve stages” / zodiac-style diagram appears as OCR text after the dictionary body.
