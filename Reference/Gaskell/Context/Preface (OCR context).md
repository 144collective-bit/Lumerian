## Editorial summary

In the preface OCR, Gaskell states that the dictionary claims to solve the origin/nature/meaning problem of world scriptures and myths; appeals to readers dissatisfied with conflicting Christian and rationalist expositions; argues scriptures belong to a higher symbolic plane of thought; and frames the work as revealing a universal metaphorical sacred language (associated in the preface with Max Müller). He distinguishes grotesque surface wording from symbolism he treats as the precious content.
