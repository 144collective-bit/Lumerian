# How to read this source

1. Open a Text note. Each subsection is marked with a stable block `^bullinger-wots-pgepubidNNNN` matching the EPUB NCX fragment, and page markers such as `[pg 024]` are kept from the transcription.
2. Treat star-name “meanings” (for example *Bethulah*, *Mozanaim*, *Kesith*, *Sirius* as “Prince”) as **Bullinger’s etymological claims**, often resting on a Hebrew or Arabic root he supplies. A working Bible link only shows that the verse he quoted exists in the installed KJV.
3. Treat Denderah, Esneh, Akkadian month-names, Albumazar, Aratus, and “Creation Tablet” material as **sources Bullinger cites or paraphrases**, not as vault-verified archaeology.
4. Greek and Latin “fables” are, in his frame, *ignorant perversions* of a primitive revelation. That judgment is his, not an editorial conclusion of this vault.
5. Compare neighbouring sources only through the evidence notes. Gaskell’s zodiacal-sign entries and Hall’s zodiac chapter occupy the same subject-field; they do not cite this book, and this book does not cite them.
