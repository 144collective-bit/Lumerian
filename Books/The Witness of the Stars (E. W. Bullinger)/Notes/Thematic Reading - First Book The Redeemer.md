# Thematic reading — First Book (The Redeemer)

Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/04 The First Book The Redeemer|book intro]] through [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/08 Chapter IV The Sign SAGITTARIUS|SAGITTARIUS]]. Block IDs `^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00005` (book intro) through `00020` (Draco, third decan of Sagittarius). VIRGO itself is `pgepubid00006`.

## VIRGO (`pgepubid00006`)

Bullinger opens the story with a virgin who bears the promised Seed. He gives the Hebrew name as *Bethulah* and emphasises a branch / ear-of-corn double image (star *Al Zimach* and *Al Mureddin / Vindemiatrix* appear on the plate). He cites [[Isaiah 007#^kjv-isa-7-14|Isaiah 7:14]] and [[Isaiah 009#^kjv-isa-9-6|Isaiah 9:6]] (and the Gospel use in [[Matthew 001#^kjv-mat-1-23|Matthew 1:23]]) as the written counterpart of the figure. He also says the Hebrew *Tsemech* ("Branch") is used of the Coming One four times and maps those uses onto the four Gospels: [[Jeremiah 023#^kjv-jer-23-5|Jeremiah 23:5]] (Matthew / King), [[Zechariah 003#^kjv-zec-3-8|Zechariah 3:8]] (Mark / Servant), [[Zechariah 006#^kjv-zec-6-12|Zechariah 6:12]] (Luke / Man), and [[Isaiah 004#^kjv-isa-4-2|Isaiah 4:2]] (John / Jehovah the Branch). That fourfold mapping is his interpretive claim. He treats a Denderah Isis identification of the figure as something "ignorantly explained." Plate 1 was visually inspected: a reclining female figure among labelled stars, with Libra toward her feet and Leo / Denebola toward her head, matching the “begin-Virgo, end-Leo” reading-order he wants.

**Coma** (`pgepubid00007`): the woman and child; he quotes Haggai 2:7 (“desire of all nations”) and recounts Albumazar’s report that the figure was associated with names he renders IHESU / CHRISTOS. That last item is a **cited tradition**, not a vault-verified manuscript reading. He treats the later "Berenice's hair" story as a perversion of the original woman-and-child figure; Egyptian *Shes-nu* he glosses "the desired son." He recounts Cooper/Bossi tracing of the Denderah planisphere, a Shakespeare allusion in *Titus Andronicus*, Balaam's star ([[Numbers 024#^kjv-num-24-17|Numbers 24:17]]), and the claim that the Star of Bethlehem was a new star in Coma. Footnote 34 records Kepler's three Jupiter-Saturn conjunctions in Pisces in the year before (a **cited tradition** / astronomical notice as he reports it, not re-computed here), together with Jewish authorities he says connected Pisces with Israel.

**Centaurus** (`pgepubid00008`): “despised sin-offering.” He equates Arabic/Chaldee *Bezeh* with [[Isaiah 053#^kjv-isa-53-3|Isaiah 53:3]] “despised,” and *Asmeath* with a sin-offering (Isa. 53:10). Cheiron’s willing death is treated as a “perversion” of the same figure.

**Boötes** (`pgepubid00009`): “the Coming One,” from Hebrew *Bo*, “to come,” with [[Psalms 096#^kjv-psa-96-13|Psalm 96:13]] and Arcturus (named in [[Job 009#^kjv-job-9-9|Job 9:9]]) glossed as “He cometh.”

## LIBRA (`pgepubid00010`)

The scales: Hebrew *Mozanaim*. The star *Zuben al Genubi* he reads as “the price deficient,” *Zuben al Chemali* as “the price which covers.” Redemption is a payment. The Appendix (`pgepubid00057`) later argues that Libra itself may be a corruption of an older circular altar / lamp (*Tul-ku*, *Bir*), seized by the Scorpion (*Gir-tab*) — a historical reconstruction he offers from Akkadian names and boundary-stone figures (figs. 1–3 extracted; not independently redrawn here).

**CRUX** (`pgepubid00011`): the Southern Cross. Plate 6 was visually inspected: four principal stars drawn as a Latin cross, labelled “VI. CRUX (the Cross),” with Centaurus indicated nearby. Bullinger connects the figure with the Hebrew letter *Tau* (and later letter-forms of that character), with "It is finished" ([[John 019#^kjv-jhn-19-30|John 19:30]]), and with verses such as [[John 005#^kjv-jhn-5-24|John 5:24]]. He glosses a name *Adom* as "cut off," citing [[Daniel 009#^kjv-dan-9-26|Daniel 9:26]]. He says that in earliest times Egypt used the cross as a sign and symbol of life (an editorial resemblance, not identity, with Gaskell's later `CROSS, ANKH (CRUX ANSATA), A SYMBOL OF LIFE` entry). He also attacks later Roman Good Friday cakes as a corruption. He claims the cross was formerly visible in the latitude of Jerusalem and has since sunk with precession - that visibility history is his astronomical claim.

**Lupus / Victima** (`pgepubid00012`): the victim slain, even “by the Centaur, i.e. by Himself,” citing John 10:15–18 (self-laid-down life).

**Corona** (`pgepubid00013`): the crown immediately after the cross; Philippians 2 / Hebrews 2:9 / Revelation 5:9 in his application.

## SCORPIO (`pgepubid00014`)

The scorpion seeks to wound but is trodden. Name *Akrab*; Antares as the wounding one. He reads [[Psalms 091#^kjv-psa-91-13|Psalm 91:13]] (“tread upon the lion and adder”) as written commentary.

**Serpens and Ophiuchus** (`pgepubid00015`): a man in conflict with a serpent that reaches for the crown — dominion contested. This is one of the strongest supported joins to the vault’s [[Symbols & Concepts/Serpent|Serpent]] hub, because the book’s entire program is the Genesis 3:15 contest, pictured rather than allegorised as inner wisdom.

**Hercules** (`pgepubid00016`): kneeling, right heel lifted “as if wounded,” left foot on the dragon’s head; star-names *Ras al Gethi* “head of him who bruises,” *Ma'asyn* “sin-offering.” Denderah *Bau*, "who cometh." He says this Mighty Man shall destroy the Old Serpent and "open the way again, not to fabled 'apples of gold,' but to the 'tree of life' itself" - a source-specific mention of the biblical tree of life, set against the Hesperides story. Footnote 44 notes that Bayer placed an apple branch in Hercules' hand as an emblem of those apples. This is the passage now linked from [[Symbols & Concepts/Tree of Life|Tree of Life]]; it is not a sephiroth teaching and not a Golden-Legend cross-wood story.

## SAGITTARIUS (`pgepubid00017`)

The two-natured conqueror “conquering and to conquer,” aligned by Bullinger with Psalm 45:3–5 and [[Revelation 006#^kjv-rev-6-2|Revelation 6:2]]. Hebrew/Syriac *Kesith*, the Archer.

**Lyra** (`pgepubid00018`): praise prepared (Psalm 65:1; Revelation 11:17; 19:7).

**Ara** (`pgepubid00019`): an altar inverted, fires pointing downward to Tartarus - judgment (curse and everlasting fire; he applies [[Psalms 021#^kjv-psa-21-9|Psalm 21:9]]), not a temple-altar of worship. That judgment-fire picture is a supported neighbour, by contrast, of [[Symbols & Concepts/Fire|Fire]] (Gaskell's interior or purificatory fire and Hall's elemental fire are different frames, not direct contradictions of a shared proposition). Denderah again *Bau*, with Isaiah 63:1 ("who is this that cometh from Edom").

**Draco** (`pgepubid00020`): "that old serpent, called the Devil" as in [[Revelation 012#^kjv-rev-12-9|Revelation 12:9]]. He notes that Thuban (in Draco) was the pole star about 4,620 years ago - an astronomical claim he uses to argue the system's antiquity. Together with Cetus and Hydra he forms a book-end pattern of the enemy in each of the three "books."
