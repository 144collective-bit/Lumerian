# Thematic reading — Second Book (The Redeemed)

Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/09 The Second Book The Redeemed|book intro]] through [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/13 Chapter IV The Sign ARIES|ARIES]].

## CAPRICORNUS (`pgepubid00022`)

A dying goat with a fish’s tail: atonement slain, yet life for the redeemed. He records *Hu-penius*, "the place of the sacrifice." He pairs the two goats of [[Leviticus 016#^kjv-lev-16-22|Leviticus 16]] (one slain, one living) as a death-and-resurrection picture, and cites [[John 012#^kjv-jhn-12-24|John 12:24]] (the corn of wheat that dies and brings forth fruit). He also claims that when the Promised Seed was born the sun was actually in Capricornus, so that the sign pointed out when the "Sun of Righteousness" should arise and "the Light of the World" appear ([[Galatians 004#^kjv-gal-4-4|Galatians 4:4]]; days, he says, began to lengthen when "the true light" had come). Those solar-position claims are his astronomy. This is his reading of a composite figure, not a zoological description.

**Sagitta** (`pgepubid00023`): the arrow already in flight, “of God,” applied to the stricken Messiah (Psalm 38:2; Isaiah 53:4–5; Job 6:4) — distinguished from Sagittarius’s as-yet-unshot arrows against enemies.

**Aquila** (`pgepubid00024`): the falling, pierced eagle; *Al Okal* glossed “wounded in the heel.”

**Delphinus** (`pgepubid00025`): the fish leaping upward — “the dead One rising again.” He cites Romans 4:25 and firstfruits language. This is a supported neighbour of [[Symbols & Concepts/Death, Resurrection, and Initiation|Death, Resurrection, and Initiation]], with the limit that Bullinger is narrating Christ’s death and resurrection for a people, not an initiatory interior drama.

## AQUARIUS (`pgepubid00026`)

The water-bearer pouring living waters. Hebrew *Deli*; he uses [[Numbers 024#^kjv-num-24-7|Numbers 24:7]] among other water-blessing texts, and applies [[Isaiah 044#^kjv-isa-44-3|Isaiah 44:3]] and Ezekiel 36 to Spirit poured on Israel. He expressly refuses to read Pentecost or the Church into the sign: the Church, he says, is nowhere seen in these signs. The waters are, in his frame, both literal blessing and Spirit given to Israel. Supported neighbour of [[Symbols & Concepts/Water|Water]], **not** of Gaskell's "higher water = Truth / lower water = illusion," which is a different interpretive frame rather than a proposition-for-proposition clash.

**Piscis Australis**, **Pegasus**, **Cygnus** (`pgepubid00027`–`00029`): blessings received, brought near (*Markab*, “returning from afar”), and the Blesser himself returning (*Tes-ark*, “this from afar”; *Deneb* as “the judge” in his glossary).

## PISCES (`pgepubid00030`)

The fishes: multitudes (*Dagim*), blessed yet bound. He argues that the Church is a "mystery" hidden from the Old Testament, so this sign must not be read as the Church; it refers to Israel's seed. The two fishes are, in his reading, Israel's heavenly calling versus Israel's earthly portion.

**The Band** (`pgepubid00031`): binds the fishes and, in the picture he describes, also binds Cetus. Hosea 11:4 (bands of love) is applied.

**Andromeda** (`pgepubid00032`): the chained woman as the captive daughter of Zion - Israel in affliction (*Al Phiratz* "broken down," *Mirach* "weak"), not a generic picture of "the redeemed" and not the Church. Neighbour, by subject only, of Gaskell's `ANDROMEDA, DAUGHTER OF CEPHEUS AND CASSIOPEA` - Gaskell's psychology is not Bullinger's Israel-in-bondage reading. Editorial comparison (differing frames), not equivalence.

**Cepheus** (`pgepubid00033`): the crowned king of Israel, foot on the polar star; Denderah *Pe-ku-hor*, "this one cometh to rule." He derives the Greek name from Hebrew "the branch."

## ARIES (`pgepubid00034`)

The ram or lamb, living, as against the dying goat of Capricornus - victory prepared after sacrifice. He claims that at the Crucifixion, on the 14th of Nisan, the sun stood at the very spot marked by *El Nath* and *Al Sheratan*. That dating and solar position are his astronomical-historical claims.



**Cassiopeia** (`pgepubid00035`): the same woman now freed and enthroned (Arabic *El Seder*, "the freed"; *Ruchba*, "the enthroned"). He identifies her with restored Israel as the Bride of the Lamb, and distinguishes that from the Church as Christ's Body / "one new man" (he cites [[Ephesians 002#^kjv-eph-2-15|Ephesians 2:15]] and Ephesians 5:25). Footnote 57 refuses to read the Queen of Psalm 45 as the Church-Bride, lest prophecies that "belong to Israel" be given to the Church. The W-shape diagram is extracted (image-unreviewed beyond the extracted diagram file).

**Cetus** (`pgepubid00036`): the sea-monster bound, applied to [[Revelation 020#^kjv-rev-20-2|Revelation 20:1-3]] (the dragon bound a thousand years) and also read as Leviathan (he uses Job 41, Isaiah 27:1, and Psalm 74). Denderah *Knem*, "subdued."

**Perseus** (`pgepubid00037`): *Peretz*, “the Breaker,” applied to Micah 2:13; he carries the severed head of the enemy (Medusa in the later myth, which he treats as a corrupted remnant of the same idea).
