# Thematic reading — Third Book and closing

Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/14 The Third Book The Redeemer|book intro]] through [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/23 Project Gutenberg License|license]].

## TAURUS (`pgepubid00039`)

The bull as Messiah coming to rule / judge. He excludes the Church from the judgment pictured here: the Church, he says, will be caught up to meet the Lord and will be with Him, safe from that judgment. He notes the Denderah pairing with Isis/Apis and says Egypt retained the figure while forgetting the meaning - a polemic, not a demonstrated history of transmission.

**Orion** (`pgepubid00040`): “coming forth as light.” He derives *Oarion* from a Hebrew root “light,” and Akkadian *Ur-ana* as “light of heaven”; Denderah *Ha-ga-t*, “he who triumphs.” This is a source-specific join to [[Symbols & Concepts/Light|Light]], with the etymology remaining his claim. He also calls the coming figure "the Light of the world," citing [[Isaiah 060#^kjv-isa-60-1|Isaiah 60:1-3]] beside [[Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-1|Psalm 19:1]]. He stresses that the coming one is pictured as a glorious man, not "a mere animal."

**Eridanus** (`pgepubid00041`): river issuing from Orion's foot, "river of the Judge" / "River of Wrath," not (for him) the Po or the Euphrates; *Achernar*, "the after part of the river." He applies Daniel's "fiery stream" ([[Daniel 007#^kjv-dan-7-10|Daniel 7:9-11]]), and also Psalm 97:3 and Isaiah 30:27-33 and 66:15, and says the picture speaks of final judgment and the lake of fire. Together with Ara, this is the book's developed judgment-fire thesis (see [[Symbols & Concepts/Fire|Fire]], labelled as a differing frame from Gaskell's interior/purificatory fire, not as a direct contradiction).

**Auriga** (`pgepubid00042`): the shepherd who gathers lambs, applied to Isaiah 40:10-11 - safety for the redeemed in the day of wrath. He quotes [[Malachi 004#^kjv-mal-4-2|Malachi 4:1-3]] ("Sun of Righteousness") as the connected teaching of Eridanus and Auriga. He notes that the Denderah figure's sceptre ends in a cross, repeating that with the Egyptians the cross was a sign of life.

## GEMINI (`pgepubid00043`)

He rejects a simple Castor-and-Pollux inventiveness. Coptic *Pi-Mahi* and Hebrew *Thaumim*, “united”; the pair shows, in his reading, twofold nature (God and man), twofold work (suffering and glory), and twofold coming. Acts 28:11 (the ship *Dioskouroi*) is mentioned as a later cultural remnant, not as proof of the thesis.

**Lepus** (`pgepubid00044`): modern “hare,” but in Denderah an unclean bird on a serpent under Orion’s feet (*Bashti-beki*, “confounded / failing”) — the enemy trodden. With Orion he applies [[Malachi 004#^kjv-mal-4-2|Malachi 4:2]] ("the Sun of Righteousness shall arise") to the coming one who treads down the wicked.

**Canis Major / Sirius** (`pgepubid00045`): Sirius as *Sar*, “Prince,” applied to [[Isaiah 009#^kjv-isa-9-6|Isaiah 9:6]]. Hawk vs serpent in Denderah.

**Canis Minor / Procyon**: the exalted Redeemer; star-name glosses follow the same method as Sirius.

## CANCER (`pgepubid00047`)

He says the crab is *not* the original picture. Denderah shows a scarab; the name *Klaria* he reads as "cattle-folds," Arabic *Al Sartan* "who holds or binds." Footnote 69 records the scarabaeus as an Egyptian emblem of the resurrection of the body (worm of the earth issuing as a winged creature) - a cited Egyptian emblem, used to support his substitution of the scarab for the crab, not an independent Egyptological finding. The theme becomes possessions held fast, not an astrological crab-nature.

**Ursa Minor / Major** (`pgepubid00048`–`00049`): he rejects “bears” (no such tail, no bear in the older planispheres he names) and reads *Dubheh* / *Dōhver* as fold or herd — lesser and greater sheepfolds.

**Argo** (`pgepubid00050`): the ship of homecoming. He allows that some connect the story with Noah’s ark, then reads Jason, the recovered treasure, and the serpent-guardian through his Gospel grid. This is **not** treated here as a Tree of Life text; see the review queue for the rejected lexical join.

## LEO (`pgepubid00051`)

The Lion of the tribe of Judah; reading-order closes where it began (woman’s seed to the lion). *Pi Mentekeon*, “the pouring out,” he applies as wrath poured on the serpent, not as blessing (contrast Aquarius).

**Hydra** (`pgepubid00052`): *Al Phard*, "the separated" / put away - the old serpent destroyed. He calls Hydra the "female serpent" and "abhorred."

**Crater** (`pgepubid00053`): the cup of wrath, with Psalm 75:8.

**Corvus** (`pgepubid00054`): birds of prey devouring the enemy; nine stars as “the number of judgment” (his numerology).

## Summary (`pgepubid00055`)

He contrasts Jerome’s *ipse* (he) with a later *ipsa* (she) on Genesis 3:15, and argues that Roman images rest Mary’s foot *on* the serpent rather than crushing it. The star-pictures, he says, never give the woman the victor’s role: Ophiuchus, Orion/Lepus, Leo/Hydra, Hercules/Draco, and Cepheus do. Four women appear: Virgo and Coma with the Redeemer; Andromeda and Cassiopeia with the Redeemed. He then maps Philippians 2:5–11 as a double seven (humiliation / exaltation) onto that promise.

That anti-Marian polemic is Bullinger’s confessional argument. The vault records it as his claim and does not take it as a historical finding about Jerome’s manuscripts without other evidence.

## “For Signs And For Seasons” (`pgepubid00056`)

Opening: [Genesis 1:14](<Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001.md#^kjv-gen-1-14>) - *othoth* as signs of someone/something to come; *moed* as appointed time, illustrated from Genesis 17:21, 18:14, and 21:2 (each tied by him to the promised Seed). He then develops solar-sidereal difference, the ~25,800-year precessional cycle, the Saros, and a prophetic arithmetic that produces 594, 666, and 1,260 from 18 × 33 / 37 / 70 (594 + 666 = 1,260). He holds both a short literal fulfilment in days and a long one in years, and distinguishes his futurist reading from historicist readings of the same numbers.

Fixed dates he uses: 625 BC (beginning of Babylon under Nebuchadnezzar), 31 BC (Actium), and AD 636-7 (Omar). From those he draws eclipse-cycle spans and a total of 2,520 years. He names AD 1896-7 as marking the end of the "Times of the Gentiles" ([[Luke 021#^kjv-luk-21-24|Luke 21:24]] is the biblical phrase in view) while saying he is not "fixing dates" in the ordinary sense. The Church, he says, is unaffected and waits for 1 Thessalonians 4. Footnotes 77-87 carry supporting notices: Vallancey on Saros gematria (666), Maunder's eclipse data, and lunar / zodiacal / solar reckonings he associates with 587 BC / AD 1860, 608 BC / AD 1879, and 624 BC / AD 1896-7.

**The eclipse narrative, the gematria, and the projected terminus are his interpretive astronomy and chronology**, even where the Saros as an eclipse cycle is a real astronomical period. The named 1896-7 date is now past; this vault records the claim and does not evaluate whether it was fulfilled.

## Appendix on LIBRA (`pgepubid00057`)

Historical reconstruction of Libra from altar/lamp to scales via Scorpio’s claws, with three extracted figures. Source-specific; not used to rewrite the Libra chapter’s redemption-price reading, which he had already given as the *present* sign’s meaning.

## Footnotes and license (`pgepubid00058`–`00059`)

Footnotes 1-95 (about 4,325 words) were read in full. They include lexical supports for the Psalm 19 sun/law parallel (notes 1-2 on "structure" and the sun-verbs; note 10 on *chammah* as a name for the sun), source citations (Boys on Psalm structure, astronomical authorities), the Coma/Kepler notice (note 34), Hevelius and Halley's modern constellations used to argue that the ancient figures were designed (note 37), Ophiuchus as Aesculapius and the serpent as a medical symbol, which he calls a perversion of the Coming One as healer (note 43), Bayer's Hesperides apple-branch (note 44), Cassiopeia's Queen not to be read as the Church-Bride (note 57), the scarabaeus as an Egyptian resurrection emblem (note 69), and the Seasons chronological notes (77-87). Callouts 1-95 each have exactly one source pointer; see [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Evidence/Reports/Footnote Callout Map|Footnote callout map]]. The Gutenberg license was preserved and read for attribution, not treated as part of Bullinger's argument.
