## How to read this source

Bullinger’s thesis is that the twelve signs and their accompanying constellations were a primitive, divine picture-revelation of [[Genesis 003#^kjv-gen-3-15|Genesis 3:15]] — the promised Seed who would be bruised in the heel and would bruise the serpent’s head — later written “in the Volume of the Book.” He reads Hebrew, Arabic, Coptic, Akkadian, and Egyptian *names* of stars and figures as a Christian narrative in three “books”:

1. The Redeemer (first coming / sufferings) — Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius.
2. The Redeemed (result of those sufferings) — Capricornus, Aquarius, Pisces, Aries.
3. The Redeemer (second coming / glory) — Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo.

**Those etymologies, dates of antiquity, and the claim that the figures themselves preach the Gospel are Bullinger’s claims.** They are not independently verified astronomical or historical facts in this vault. Separate:

- *source claim* — what Bullinger asserts (a star-name meaning, a Denderah reading, a date);
- *KJV wording* — the installed verse he cites, which can be opened and checked;
- *other authors* — Gaskell’s zodiac entries, Hall’s zodiac chapter, Albumazar as Bullinger quotes him;
- *editorial comparison* — a researcher juxtaposition, not influence and not identity.

Do not treat a working verse link as confirmation of an etymology.
