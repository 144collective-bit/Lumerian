## The First Book. The Redeemer.

^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00005

The First Book. The
        Redeemer.

(His First
        Coming.)

“The Sufferings of Christ.”

The First Book is
        occupied with the PERSON of the Coming One. It covers the whole
        ground, and includes the conflict and the victory of the Promised
        Seed, but with special emphasis on His Coming. The book opens with
        the promise of His coming, and it closes with the Dragon cast down
        from heaven.
