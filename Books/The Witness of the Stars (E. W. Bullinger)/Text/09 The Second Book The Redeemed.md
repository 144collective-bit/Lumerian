## The Second Book. The Redeemed.

^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00021

The Second Book. The
        Redeemed.

The
        Result of the Redeemer's Sufferings.

In the First
        Book we have had before us the work of the Redeemer set forth as it
        concerned His own glorious person. In this Second
        Book it is presented to us as it affects others. Here we see the
        results of His humiliation, and
        conflict, and victory—“The sufferings of
        Christ” and the blessings they procured for His redeemed
        people.

In Chapter I. we
        have the Blessings procured.

In Chapter II.
        their Blessings ensured.

In Chapter III.
        their Blessings in abeyance.

In Chapter IV.
        their Blessings enjoyed.
