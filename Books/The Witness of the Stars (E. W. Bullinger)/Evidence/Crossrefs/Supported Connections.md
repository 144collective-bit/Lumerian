# Supported connections

Each row names a source passage, an existing vault target, why the join is more than a shared word, and an evidence tier. Tiers used here:

- `explicit citation` — Bullinger quotes or clearly points to a verse that exists as an installed KJV block.
- `source-specific interpretation` — his Christian-celestial reading of a figure or name.
- `editorial comparison` / `editorial resemblance` — a researcher juxtaposition with Hall or Gaskell; not citation and not influence.
- `contrast` - the same symbol is used to different ends (a differing interpretive frame, not automatically a proposition-for-proposition contradiction).

No historical influence between Bullinger, Gaskell, and Hall is inferred. Verse links confirm wording, not etymology.

## Relations written into hubs

### 1. Christ

- **Source:** VIRGO and the First Book program — promised Seed; [[Isaiah 007#^kjv-isa-7-14|Isaiah 7:14]], [[Isaiah 009#^kjv-isa-9-6|Isaiah 9:6]], [[Matthew 001#^kjv-mat-1-23|Matthew 1:23]]; Coma / Albumazar as a reported tradition (`^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00006`, `00007`).
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Christ|Christ]] (existing Gaskell/Hall/KJV discussion of Christ / Christos).
- **Why:** He identifies the celestial child and the coming Prince with Jesus the Messiah, not with a generic solar hero. That is the book’s explicit subject.
- **Tier:** `explicit citation` for the verses; `source-specific interpretation` for the figures; Albumazar remains `cited tradition`.
- **Limit:** Does not make Gaskell’s interior Christ-principle the same object.

### 2. Cross and Crucifixion

- **Source:** CRUX (`^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00011`); Plate 6 visually inspected; “It is finished”; [[John 005#^kjv-jhn-5-24|John 5:24]]; [[John 019#^kjv-jhn-19-30|John 19:30]] ("It is finished"); *Adom* as "cut off" ([[Daniel 009#^kjv-dan-9-26|Daniel 9:26]]); Corona immediately after (`00013`). Egypt, he says, used the cross as a sign of life; Auriga's Denderah sceptre ends in a cross (`00042`).
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Cross and Crucifixion|Cross and Crucifixion]].
- **Why:** He treats a named southern constellation as a picture of the historical cross of Christ, then a crown. That is a concrete figure-to-passion join, not the word “cross” appearing at random.
- **Tier:** `source-specific interpretation` (plus visual confirmation that the plate is drawn as a cross). Differing frames (`contrast` / `editorial comparison`) relative to Gaskell's quaternary / Logos-ray cross and Hall's pagan-antecedent / LVX discussion. An `editorial resemblance` (not identity) also exists with Gaskell's `CROSS, ANKH (CRUX ANSATA), A SYMBOL OF LIFE`, because both associate an Egyptian cross-form with life; Bullinger is speaking of the historical passion and a constellation, Gaskell of an interior symbol.
- **Limit:** Visibility-at-Jerusalem and *Tau* claims stay his.

### 3. Serpent

- **Source:** Genesis 3:15 as the book’s plot ([[Genesis 003#^kjv-gen-3-15|Genesis 3:15]]); Serpens & Ophiuchus (`00015`); Hercules’ heel and foot (`00016`); Draco (`00020`) with [[Revelation 012#^kjv-rev-12-9|Revelation 12:9]] (Thuban as former pole star, about 4,620 years ago, is his dating); Hydra (`00052`, the "female serpent"); Cetus bound (`00036`, also Leviathan); Summary's refusal of a woman's foot as the crusher. Footnote 43: the Greek fable that names Ophiuchus Aesculapius, with the serpent as the symbol of medicine, which he calls a perversion of the Coming One as healer.
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Serpent|Serpent]].
- **Why:** Multiple constellations are read as one enemy (the Old Serpent) in conflict with the Redeemer — the same promise, restated. That is structural, not a keyword hit on “serpent.”
- **Tier:** `explicit citation` of Gen 3:15 and Rev 12:9 as he uses them; `source-specific interpretation` of the figures; `contrast` (differing frames) with Gaskell's multi-meaning serpent and Hall's initiatory serpent. `editorial comparison` with Gaskell's `SERPENT ASSCULAPIUS` entry (Higher Self as divine healer via buddhic forces) - same classical name, different object.
- **Limit:** Do not flatten those other entries into “serpent = Satan.”

### 4. Sun

- **Source:** Preface/Introduction use of Psalm 19 - the sun's going forth and the heavens that declare ([[Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-1|Psalm 19:1]], [[Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-7|Psalm 19:7]]; footnotes 2 and 10 on the Psalm's sun-verbs and *chammah* as a name for the sun); Seasons on [Genesis 1:14](<Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001.md#^kjv-gen-1-14>). Capricornus (`00022`): the sun, he says, stood in that sign at Christ's birth, when the "Sun of Righteousness" and "true light" appeared. Aries (`00034`): the sun stood at *El Nath* and *Al Sheratan* on 14 Nisan in the year of the Crucifixion. Auriga and Lepus quote [[Malachi 004#^kjv-mal-4-2|Malachi 4:2]].
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Sun|Sun]], which already holds Gaskell’s “Sun of righteousness” and Hall’s solar-cult material.
- **Why:** He treats the sun as a *created time-keeper and a speaker in a paired revelation*, not as a deity to be worshipped (he rejects heathen dismay at heavenly signs).
- **Tier:** `explicit citation` of Ps 19, Gen 1:14, and Malachi 4:2 (a shared citation with Gaskell's "SUN OF RIGHTEOUSNESS" entry and the existing Sun-hub Malachi bridge - shared verse, not shared meaning). The Capricornus/Aries solar positions are `source-specific interpretation`. `contrast` (differing frames) with Hall's solar-cult reduction and with Gaskell's interior "central source of Light and Life."
- **Limit:** Not a claim that Bullinger taught Gaskell’s Higher Self.

### 5. Light

- **Source:** Orion, "coming forth as light" / *Ur-ana* "light of heaven" (`^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00040`); he also names the figure "the Light of the world" and cites [[Isaiah 060#^kjv-isa-60-1|Isaiah 60:1-3]] with [[Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-1|Psalm 19:1]]. Psalm 19 "enlightening" language is developed in footnote 2.
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Light|Light]].
- **Why:** He assigns light to the *person* of the coming Redeemer in Orion, not only to created brightness.
- **Tier:** `source-specific interpretation` (etymology unverified). `editorial resemblance` only at the broad level that Light, Sun, and Christ already neighbour one another in this vault.
- **Limit:** Do not “confirm” *Ur-ana* because the Light hub exists.

### 6. Logos and the Word

- **Source:** Introduction close: the star-revelation “coinciding … with that afterwards recorded in the Volume of the Book”; Psalm 19’s A/B (heavens’ speech / the law). Summary: stars and written Word filled with one subject.
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Logos and the Word|Logos and the Word]].
- **Why:** He argues two coordinated revelations (pictured heavens and written Scripture), not a metaphysical Logos emanating through a quaternary.
- **Tier:** `source-specific interpretation` of Ps 19’s structure; `explicit citation` of the Psalm; `contrast` with Gaskell’s Logos-as-Higher-Self and Hall’s “Second Logos or Christ.”
- **Limit:** Neighbourhood is not identity. He rarely uses the word “Logos.”

### 7. Water

- **Source:** AQUARIUS pouring living waters (`^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00026`). He applies [[Isaiah 044#^kjv-isa-44-3|Isaiah 44:3]] and Ezekiel 36, and refuses to read Pentecost or the Church into the sign: the waters are for Israel.
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Water|Water]].
- **Why:** The sign's whole chapter-thesis is water as blessing bestowed (on Israel, in his dispensational frame), not a passing mention of a river.
- **Tier:** `source-specific interpretation`. `contrast` with Gaskell’s higher/lower water (Truth vs illusion) and with Eridanus, which he reads as a *river of wrath* (do not fold Eridanus into this Water join).
- **Limit:** Not a baptism-rite claim (Baptism hub left untouched).

### 8. Death, Resurrection, and Initiation

- **Source:** Capricornus slain + Delphinus rising (`00022`, `00025`), read through the two goats of [[Leviticus 016#^kjv-lev-16-22|Leviticus 16]] and [[John 012#^kjv-jhn-12-24|John 12:24]]; Lupus as self-laid-down life; Aries as the living lamb after the dying goat. Footnote 69: the scarabaeus as an Egyptian emblem of resurrection of the body (Cancer at Denderah).
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Death, Resurrection, and Initiation|Death, Resurrection, and Initiation]].
- **Why:** He narrates death and resurrection as historical-redemptive events pictured in successive figures.
- **Tier:** `source-specific interpretation`. `contrast` with Hall’s interior initiatory death (already labelled separately on that hub).
- **Limit:** Do not read Delphinus as a mystery-school raising.

## 9. Tree of Life

- **Source:** Hercules (`^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00016`): the Mighty Man shall destroy the Old Serpent and "open the way again, not to fabled 'apples of gold,' but to the 'tree of life' itself." Footnote 44: Bayer's apple branch as an emblem of the Hesperides apples.
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Tree of Life|Tree of Life]].
- **Why:** He names the biblical tree of life explicitly and contrasts it with the Greek golden apples. That is more than a shared word on a different story (Argo's fleece remains in the review queue).
- **Tier:** `source-specific interpretation`. Thin but explicit. Not a sephiroth teaching and not the Golden Legend cross-wood story already distinguished on that hub.
- **Limit:** One sentence plus a footnote about a later artist's emblem. Do not enlarge it into a Bullinger doctrine of the tree.

## 10. Fire

- **Source:** Ara (`^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00019`), an inverted altar whose fires point downward to Tartarus (curse and everlasting fire; [[Psalms 021#^kjv-psa-21-9|Psalm 21:9]]); Eridanus (`00041`), the "River of the Judge" / "River of Wrath," with Daniel's fiery stream ([[Daniel 007#^kjv-dan-7-10|Daniel 7:9-11]]), Psalm 97:3, and Isaiah 30:27-33 and 66:15, speaking of the lake of fire.
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Fire|Fire]].
- **Why:** Two developed judgment-fire pictures, not a passing use of the word "fire."
- **Tier:** `source-specific interpretation` and `explicit citation` of those judgment texts; `contrast` (differing interpretive frames) with Gaskell's higher fire as divine love / purification and with Hall's elemental fire-beings. Gaskell's `FIRE, DESTROYING` (a consuming fire that dispels ignorance) is a neighbouring lemma, not the same object.
- **Limit:** Do not merge Ara or Eridanus into Aquarius's blessing-water, or into baptismal fire.
