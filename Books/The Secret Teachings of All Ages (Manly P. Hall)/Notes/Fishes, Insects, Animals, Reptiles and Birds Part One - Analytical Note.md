## Thesis / through-line

Hall surveys living creatures as sacred emblems: fish/avatars and dolphin-Christ; scarab as Egyptian regeneration; insects/scorpion talismans; then a sustained serpent argument that spinal fire / brazen serpent / uraeus overturn a purely evil reading of the snake.
