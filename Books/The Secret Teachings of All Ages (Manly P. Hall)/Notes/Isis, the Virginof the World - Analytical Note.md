# Isis, the Virgin of the World — analytical note (deep pass)

**Not an endorsement** of Hall’s comparative religion. Full unit [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Isis, the Virginof the World.md|Text]] (PDF **120–132**). Title orthography follows vault filename (`Virginof`).

## Thesis / through-line

Hall reads **Isis** as the veiled receptive Nature / Wisdom figure of Hermetic symbolism: attributes (sistrum, boat/asp, starry robe) from classical description (Apuleius/Plutarch stream), the **Osiris dismemberment** cycle as dying-god allegory, and Christian/Madonna parallels as later echoes. A direct **John iii. 3** citation appears inside an alchemical-colour gloss on Isis’ robe.

## Argument map (source-linked)

- **PDF 120** (^hall-stota-pdf-120): Declares Hermetic symbolism should begin with Saitic Isis.
- **PDF 121** (^hall-stota-pdf-121): Plate *ISIS, QUEEN OF HEAVEN*; Apuleius-style aretalogy (“call me by my true name, Queen Isis”); Le Plongeon Maya “Queen Moo” historicising aside.
- **PDF 122** (^hall-stota-pdf-122): Isis as receptive Nature / Song of Solomon “dark maid” analogy—Hall’s solar-masculine / lunar-feminine schema.
- **PDF 123** (^hall-stota-pdf-123): Osiris murder narrative; Isis’ mourning and search (children as witnesses).
- **PDF 124** (^hall-stota-pdf-124): Veiled Isis = secret doctrine disclosed only to initiates.
- **PDF 125** (^hall-stota-pdf-125): Plate *THE SISTRUM* + long Plutarch quotation on motion vs Typhonic corruption; then Hall: black→new life, quoting **“Except a man be born again…” (John iii. 3.)** amid alchemical colour triad.
- **PDF 126–127** (^hall-stota-pdf-126, ^hall-stota-pdf-127): Isis as name of an “occult medicine”; grapes/wheat from breasts = Nature as nutrition.
- **PDF 128–131** (^hall-stota-pdf-128 ff.): *THE EGYPTIAN MADONNA*; *OSIRIS, KING OF THE UNDERWORLD*; mummification section head—iconographic Christian/Egyptian parallels.
