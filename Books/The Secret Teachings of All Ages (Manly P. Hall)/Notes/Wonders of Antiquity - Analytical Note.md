**Not an endorsement** of Hall’s claims. **Review status:** source-read analytical draft (full unit PDF 176–188), **not** human-reviewed publication QA. Quotation/scan verification for scripture is separate (pass-2 corpus); Gaskell OCR remains unreviewed.

## Thesis / through-line

Hall inventories ‘wonders’—eternal lamps, then concentrates on Greek oracles (Delphi/Pythia, Dodona)—as institutions where gods allegedly spoke, mixing antiquarian description with Mystery-friendly reading of prophetic frenzy.

## Argument map (source-linked)

- **PDF 176** (^hall-stota-pdf-176): Egyptian/Greek/Roman practice of sealing lighted lamps in sepulchers as death-god offerings.
- **PDF 179** (^hall-stota-pdf-179): Section THE GREEK ORACLES; Apollo’s prophetic establishments.
- **PDF 180** (^hall-stota-pdf-180): Delphian tripod plates/parts (Beaumont’s four-part description); caution against hasty conclusions as tripod form changed.
- **PDF 181** (^hall-stota-pdf-181): Pythian Apollo plate; fumes from fissure linked to Python’s body; Pythia etymology as religious frenzy.
- **PDF 182** (^hall-stota-pdf-182): Discovery story: shepherds/goats near Parnassus chasm.
- **PDF 183** (^hall-stota-pdf-183): Delivery of oracle then release of spirit; rest chamber after ecstasy.
- **PDF 184** (^hall-stota-pdf-184): Later older women dressed as girls as Pythiæ—institutional change after assaults.
- **PDF 185** (^hall-stota-pdf-185): Dodonean Jupiter plate.
