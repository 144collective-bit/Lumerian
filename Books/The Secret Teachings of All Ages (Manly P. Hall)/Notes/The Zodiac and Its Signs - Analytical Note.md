# The Zodiac and Its Signs — analytical note (deep pass)

**Not an endorsement** of Hall’s claims. **Review status:** source-read analytical draft (full unit PDF 147–160), **not** human-reviewed publication QA. Quotation/scan verification for scripture is separate (pass-2 corpus); Gaskell OCR remains unreviewed.

Source text: [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Zodiac and Its Signs.md|The Zodiac and Its Signs.md]]

## Thesis / through-line

Hall argues ancient religion, philosophy, and science were shaped by planetary/zodiacal study; zodiac signs are not rustic inventions but a cosmological alphabet later tied to equinoxes, precession, and human anatomy (zodiac-man).

- **PDF 152** (^hall-stota-pdf-152): Ritual calendar keyed to sun crossing equator / solstice turning points.
- **PDF 153** (^hall-stota-pdf-153): Libra as power of choice; early humanity compared to angels without moral knowledge of good/evil as Hall frames it.
