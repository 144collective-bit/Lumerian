## Thesis / through-line

Hall defines ceremonial magic as formulaic spirit-control, contrasts it with virtuous life, taxonomizes black magic / Faust, then illustrates Levi sword, Mendes goat, pacts, and planetary pentacles — descriptive grimoire tour with moral caution.
