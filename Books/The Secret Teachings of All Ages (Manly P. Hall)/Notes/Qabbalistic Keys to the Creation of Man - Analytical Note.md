## Thesis / through-line

Hall presents Qabbalistic hermeneutics (Gematria, Notarikon, Temurah) as keys unlocking Genesis/creation of Man, with Mercavah/Ezekiel vision plate and Noah zodiacal ark; includes two explicit verse forms used as gematria/temple-number examples.
