## Thesis / through-line

Hall reads flora as sexual-creative and wisdom emblems (yoni/phallus; Eden/Ark/Gate), then tree-cult (Round Table tree, Yggdrasil, bodhi, biblical trees) as proxies of Divinity and inverted-pyramid Tree of Life geometry.
