# The Initiation of the Pyramid — analytical note (deep pass)

**Not an endorsement** of Hall’s archaeology or initiatory reconstructions. Full unit [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Initiation of the Pyramid.md|Text]] (PDF **108–119**).

## Thesis / through-line

Hall treats the **Great Pyramid of Gizeh** as both a precision monument and a **Mystery school fabric**—passages and chambers as a drama of death/rebirth—while rehearsing classical anecdotes (Herodotus, Al-Ma’mun) and 19th-century measured diagrams (Smyth). The **Sphinx** is then folded in as related riddle/portal lore (Œdipus plate; P. Christian / Iamblichus portal theory flagged as unproven).

## Argument map (source-linked)

- **PDF 108** (^hall-stota-pdf-108): Pyramid as “supreme among the wonders”; mute witness to unknown civilization—sets lost-wisdom frame.
- **PDF 109** (^hall-stota-pdf-109): Herodotus used to hint that classical reporters may themselves have been initiates; Hall invites suspicion of concealment rather than supplying epigraphic proof.
- **PDF 110** (^hall-stota-pdf-110): Al-Ma’mun treasure anecdote (workers “discovering” planted funds)—Hall’s wonder-tale layer.
- **PDF 111–113** (^hall-stota-pdf-111 ff.): Construction materials (limestone/granite), chamber inventory **K/H/F/O** with dimensions for King’s Chamber; debates on “well” as robbers’ hole (Dupré vs Smyth). Plate: *A VERTICAL SECTION OF THE GREAT PYRAMID* (Smyth)—visually confirmed annotated section.
- **PDF 114–115** (^hall-stota-pdf-114, ^hall-stota-pdf-115): *ŒDIPUS AND THE SPHINX* (Lévi). Hall notes popular Sphinx-as-portal theory “has never been substantiated,” then quotes P. Christian’s initiatory portal narrative anyway as tradition.
- **PDF 116–119**: Continues Sphinx/pyramid initiation lore and Hall’s comparative Mystery reading (source pages carry the remainder of the chamber/initiation argument).
