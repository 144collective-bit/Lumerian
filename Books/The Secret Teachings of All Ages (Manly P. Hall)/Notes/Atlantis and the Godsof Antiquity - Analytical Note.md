# Atlantis and the Godsof Antiquity — analytical note (deep pass)

**Not an endorsement** of Hall’s worldview. Close summary derived from reading the full imported unit [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Atlantis and the Godsof Antiquity.md|Atlantis and the Godsof Antiquity.md]] (PDF **81–93**).

## Thesis / through-line

Hall uses the Atlantis narrative (via Plato and later reception) as a mythic key to ‘dying god’ cults—Tammuz/Ishtar, Atys/Adonis, Sabazius, and the Cabiri of Samothrace. Geological/speculative Atlantis claims are mixed with comparative mythology; the chapter’s durable core is the recurring murdered-and-revived deity pattern.
