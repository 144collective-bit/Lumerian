## Thesis / through-line

Hall insists alchemical formulae are primarily allegories of human regeneration (metals + divine alchemy); Egyptian key plate (Kircher); toad/putrefaction symbolism; Philalethes/Rosicrucian links.

## Argument map (source-linked)

- **PDF 495** (^hall-stota-pdf-495): Rosicrucians/Illuminati/Arabian sects as alchemical organizations.
- **PDF 496** (^hall-stota-pdf-496): Emblems primarily allegorical until esoteric significance grasped.
- **PDF 497** (^hall-stota-pdf-497): Key to Alchemy according to Egyptians plate (Kircher); relates base metals and human regeneration.
- **PDF 498** (^hall-stota-pdf-498): Toad = putrefaction/Death of the Compound; also lower elements in man.
