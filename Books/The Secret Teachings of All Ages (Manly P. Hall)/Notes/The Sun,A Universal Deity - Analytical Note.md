# The Sun, A Universal Deity — analytical note (deep pass)

**Not an endorsement** of solar-cult reductionism. Full unit [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Sun,A Universal Deity.md|Text]] (PDF **133–146**). Filename comma orthography preserved.

## Thesis / through-line

Hall argues sun-adoration is the root form of religion; later theologies (including Christian Trinity, Masonry, Samson, zodiacal labours) are **amplifications of solar process** (rising/noon/setting; twelve houses). Plates (lion of the sun, winged globe, Hindu regent, alchemical sun) supply iconographic witnesses. A **Col. 1** chapter-level form appears as provisional (no verse).
