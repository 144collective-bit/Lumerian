## Thesis / through-line

Hall rehearses Rosicrucian origin legends (Damcar, Christian Rosenkreuz), androgynous alchemy, adeptship as ignorance-to-wisdom transmutation, and competing theories (Agrippa, Buddhist invasion, etc.), with Geheime Figuren rose plates and Andreae portrait.
