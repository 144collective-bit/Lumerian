# The Life and Teachings of Thoth Hermes Trismegistus — analytical note (deep pass)

**Not an endorsement** of Hall’s historical or initiatory claims. Derived from reading the full unit [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Life and Teachings of Thoth Hermes Trismegistus.md|Text]] (PDF **94–107**).

## Thesis / through-line

Hall conflates Egyptian **Thoth**, Greek **Hermes**, and the legendary **Hermes Trismegistus** into a single culture-hero of writing, measure, and revealed “Way,” then retells **Poimandres / Divine Pymander** as a visionary initiation into Word, Light, and mind. Alchemy’s “hermetic” vocabulary is treated as a linguistic fossil of that prestige. The chapter is doxographic and mythic, not a critical dating of the Hermetica.

## Argument map (source-linked)

- **PDF 94** (^hall-stota-pdf-94): Opening temple-veil drama frames Hermes as initiator; thunder/lightning and rent veil set a Mystery-school tone before any documentary claim.
- **PDF 95** (^hall-stota-pdf-95): Plate *SUPPOSITIONS CONCERNING THE IDENTITY OF HERMES* (Historia Deorum Fatidicorum). Hall cites Iamblichus and others on Hermes’ identity; greenish-mist apparition episode belongs to Hall’s narrative framing, not evidence.
- **PDF 96–97** (^hall-stota-pdf-96, ^hall-stota-pdf-97): Cadmus/Thoth identifications via Bryant; Hall stresses Hermes as culture-bringer (letters, measure, time). Etymology of “hermetic” / “hermetically sealed” is offered as survival of alchemical reverence (**PDF 97**).
- **PDF 98** (^hall-stota-pdf-98): Clement-style catalogue of **forty-two books of Hermes** (36 philosophical + 6 medical) presented as Egyptian clerical curriculum—Hall reports the tradition rather than verifying a corpus.
- **PDF 99–100** (^hall-stota-pdf-99, ^hall-stota-pdf-100): Plate *THOTH, THE IBIS-HEADED* (Wilkinson). Section **POIMANDRES, THE VISION OF HERMES**; Hall lists Everard’s 1650 *Divine Pymander* among sources for his paraphrase.
- **PDF 101–107** (^hall-stota-pdf-101 ff.): Extended Poimandres paraphrase—darkness/waters, Word of God as the seeing/hearing principle in man, ascent ethics. Closes by tying Hermetic “One Way” to perennialist reading across faiths (**PDF 99** seed claim developed through the vision).
