# The Human Body in Symbolism — analytical note (deep pass)

**Not an endorsement** of Hall’s claims. **Review status:** source-read analytical draft (full unit PDF 216–227), **not** human-reviewed publication QA. Quotation/scan verification for scripture is separate (pass-2 corpus); Gaskell OCR remains unreviewed.

## Thesis / through-line

Hall elevates the human body as the oldest universal symbol and alleged fountainhead of theology: spirit:body :: God:universe; heart as spiritual organ; microcosm assigned elements/planets/signs; Böhme plates as witnesses.

## Argument map (source-linked)

- **PDF 216** (^hall-stota-pdf-216): Body as oldest, most profound, universal symbol; triune nature analysis in several cultures.
- **PDF 217** (^hall-stota-pdf-217): Biology as fountainhead of doctrines—modern religion ignorant of this (Hall’s claim).
- **PDF 218** (^hall-stota-pdf-218): Spirit anterior to form; Mysteries teaching.
- **PDF 219** (^hall-stota-pdf-219): Tetragrammaton in the human heart (Böhme); heart as most spiritual organ.
- **PDF 220** (^hall-stota-pdf-220): Generative/heart/brain centers mapped to physical/emotional/mental bodies.
- **PDF 221** (^hall-stota-pdf-221): Hand with Christ and twelve apostles; body as house of the individual, temple analogy.
- **PDF 222** (^hall-stota-pdf-222): Elements, planets, constellations assigned to body centers/organs.
- **PDF 224** (^hall-stota-pdf-224): Threefold life of the inner man plate.
