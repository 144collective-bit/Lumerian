## Thesis / through-line

Hall presents the Bembine / Mensa Isiaca as a contested Hermetic key to Egyptian–Chaldean–Greek theology, surveying Lévi and Westcott ‘keys,’ Tarot/trump hints, and Waite’s scepticism—without settling provenance.
