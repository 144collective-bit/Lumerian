## Thesis / through-line

Hall claims the great key of number-science died with Pythagoras; remaining names are enigmatical. He surveys gematria-like letter values, odd/even gendering of numbers, and plates (Hebrew–Greek alphabets; Sieve of Eratosthenes) as remnants.
