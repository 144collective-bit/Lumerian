## Thesis / through-line

Hall diagrams four worlds (Atziluth-Briah-Yetzirah-Assiah) as concentric Sephirothic reflections from the primordial point, with Creative Triad/shin flames and Choirs of Yetzirah — cosmogony as nested emanation map.
