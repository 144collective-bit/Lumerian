# Mystic Christianity — analytical note (pilot)

**Not an endorsement** of Hall’s worldview. Claims below are Hall’s (or authors he quotes), summarised with links back to the source chapter.

Source: [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity|Mystic Christianity — source text]] (PDF 590–601).

## Hall’s argument (faithful overview)

1. **Hidden / discrepant biography of Jesus.** Hall opens by asserting that the “true story” of Jesus of Nazareth is not fully given in the accepted Gospels or Apocrypha, and that early Fathers’ writings show irreconcilable discrepancies based largely on folklore ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-590|PDF 590]]).

2. **Irenaeus vs the “thirty-three years” pattern.** He quotes at length St. Irenæus, *Against Heresies*, arguing (via apostolic tradition including John) that Jesus lived to advanced age as a teacher, against a one-year ministry after baptism. Irenaeus cites Luke’s wording that Jesus was “beginning to be about thirty” at baptism ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-590|PDF 590]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-591|PDF 591]]). Hall (via Godfrey Higgins) treats this as evidence the later 33-year schema may have been adjusted to fit earlier Savior-God patterns.

3. **Pagan parallelisms admitted by early apologetics.** Justin Martyr’s *Apology* is quoted to show second-century willingness to compare Christ with “sons of Jupiter” ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-591|PDF 591]]).

4. **Possible multiple Joshuas / Essenian line; Asiatic study legends.** Hall relays Springett’s quotation from an undisclosed sectarian book about an Essenian Joshua stoned at 36, plus modern claims of India/Ceylon sojourns — presented as contested/supplementary, not proven ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-592|PDF 592]]).

5. **Melchizedek order and Christos vs Jesus.** He stresses New Testament language “after the order of Melchizedek,” wine/bread ceremony parallels, and a philosophical distinction: Jesus as teacher confused with *Christos* (solar / inner god-man). He attributes to Jesus the saying “I am the way, the truth, and the life…” ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-592|PDF 592]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-593|PDF 593]]).

6. **Bacchic / solar parallels (Panther, IHS).** Surname “Panther” traditions and IHS/ΥΗΣ numerical symbolism are used to argue early confusion with Bacchic rites ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-593|PDF 593]]).

7. **Essenes as custodians; Grail church.** Essenes (with Josephus praise) are proposed as custodians/initiators; discussion moves to the Secret Church of the Holy Grail, Joseph of Arimathea, cup and spear ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-594|PDF 594]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-597|PDF 597]]).

8. **Arthurian / Round Table symbolism.** Round Table, Siege Perilous, Garter/St George and the dragon (higher nature vs lower), Arthur as solar myth, knight counts linked to zodiac / apostles / twenty-four elders in Revelation ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-597|PDF 597]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-601|PDF 601]]). Serpent/dragon as initiate title; confusion with evil linked to misreading Genesis ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-599|PDF 599]]).

9. **Nimbus / aureole.** Closing figure group explains solar vs lunar halo forms after Audsley ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-600|PDF 600]]).
