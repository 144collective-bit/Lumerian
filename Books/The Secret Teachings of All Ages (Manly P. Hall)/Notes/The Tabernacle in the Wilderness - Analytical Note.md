## Thesis / through-line

Hall reads the wilderness Tabernacle as an initiatory microcosm copied from Egyptian temple mysticism, detailing architects Besaleel/Aholiab, court dimensions, high-priest garments, Urim/Thummim — Qabbalah as Jewish name for these secret writings.
