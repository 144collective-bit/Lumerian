## Thesis / through-line

Hall presents harmony as prerequisite of beauty; credits Pythagoras with diatonic scale discovery and music-of-the-spheres cosmology; uses Fludd monochord plates to diagram earth-to-heaven intervals and discusses Greek modes’ ethical power (Plato).
