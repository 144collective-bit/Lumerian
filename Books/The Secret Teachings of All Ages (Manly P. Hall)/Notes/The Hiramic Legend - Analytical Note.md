**Not an endorsement** of Hall’s claims. **Review status:** source-read analytical draft (full unit PDF 228–240), **not** human-reviewed publication QA. Quotation/scan verification for scripture is separate (pass-2 corpus); Gaskell OCR remains unreviewed.

## Thesis / through-line

Hall contrasts biblical CHiram (returns home) with Masonic murdered Master; reads the legend as ancient dying-god pattern applied to temple building, Christ analogy, and optional Bacon overlay—explicitly Masonic allegory, not historiography.
