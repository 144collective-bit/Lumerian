# How to read this source

1. Open a Text note. Page markers such as `[Pg 24]` from the transcription sit beside stable blocks `^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_24` or `^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_24`. Chapter heads use `^blavatsky-isis-v1-CHAPTER_I` (and the volume 2 equivalents).
2. Footnote callouts in volume 1 point to `Volume 1 Science/Text/21 Footnotes` blocks `^blavatsky-isis-v1-Footnote_N` (N = 1-918, complete pairing). Volume 2 bodies are split across `21 Footnotes`, `22 Footnotes B`, and `23 Footnotes C` as `^blavatsky-isis-v2-Footnote_N` (N = 1-1172).
3. Treat etymologies, Oriental narratives, and scientific or ecclesiastical polemics as **her claims**.
4. Do not use the index or the Bouton catalogue pages as if they were her argument.
5. Compare neighbouring sources only through the evidence notes. Shared words are not influence.
