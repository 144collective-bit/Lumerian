## How to read this source

Blavatsky presents a "Wisdom-Religion" or Hermetic philosophy as the middle key between what she calls the twin pretensions to infallibility of modern Science and of Theology (vol. 1 preface and "Before the Veil"). Volume 2 is, in her words, directed against theological Christianity and especially the Vatican, while claiming "not one word against the pure teachings of Jesus."

**Historical reconstructions, etymologies, dating of the Vedas, identifications of gods, and accounts of adepts or phenomena are her claims.** Separate:

- *source claim* — what she asserts;
- *KJV wording* — an installed verse she cites or that matches a quotation;
- *other authors* — Gaskell, Hall, Bullinger, or the other volume of this work;
- *editorial comparison* — a researcher juxtaposition, not influence.

Hall (1928) wrote after this book (1877/78). Shared subjects (elementals, mysteries, Kabala) are not evidence that he used these volumes unless a later pass finds an explicit citation.
