## Isis hub

- **Source:** Preface: "Reverently we stepped in spirit within the temple of Isis; to lift aside the veil of 'the one that is and was and shall be' at Saïs" (`^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_vi` to `Page_vii`).
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Isis|Isis]], which already holds Gaskell's `VEIL OF ISIS` as "the higher mind inaccessible to the lower consciousness."
- **Why:** She uses the Sais veil as the book's governing image of withheld-then-granted knowledge, not as a passing mythic name.
- **Tier:** `source-specific interpretation`; `editorial comparison` with Gaskell's VEIL OF ISIS (same motif; Gaskell interiorises it; she narrates a disclosure by living "sages"). Differing frames, not a demonstrated contradiction of one proposition.
- **Limit:** Bullinger's aside that Egypt "forgot" an Isis/Apis meaning (Taurus chapter of *Witness of the Stars*) is a different, unread-to-unread pairing until his passage and her later Egyptian chapters are set beside each other. ## Light hub

- **Source:** Glossary "Astral Light" (`^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_xxv` to `Page_xxvii`): sidereal light of Paracelsus, ether, *anima mundi*, identical with *akâsa*; the agent of magic and mesmerism.
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Light|Light]] (and neighbouring Sun language in the *Smaragdine Tablet* quotation, "Its father is the sun; its mother is the moon").
- **Why:** A developed technical sense of a cosmic light/ether, not a decorative use of the word.
- **Tier:** `source-specific interpretation`. `editorial resemblance` to other vault talk of a world-medium or higher light; not a citation of Gaskell or Hall.
- **Limit:** Do not equate this with Bullinger's Orion as "Light of the world" (a Gospel-person reading).

### Periodic deluge × Water hub × Genesis 6

- **Source:** At the end of every decimillennium / neros, a semi-universal deluge “like the legendary Noachian flood”; several such deluges (Plato, *Timaeus*); footnote 100 on giants ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_31|pages 30–31]]).
- **Targets:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Water|Water]]; [[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-4|Genesis 6:4]]–[[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-7|6:7]].
- **Relation:** She treats Noah’s flood as one instance of a repeating cosmological event. That is a different frame from a single Genesis flood. Footnote 100’s “special destruction of these giants” does not match the installed reason in 6:5–7 (man’s wickedness; destruction of man and animals).
- **Tier:** `contrast` of frames (periodic vs the Genesis narrative as commonly read); the giant-purpose clause is her overlay on Genesis, not a wording match.
- **Limit:** Not a comparison with unread Volume 2 material on Genesis.

### Saros / great year / precession × Bullinger

- **Source:** Great year of six *sars*; fn. 96 (Berosus: *sar* = 3,600 years, *neros* = 600, *sossus* = 60; Webster wrong to make *saros* an eclipse cycle of “about 6,586 years”); Higgins 24,000-year discussion; yuga table (pages 30–34).
- **Target:** Bullinger [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/20 For Signs and For Seasons#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00056|For Signs and For Seasons]]: precessional cycle of 25,800 years, and *Saros* as an eclipse cycle of 18 years and 11 days.
- **Relation:** Both discuss long astronomical cycles and use the name *saros*. They do **not** mean the same period by that name: she (via Berosus) gives 3,600 years and rejects an eclipse-cycle identification; he identifies Saros with the ~18-year eclipse cycle and separately states a 25,800-year precession. That is a contrast about the technical term, not proof that either author was answering the other (he wrote later). Shared neighbourhood only: twelve zodiac signs and knowledge of precession, which she reports from Draper’s Babylonian summary (pages 21–22).
- **Tier:** `contrast` on the meaning of *saros*; `editorial resemblance` limited to the fact that ancient Mesopotamian observers knew a twelve-sign zodiac and precession (Draper’s list vs Bullinger’s precessional cycle). Similarity of subject is not influence.
- **Limit:** Do not merge her kalpa arithmetic with his 25,800-year figure.

### Ecpyrosis × Fire hub

- **Source:** Popular Greek tradition that the world is alternately burned (Ecpyrosis / summer of the great year) and deluged (pages 30–31); Higgins: destruction *by fire* when the sun arrives at the equator (page 34).
- **Target:** [[Symbols & Concepts/Fire|Fire]], which already holds Gaskell’s higher/lower fire, Hall’s elementals, and Bullinger’s judgment-fire (Ara, Eridanus).
- **Relation:** Another cyclic world-fire frame, distinct from baptismal fire, elemental fire, and Bullinger’s lake-of-fire reading.
- **Tier:** `contrast` of frames (not a direct contradiction of one shared proposition).
- **Limit:** She is reporting traditional cyclic destruction, not commenting on Ara.
