## Argument of the chapter (as she makes it)

Modern names, she says, conceal old doctrines. An unnamed “old Book,” older than the Hebrew *Siphra Dzeniouta* compiled from it, illustrates the Divine Essence emanating from Adam as a luminous arc that returns a higher humanity and darkens as it nears the earth ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_1|page 1]]; footnote 50: Adam here in the sense of Greek *anthropos*). Hermetic philosophers, she reports, claim *seventy thousand* years of experience (footnote 51) that matter grew gross after a “fall,” that the first human body was half-ethereal, and that many races lived before the “mystic Adam.” “Coats of skin” close a downward arc of a cycle ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_2|page 2]]).

She treats archaeology (the Ebers papyrus as a Hermetic medical book; palaeolithic tools; Schliemann; Mariette) as failing to prove that early mankind was uniformly savage. Human history, “they” (the ancient andrologists) divided into **cycles** of civilisation and relapse ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_5|page 5]]). Chaldean cycles, she adds, do not embrace all mankind at once (page 6).

Plato’s method (universals to particulars; geometry) is preferred to Aristotle and to modern induction trapped in a finite circle of matter. Pythagorean “sacred numbers,” learned from Egyptian hierophants, are said to reconcile matter and spirit; the lower must emanate from the higher and be reabsorbed ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_7|pages 6–7]]). The veil of sanctuary secrecy, not ignorance, is why moderns find the ancients shallow (page 8).

**Tetractys:** four is sacred; the dodecahedron is “the trine” of four and the figure the Demiurgus used; four replaces the ineffable name as the mystics’ oath — the Tetractys ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_9|page 9]]). Pythagorean metempsychosis, properly read, would supply evolution’s “missing link” (her comparison).

**Vedic astronomy (her reading of Haug / the *Aitareya Brahmana*):** the “Serpent-Mantra” (*Sarpa-râjni*, earth as queen of serpents) is taken as evidence that the earth was known to be spherical and once gelatinous ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_10|page 10]]; fnn. 57–59). A passage that the sun “never sets nor rises” is taken as heliocentric knowledge by 2000 B.C.; Anâhit sentenced to turn round the sun; *Sattras* as imitations of the solar year (pages 10–11). These datings and the astronomical inferences are hers (via Haug). The English word “serpent” here names a Vedic earth-figure, not the Eden serpent.

**Two souls:** an Astral Soul (fluidic, perishable at each threshold) and the *Augoeides* (portion of Divine Spirit). God infused a portion of Divine Spirit into matter (pages 12–13). Zeno’s active/passive (male/female) pair produces the four elements.

**Genesis 1:30:** she quotes “I gave *a living soul*” as the sense of the Hebrew and calls “wherein *there is life*” an erroneous translation (page 13; footnote 64 points to Genesis i. 30). The installed KJV at [Genesis 1:30](<Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001.md#^kjv-gen-1-30>) does read “wherein there is life.” That confirms the wording she attacks; it does not confirm her Hebrew reconstruction.

**El / Al = sun:** via Drummond and Higgins she treats biblical *El* as *Al*, Mithra, the Sun; *Beth-El* as “House of the Sun” (page 13). Footnote 66 adds that hierophants considered the visible sun only an emblem of a central, invisible, spiritual Sun, and that leaving *Al* in place would have made “the *Jehovah* of Moses and the sun” appear identical to the uninitiated. Etymology and the Jehovah–sun identification are her (and her cited antiquarians’) claims, not vault-verified linguistics.

**Kabala and Sinai:** “the fundamental geometrical figure of the Kabala” was “given by the Deity itself to Moses on Mount Sinai” (page 14; footnote 67 cites Exodus xxv. 40). The installed verse [[Exodus 025#^kjv-exo-25-40|Exodus 25:40]] speaks of making furnishings “after their pattern, which was shewed thee in the mount.” She reads that pattern as the kabalistic figure. Volume 2’s “Mysteries of the Kabala” remains unread.

Isis in this chapter is “but the symbol of nature.” Modern philosophers “lift the Veil of Isis” and see only physical forms; the soul escapes them ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_16|page 16]]). En-Soph is a *Power*, not a personal God in the popular sense (same page).

**Magic** is “as old as man” (pages 18–19), distinguished from later sorcery; practiced in the *Vedas* and *Manu*, among Druids, and reformed rather than founded by Zoroaster. Apollonius of Tyana is an allegorical Hermetic counterpart of Solomon; she asks whether modern Masons understand who Hiram is (page 19). Pages 29–30 develop that critique: Masons seek a “lost word” in darkness; they should multiply 333 and strike 666, “the number of the Apocalyptic Beast” (editorial match to [[Revelation 013#^kjv-rev-13-18|Revelation 13:18]]; she does not print the verse). Volume 2 “Jesuitry and Masonry” is still unread; Hall’s Masonic chapters were not re-read against these pages on this pass.

**Scripture she cites or paraphrases in this chapter (wording checked against the installed KJV):**

- John i. 12, read as the anonymous Gnostic author of that gospel teaching that followers of Jesus’ “esoteric doctrine” become “sons of God” ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_2|page 2]]). Installed wording: [[John 001#^kjv-jhn-1-12|John 1:12]] (“as many as received him… power to become the sons of God”). The “anonymous Gnostic” authorship is her claim.
- “Know ye not, ye are *gods*?” put in “the Master’s” mouth (page 2). Installed [[John 010#^kjv-jhn-10-34|John 10:34]] is “Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods?” citing the law; [[Psalms 082#^kjv-psa-82-6|Psalm 82:6]] is “I have said, Ye are gods….” Her wording is a paraphrase, not a verbatim KJV quotation.
- Genesis i. 30 and Exodus xxv. 40, above.
- Acts vii. 22 on Moses (page 25; footnote 87): matches [[Acts 007#^kjv-act-7-22|Acts 7:22]] closely (“learned in all the wisdom of the Egyptians, and was mighty in words and in deeds”). She uses it inside a narrative that Moses learned magic from the Egyptian princess’s household (Thermuthis / Batria) — that surrounding story is not in the verse.
- Ennemoser is cited for the further claim that Jesus “passed the earlier years of his life” in Egypt (page 24). The installed Gospels do not narrate that sojourn as she states it; do not treat it as KJV fact.
- Footnote 100: the Babylonian story that giants survived the flood and founded Babylon is “in flat contradiction of the Bible narrative, which tells us that the deluge was sent for the special destruction of these *giants*.” Installed [[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-4|Genesis 6:4]] mentions giants; [[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-5|6:5]]–[[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-7|6:7]] give the reason as man’s great wickedness and announce the destruction of man, beast, creeping thing, and fowl. Her “special destruction of these giants” overstates the installed wording. Differing summaries, not a verse that itself says what footnote 100 says.

**Closing cosmology:** a “great year” of six *sars* (Aristotle via Censorinus) brings polar and equatorial climates to exchange places, with cataclysms; about every decimillennium / neros a semi-universal deluge; Greek popular tradition of alternate Cataclysm (winter) and Ecpyrosis (summer) ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_30|pages 30–31]]). Footnote 96: she rejects Webster’s identification of *saros* with an eclipse cycle of “about 6,586 years,” and reports Berosus: *sar* = 3,600 years, *neros* = 600, *sossus* = 60. She then prints the four yugas (Satya 1,728,000 through Kali 432,000; Maha-yug 4,320,000; kalpa 4,320,000,000) and says we are in the Kali-yug of the twenty-eighth age of the seventh manwantara (pages 31–32). Higgins’s 24,000-year “great year” (four times 6,000; world destroyed by fire or water at each 6,000-year turn) is rehearsed and then criticised as a calculation that ignored sacerdotal secrecy and produced Adventist-style expectations (pages 33–34). “As above, so it is below…” (page 35) restates the hermetic formula already quoted from the Smaragdine Tablet in the glossary.

The chapter ends by refusing both the preacher’s personal God-and-Devil and the materialist’s “gray matter,” affirming an intuited God in nature — the “Central Spiritual Sun” (page 29) — and a threefold post-mortem distinction (*manes*, *anima*, *umbra*) drawn from Ovid (page 37). A new cycle, she says, is about to begin (page 38).

## What this vault should not do with Chapter I

- Do not treat 70,000 years, pre-Adamic winged races, Vedic heliocentrism-by-2000-B.C., or the yuga arithmetic as independently verified chronology.
- Do not equate the Vedic “Serpent-Mantra” with the biblical serpent, or *El* with the installed KJV’s “God,” on her etymology alone.
- Do not read footnote 100 as a faithful restatement of Genesis 6:5–7.
- Do not take the Masonic 333/666 passage, or the Hiram question, as a completed comparison with Hall or with unread Volume 2, Chapter VIII.
