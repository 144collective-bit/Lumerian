## Text

> [!note] Unnumbered preface / superscription (before Judges 3:21)
> And he arose out of his seat. And he arose out of his seat.

###### Judges 3:1

Now these are the nations which the LORD left, to prove Israel by them, even as many of Israel as had not known all the wars of Canaan;

^kjv-jdg-3-1

###### Judges 3:2

Only that the generations of the children of Israel might know, to teach them war, at the least such as before knew nothing thereof;

^kjv-jdg-3-2

###### Judges 3:3

Namely, five lords of the Philistines, and all the Canaanites, and the Sidonians, and the Hivites that dwelt in mount Lebanon, from mount Baalhermon unto the entering in of Hamath.

^kjv-jdg-3-3

###### Judges 3:4

And they were to prove Israel by them, to know whether they would hearken unto the commandments of the LORD, which he commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.

^kjv-jdg-3-4

###### Judges 3:5

And the children of Israel dwelt among the Canaanites, Hittites, and Amorites, and Perizzites, and Hivites, and Jebusites:

^kjv-jdg-3-5

###### Judges 3:6

And they took their daughters to be their wives, and gave their daughters to their sons, and served their gods.

^kjv-jdg-3-6

###### Judges 3:7

And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD, and forgat the LORD their God, and served Baalim and the groves.

^kjv-jdg-3-7

###### Judges 3:8

Therefore the anger of the LORD was hot against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of Chushanrishathaim king of Mesopotamia: and the children of Israel served Chushanrishathaim eight years.

^kjv-jdg-3-8

###### Judges 3:9

And when the children of Israel cried unto the LORD, the LORD raised up a deliverer to the children of Israel, who delivered them, even Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother.

^kjv-jdg-3-9

###### Judges 3:10

And the Spirit of the LORD came upon him, and he judged Israel, and went out to war: and the LORD delivered Chushanrishathaim king of Mesopotamia into his hand; and his hand prevailed against Chushanrishathaim.

^kjv-jdg-3-10

###### Judges 3:11

And the land had rest forty years. And Othniel the son of Kenaz died.

^kjv-jdg-3-11

###### Judges 3:12

And the children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the LORD: and the LORD strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel, because they had done evil in the sight of the LORD.

^kjv-jdg-3-12

###### Judges 3:13

And he gathered unto him the children of Ammon and Amalek, and went and smote Israel, and possessed the city of palm trees.

^kjv-jdg-3-13

###### Judges 3:14

So the children of Israel served Eglon the king of Moab eighteen years.

^kjv-jdg-3-14

###### Judges 3:15

But when the children of Israel cried unto the LORD, the LORD raised them up a deliverer, Ehud the son of Gera, a Benjamite, a man lefthanded: and by him the children of Israel sent a present unto Eglon the king of Moab.

^kjv-jdg-3-15

###### Judges 3:16

But Ehud made him a dagger which had two edges, of a cubit length; and he did gird it under his raiment upon his right thigh.

^kjv-jdg-3-16

###### Judges 3:17

And he brought the present unto Eglon king of Moab: and Eglon was a very fat man.

^kjv-jdg-3-17

###### Judges 3:18

And when he had made an end to offer the present, he sent away the people that bare the present.

^kjv-jdg-3-18

###### Judges 3:19

But he himself turned again from the quarries that were by Gilgal, and said, I have a secret errand unto thee, O king: who said, Keep silence. And all that stood by him went out from him.

^kjv-jdg-3-19

###### Judges 3:20

And Ehud came unto him; and he was sitting in a summer parlour, which he had for himself alone. And Ehud said, I have a message from God unto thee.

^kjv-jdg-3-20

###### Judges 3:21

And Ehud put forth his left hand, and took the dagger from his right thigh, and thrust it into his belly:

^kjv-jdg-3-21

###### Judges 3:22

And the haft also went in after the blade; and the fat closed upon the blade, so that he could not draw the dagger out of his belly; and the dirt came out.

^kjv-jdg-3-22

###### Judges 3:23

Then Ehud went forth through the porch, and shut the doors of the parlour upon him, and locked them.

^kjv-jdg-3-23

###### Judges 3:24

When he was gone out, his servants came; and when they saw that, behold, the doors of the parlour were locked, they said, Surely he covereth his feet in his summer chamber.

^kjv-jdg-3-24

###### Judges 3:25

And they tarried till they were ashamed: and, behold, he opened not the doors of the parlour; therefore they took a key, and opened them: and, behold, their lord was fallen down dead on the earth.

^kjv-jdg-3-25

###### Judges 3:26

And Ehud escaped while they tarried, and passed beyond the quarries, and escaped unto Seirath.

^kjv-jdg-3-26

###### Judges 3:27

And it came to pass, when he was come, that he blew a trumpet in the mountain of Ephraim, and the children of Israel went down with him from the mount, and he before them.

^kjv-jdg-3-27

###### Judges 3:28

And he said unto them, Follow after me: for the LORD hath delivered your enemies the Moabites into your hand. And they went down after him, and took the fords of Jordan toward Moab, and suffered not a man to pass over.

^kjv-jdg-3-28

###### Judges 3:29

And they slew of Moab at that time about ten thousand men, all lusty, and all men of valour; and there escaped not a man.

^kjv-jdg-3-29

###### Judges 3:30

So Moab was subdued that day under the hand of Israel. And the land had rest fourscore years.

^kjv-jdg-3-30

###### Judges 3:31

And after him was Shamgar the son of Anath, which slew of the Philistines six hundred men with an ox goad: and he also delivered Israel.

^kjv-jdg-3-31
