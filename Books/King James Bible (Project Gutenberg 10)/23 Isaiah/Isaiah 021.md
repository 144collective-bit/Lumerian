## Text

> [!note] Unnumbered preface / superscription (before Isaiah 21:10)
> And he answered and said, Babylon is fallen, is fallen; and all the graven images of her gods he hath broken unto the ground. And he answered and said, Babylon is fallen, is fallen; and all the graven images of her gods he hath broken unto the ground.

###### Isaiah 21:1

The burden of the desert of the sea. As whirlwinds in the south pass through; so it cometh from the desert, from a terrible land.

^kjv-isa-21-1

###### Isaiah 21:2

A grievous vision is declared unto me; the treacherous dealer dealeth treacherously, and the spoiler spoileth. Go up, O Elam: besiege, O Media; all the sighing thereof have I made to cease.

^kjv-isa-21-2

###### Isaiah 21:3

Therefore are my loins filled with pain: pangs have taken hold upon me, as the pangs of a woman that travaileth: I was bowed down at the hearing of it; I was dismayed at the seeing of it.

^kjv-isa-21-3

###### Isaiah 21:4

My heart panted, fearfulness affrighted me: the night of my pleasure hath he turned into fear unto me.

^kjv-isa-21-4

###### Isaiah 21:5

Prepare the table, watch in the watchtower, eat, drink: arise, ye princes, and anoint the shield.

^kjv-isa-21-5

###### Isaiah 21:6

For thus hath the LORD said unto me, Go, set a watchman, let him declare what he seeth.

^kjv-isa-21-6

###### Isaiah 21:7

And he saw a chariot with a couple of horsemen, a chariot of asses, and a chariot of camels; and he hearkened diligently with much heed:

^kjv-isa-21-7

###### Isaiah 21:8

And he cried, A lion: My lord, I stand continually upon the watchtower in the daytime, and I am set in my ward whole nights:

^kjv-isa-21-8

###### Isaiah 21:9

And, behold, here cometh a chariot of men, with a couple of horsemen.

^kjv-isa-21-9

###### Isaiah 21:10

O my threshing, and the corn of my floor: that which I have heard of the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, have I declared unto you.

^kjv-isa-21-10

###### Isaiah 21:11

The burden of Dumah. He calleth to me out of Seir, Watchman, what of the night? Watchman, what of the night?

^kjv-isa-21-11

###### Isaiah 21:12

The watchman said, The morning cometh, and also the night: if ye will enquire, enquire ye: return, come.

^kjv-isa-21-12

###### Isaiah 21:13

The burden upon Arabia. In the forest in Arabia shall ye lodge, O ye travelling companies of Dedanim.

^kjv-isa-21-13

###### Isaiah 21:14

The inhabitants of the land of Tema brought water to him that was thirsty, they prevented with their bread him that fled.

^kjv-isa-21-14

###### Isaiah 21:15

For they fled from the swords, from the drawn sword, and from the bent bow, and from the grievousness of war.

^kjv-isa-21-15

###### Isaiah 21:16

For thus hath the LORD said unto me, Within a year, according to the years of an hireling, and all the glory of Kedar shall fail:

^kjv-isa-21-16

###### Isaiah 21:17

And the residue of the number of archers, the mighty men of the children of Kedar, shall be diminished: for the LORD God of Israel hath spoken it.

^kjv-isa-21-17
