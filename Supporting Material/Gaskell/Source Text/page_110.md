The Incarnate Self evolving in the souls of humanity, struggles upward to enjoy in the buddhic consciousness the fruit of experience and aspiration, while the Supreme Self is a witness and inactive. The Incarnate Self is immersed in illusion and ignorance, suffering and sorrow, but when having conquered the lower nature and risen above it, it perceives the Supreme Self, then its sorrow ceases ; ignorance and illusion are dispelled, and the Truth is made manifest.
