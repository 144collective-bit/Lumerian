VARA,—AN ENCLOSURE, FOUR SQUARE :— A symbol of the lower quaternary, —the arena of life. ** Then make that vara the length of a riding-ground on each of the four sides; and bring thither the seeds of cattle, oxen, men, dogs, birds, and red blazing fires.’’— Vendtdad, II. The Supreme now directs that the lower quaternary be prepared in order that the six centres of the qualities for the manifestation of the Divine life may be introduced.
