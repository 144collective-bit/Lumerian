The higher nature, atma-buddhi (upper shell), influences the lower through the mind (air). It bends down to the lower whenever the lower aspires towards truth and goodness. The planes of manifestation are the means in part by which the soul is enabled to sacrifice the lower for the higher, and through spiritual idealism rise triumphant at the cycle’s end. TRANSMUTATION OF LOWER QUALITIES INTO HIGHER :— This is a spiritual process which takes place in the soul by slow degrees, as evolution proceeds on the lower planes. Transmutation is effected by the Higher Self acting through buddhi-manas on the lower qualities as they arrive at a state of preparedness for being raised to a higher condition. It is the function of the Holy Spirit, atmabuddhi, to transmute lower into higher, and this is the meaning of white magic and alchemy.
