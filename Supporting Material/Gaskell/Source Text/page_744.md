SWASTIKA, OR FYLFOT SIGN :— Emblem of the wheel or cycle of life. The cross with the four arms signifies the four ‘‘ winds,’’ i.e. the quaternary or four lower planes of mature which are the arena of the soul’s development during the cycle. The emblem thus becomes significant of high aims through a period of stress and struggle, with attainment of the heart’s desire at the ending. The widespread appearance of the emblem and its associations show it to be a sacred symbol emanating from the one source of all inspired myths and scriptures, namely, the Holy Spirit on the buddhic plane. ** This was also known as gammadion.
