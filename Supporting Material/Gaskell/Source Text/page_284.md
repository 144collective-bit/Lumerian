FIRE was laid down as an essential principle in his system that ‘he who approached to fire would receive a light from divinity,’ and that ‘through divine fire, all the stains produced by generation would be purged away.’ Therefore it was that ‘children were made to pass through fire to Moloch,’ to purge them from original sin, and through this purgation many a helpless babe became a victim to the bloody divinity. Among the Pagan Romans, this purifying by passing through the fire was equally observed ; ‘for,’ says Ovid, enforcing the practice, ‘fire purifies both the shepherd and the sheep.” Among the Hindus, from time immemorial, fire has been worshipped for its purifying efficacy. Thus a worshipper is represented by Colcbrooke, according to the sacred books, as addressing the fire: ‘Salutation to thee (O fire !), who dost seize oblations, to thee who dost shine, to thee who dost scintillate, may thy auspicious flame burn our foes; mayest thou, the Purifier, be auspicious unto us.’ ’’—A. His.top, The Two Babylons, p. 120. See AGNI, BUDDHI, CHAFF, CONFLAGRATION, CREMATION, DEVIL, EartTH, FURNACE, Goats, HELL, MAN (bad), Purcatory, TRANSMUTATION, WICKEDNESS, WATER. FIRE, ELEMENTAL BRANES :— Symbolic of elemental formative energy enclosed by the forms which are builded of astral or etheric matter to encase the monads of life. See MONADS OF FORM. IN MEMFIRE FANNED BY THE WIND :— A symbol of the spiritual emotions of love and truth as developed by the mental activities. ““Then like the young sun on the eastern mountains, or the fire when fanned by the wind, the prince (Buddha) gradually grew in all due perfection, like the moon in the fortnight of brightness.’’"— Buddha-Karita, Bk. II. 20. The “rising sun” is a symbol of the ascension of the Self in the soul ; and this ascension is implied in the ‘‘fire”* of celestial love and truth which is brought forth amid the kama-manasic nature wherein its development is commenced, as the emotion-nature first of all begins to grow up. As this refers to the period of Involution the “ perfection ” is archetypal, as is the ‘‘ very good ” of the first chapter of Genesis. See BuppHa, Fan, WIND. 277 FIRMAMENT FIRE OF HELL :— A symbol of the spiritual energy of Buddhi consuming the lower qualities on the astral plane. This takes place unceasingly during the cycle of evolution; it is the means whereby the soul is purified and perfected in its passage through life. ‘* Isis nursed the child (the son of the king and queen) and fed it by giving it her finger to suck instead of the breast; she likewise put him every night into the fire in order to consume his mortal part, whilst transforming herself into a swallow, she hovered round the pillar and bemoaned her sad fate.”— Prourarcna, Isie and Osarvs, § 16. The Wisdom element, Buddhi, now sustains the personality (child of astro-mental parentage) and affords it the means of evolution through the investing stimulation of latent capacity, by indication, or suggestion, rather than by direct means. And whilst it was night, that is, unknown to it, all that was perishable in the soul was burned away through the action of Buddhi. Meanwhile, as the spiritual daylight broke, Wisdom, through sufferings, ascends spirally round the Divine pillar of aspiration which is in the soul. See ASTARTE, COLUMN, Fan, FURNACE, Haprs, HELL, IMMORTALITY, Isis, NIGHT, PERSONALITY, RE-INCARNATION. FIRE AND STEEL :— Emblem of disintegrating and crystallising forces on the mental plane. FIRE-PRIESTS :— Symbolic of spiritual aspirations on the mental plane drawing forth the buddhic emotions. See AGNIDHRA, ATHORNE, PRIEST OF ALTAR. FIRMAMENT :— Symbol of the higher mental plane as the firm foundation of creation, and as a central plane of consciousness dividing the upper planes of Reality from the lower planes of illusion. **And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so. And God ealled the firmament Heaven.’’—GEN. i. 7, 8.
