AMEN, OR AMON (THE HIDDEN ONE) :— A symbol of the Higher Self incarnate on the lower planes of the soul. “ As Amen-Ré he was worshipped as the supremo *‘ King of the Gods,’ a creator and soul of the universe, inscrutable and eternal. The ram was sacred to him, and in this animal he was believed to be incarnate.’’—Non-classical Mythology, p. 9. The incarnate Self is symbolised by the ram of sacrifice,—the ‘‘ Lamb of God.”’ See Aru-RA, Crown (sekhet), Derry (hidden), Mut, PLumes, SCEPTRE. The Christ shall appear from within, and shall declare the Truth and the Right. He shall be born of Wisdom on the higher mental plane. It is remarkable how this conception rose and grew both in later Judaism and in Greek thought till it reached its culmination in Christianity. .
