When the soul or individuality had possessed itself of the Divine Spark, the law of the Supreme required that from the lower nature (earth) there should arise the Emotion-nature, which, after being allied with the lower mind, shall be the means through the temptations of desire (jar) of bringing strife, suffering and evils of all kinds into the human soul. But Hope, or the promise of salvation, remains to encourage the efforts of the soul to rise.
