It is nothing but the activity of the mind enslaved by sensations, desires, and passions. It is blind self-assertion of the lower mature: pain and sorrow result therefrom. Its power is proportional to its lack of intelligence. ‘Lust and anger shall not seduce, and the dog covetousness shall be destroyed *' (Guru Arjan).—MACAULIFFE, The Sikh Religion, Vol. III. p. 129.
