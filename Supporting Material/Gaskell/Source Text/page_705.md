The ego in the post-mortem state is the centre of a world of attractive memories, and with these the consciousness is filled, so that it seems to be the whole of things. “Just as there hovers in space, a hawk or an eagle, after it has circled round, folds its wings wearied, and drops to the ground, so also the spirit hastens to that state in which fallen asleep it no longer experiences any desires nor sees any dream image” (Brihad.
