He can now traverse the lower planes at will, and entrance to the higher mind (iron ceiling) is his, and Wisdom’s heights. He hath the sign (panther skin) upon him that the lower emotions have been overcome and displaced by the higher; and aspiration (staff) and the will are active within him.
