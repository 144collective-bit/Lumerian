DOVE-SELLERS :— Symbolic of hybrid qualities seemingly gentle and beneficent, but really hard and grasping. ‘* Jesus overthrew the tables of the money-changers, and the seats of them that sold doves.’’—Mar. xxi. 12. The ‘ dove-sellers’’ are those who profess to be kind and amiable, but who are in reality selfish and indifferent, and who therefore prostitute the ideals which they profess to be living up to.
