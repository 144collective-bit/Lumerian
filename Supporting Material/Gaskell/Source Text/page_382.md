HOUSE “The second place of earth's joy is whereon the fai one erecta a house wherein every blessing of life is thriving.” —Vendidad. III. “Earth’s joy” is so expressed, since the awakenment of the Self must proceed first from the physical plane. The “second place ™ signifies the plane of the higher mind whereon the indwelling Self, through the egos, builds up the causal-body within which the transmuted experiences are collected together. ** The earth brings forth for them many things of all sorts, of which they carry the most serviceable into the house and use them.’’—Anazagoras, FAIRBANKS, 10. The lower nature yields experiences for the mind, of which those which are capable of transmutation are conveyed to the causal-body wherewith to build it up.
