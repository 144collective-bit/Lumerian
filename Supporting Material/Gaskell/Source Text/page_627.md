The spiritual egos descend periodically to the lower planes in order to incarnate in human bodies; and after physical death, ascend to the higher mental plane (stone of thought). If the egos continue to be attached by desire to the lower life they return, after a period, to re-incarnation ; but if they are perfected, they are liberated from tho lower nature and return again no more.
