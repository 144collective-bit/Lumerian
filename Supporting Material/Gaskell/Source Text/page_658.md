The Self, the Saviour, is said to arrive on the mental plane to be born within the soul, minus a “ sandal,’’— that is to say, without the power to proceed mentally, or having no means for self-expression. The “ one sandal ”’ is a symbol of relativity, implying that the perfect duality of spirit-matter cannot be expressed actually on the lower planes. The “losing of a sandal’? implies that the inner sense of power is not yet realised in the soul, though the means for attaining it is now present. The ‘river Anaurus’ signifies limitation, that which separates higher from lower, truth revealed, definition of mental concepts, or that which is made evident.
