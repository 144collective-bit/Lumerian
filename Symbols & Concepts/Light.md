# Light

This is a **shared research hub**, not a universal definition. Each reading stays attributed to its source. Gaskell dictionary text is `machine-extracted-unreviewed` OCR unless a note says otherwise. Cross-source comments below are **editorial comparison** unless labelled as an explicit citation. Shared wording or a similar symbol is not evidence that one author used or influenced the other.


## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

A primary creation-symbol and a name for truth, wisdom, or manifested divinity. Because the word is so common, lexical hits are especially weak evidence.

## From E. W. Bullinger, The Witness of the Stars (6 November 2026)

Orion is titled “Light Breaking Forth in the Redeemer.” He derives the name from a Hebrew root “light” and gives Akkadian *Ur-ana* as “light of heaven.” Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/15 Chapter I The Sign TAURUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00040|ORION]]. Psalm 19’s “enlightening” language is part of the same frame (see the extracted footnotes’ opening notes).

- Tier: source-specific interpretation. The etymologies are his claims, not verified linguistics.
- Limit: Do not treat the existence of this Light hub as confirmation of *Ur-ana*.
- See [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

He also calls Orion "the Light of the world" and sets that title beside [[Isaiah 060#^kjv-isa-60-1|Isaiah 60:1-3]] and [[Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-1|Psalm 19:1]]. Capricornus uses the same "Light of the World" / "true light" language at the Nativity (see the Sun hub). The person-of-the-Redeemer reading is his.

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled (6 November 2026)

The volume 1 glossary entry "Astral Light" ([[05 Before the Veil#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_xxv|pages xxv]]–[[05 Before the Veil#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_xxvii|xxvii]]) identifies it with the sidereal light of Paracelsus, ether, *anima mundi*, and *akâsa*, and treats it as the agent of magic and mesmerism. The same pages quote the *Smaragdine Tablet*: "Its father is the sun; its mother is the moon."

- Tier: source-specific interpretation. Editorial resemblance to other vault talk of a world-medium or higher light; she does not cite Gaskell or Hall.
- Limit: Do not equate this technical "astral light" with Bullinger's Orion as "Light of the world" (a Gospel-person reading already on this hub).
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

Chapter I restates the hermetic "As above, so it is below" ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_35|page 35]]) and the "Central Spiritual Sun" already noted on the Sun hub. That continues, rather than replaces, the glossary's Astral Light / *akasa* identification.

- Tier: source-specific interpretation. Still not Bullinger's Orion as "Light of the world."
- See [[Symbols & Concepts/Sun|Sun]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter II (6 November 2026)

On [[07 Chapter II Phenomena and Forces#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_58|page 58]] she says the various scientific names for the unknown agency (psychic force, ectenic force, unconscious cerebration, and the rest) "can be condensed in two words: the kabalistic ASTRAL LIGHT." Pages 55-57 align that light with Plato, the Chaldean Oracles, and the *Anima Mundi*.

- Tier: source-specific interpretation, developing the glossary entry already on this hub.
- Limit: Still not Bullinger's Orion as "Light of the world," and not a citation of Gaskell.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter II Phenomena and Forces - Analytical Note|Chapter II analytical note]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapters III–IV (6 November 2026)

In Chapter III, once darkness is dispersed and “there was light,” Brahma's understanding opens upon archetypal forms ([[08 Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_92|pages 92-93]]). That unnumbered phrase neighbours [Genesis 1:3](<Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001.md#^kjv-gen-1-3>) but is not printed as a citation.

In Chapter IV she identifies Thury's *psychode* / *ectenic* force, Crookes's psychic force, Éliphas Lévi's astral or sidereal light, and Hindu *Akasa* as “identical” and long known to gymnosophists ([[09 Chapter IV Theories Respecting Psychic Phenomena#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_113|pages 112-113]]). That develops the glossary and Chapter II identifications already on this hub.

- Tier: `source-specific interpretation` for the force-identity; the Genesis 1:3 neighbour is resemblance only (see the review queue).
- Limit: Still not Bullinger's Orion as “Light of the world,” and not a citation of Gaskell.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind - Analytical Note|Chapter III analytical note]]; [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter IV Theories Respecting Psychic Phenomena - Analytical Note|Chapter IV analytical note]].
