# Logos and the Word

This is a **shared research hub**, not a universal definition. Each reading stays attributed to its source. Gaskell dictionary text is `machine-extracted-unreviewed` OCR unless a note says otherwise. Cross-source comments below are **editorial comparison** unless labelled as an explicit citation. Shared wording or a similar symbol is not evidence that one author used or influenced the other.


## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

A cluster, not one lemma: Gaskell’s `LOGOS (REASON-SPEECH)` and `WORD OF GOD`, the Johannine “Word,” and Hall’s later use of “Logos” in solar and Qabbalistic settings. Do not treat these labels as interchangeable.

## From E. W. Bullinger, The Witness of the Stars (6 November 2026)

He argues that the pictured heavens and “the Volume of the Book” say the same thing because they have one Divine origin. Psalm 19’s pairing of the heavens’ speech with the law is his formal warrant ([[Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-1|Psalm 19:1]], [[Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-7|Psalm 19:7]]). Source: close of [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/03 Introduction|Introduction]] and [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/19 Summary|Summary]].

- Tier: source-specific interpretation of that pairing; explicit citation of the Psalm; contrast with Gaskell’s Logos-as-Higher-Self and Hall’s “Second Logos or Christ.”
- Limit: He rarely uses the word “Logos.” Neighbourhood is not identity.
- See [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter II (6 November 2026)

On [[07 Chapter II Phenomena and Forces#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_56|page 56]] the Cosmos is "the Son" with Plato, and the younger Horus is the Idea "going forth from the *Logos*" and clothed with matter (she is using Dunlap and Movers).

- Tier: source-specific interpretation.
- Limit: Not an exegesis of the Johannine prologue, and not automatically Hall's "Light of the Logos."
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter II Phenomena and Forces - Analytical Note|Chapter II analytical note]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter III (6 November 2026)

“In all the primitive religions, the ‘Son of the Father’ is the creative God—*i.e.*, His thought made visible.” Brahma, Jehovah (Adam-Kadmon), and Osiris or rather Pimander occupy the second place of a trinity ([[08 Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_93|pages 92-93]]). This develops, without repeating, Chapter II's Cosmos-as-Son / younger Horus going forth from the *Logos*.

- Tier: `source-specific interpretation`.
- Limit: Not an exegesis of the Johannine prologue.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind - Analytical Note|Chapter III analytical note]].
