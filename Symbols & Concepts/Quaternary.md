# Quaternary

This is a **shared research hub**, not a universal definition. Each reading stays attributed to its source. Gaskell dictionary text is `machine-extracted-unreviewed` OCR unless a note says otherwise. Cross-source comments below are **editorial comparison** unless labelled as an explicit citation. Shared wording or a similar symbol is not evidence that one author used or influenced the other.


## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

Several installed “fours”: Gaskell’s QUATERNARY as four planes of manifestation; Gaskell’s CROSS opening, in which a “Divine Ray” passes through the quaternary; Hall’s tetrad / tetractys and the four arms of the cross as four elements. Counting to four is not a proof that these schemes are the same.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Pythagorean Mathematics#^hall-stota-pdf-212|Pythagorean Mathematics, PDF 212]] — Hall writes that “the tetrad is also the first geometric solid” and that “Pythagoras maintained that the soul of man consists of a tetrad, the four powers of the soul being mind, science, opinion, and sense.”
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Pythagorean Mathematics#^hall-stota-pdf-214|PDF 214]] — “The mysterious Pythagorean tetractys, or four rows of dots, increasing from 1 to 4, was symbolic of the stages of creation.” (1+2+3+4 = 10, the decad.)
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Cross and the Crucifixion#^hall-stota-pdf-608|The Cross and the Crucifixion, PDF 608]] — “There are four basic elements… and the ancients represented them by the four arms of the cross,” with earth/bull, water/scorpion-serpent-eagle, fire/lion, and air/winged human head.

An earlier page of the mathematics chapter also quotes Plutarch on a “Tetractys, or sacred Quaternion” ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Pythagorean Mathematics#^hall-stota-pdf-202|PDF 202]]); that paragraph is a quoted ancient source inside Hall, not Hall’s own four-elements cross.

Hall does **not** name Gaskell. Analytical notes: [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Notes/Pythagorean Mathematics - Analytical Note|Pythagorean Mathematics — analytical note]], [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Notes/The Cross and the Crucifixion - Analytical Note|Cross — analytical note]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter I (6 November 2026)

On [[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_9|page 9]] she holds four sacred among the Pythagoreans; the dodecahedron is "the trine" of four and the Demiurgus's figure for the universe; four replaces the ineffable name as the mystics' oath — the Tetractys.

- Tier: source-specific interpretation; editorial resemblance to Hall's tetractys report at [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Pythagorean Mathematics#^hall-stota-pdf-214|PDF 214]] and the quoted "sacred Quaternion" at [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Pythagorean Mathematics#^hall-stota-pdf-202|PDF 202]]. Hall wrote later; no influence inferred. She emphasises the name-oath and dodecahedron; Hall emphasises 1+2+3+4 = 10.
- Limit: Gaskell's QUATERNARY ("four planes of manifestation") remains a neighbouring four, not this oath or Hall's decad.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter III (6 November 2026)

Brahma, the creating *demiurgos*, “has in his allegorical representations four heads, answering to the four cardinal points” ([[08 Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_91|page 91]]).

- Tier: `source-specific interpretation`. Editorial neighbourhood of this hub's other fours (Pythagorean Tetractys from Chapter I; Hall's quaternion; Gaskell's four planes) — a shared number, not the same object.
- Limit: Do not treat four heads as the Tetractys oath already recorded from Chapter I.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind - Analytical Note|Chapter III analytical note]].
