# Tree of Life

Keep the Edenic “tree of life,” the “tree of knowledge,” and the Qabbalistic Tree of the Sephiroth in separate boxes. They share a tree-image; they are not shown here to be the same object.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

Do **not** read Hall’s Sephirothic tree as this Eden tree. Hall’s chapter [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Tree of the Sephiroth|The Tree of the Sephiroth]] (PDF 373–382) describes a ten-globe diagram:

> The Sephirothic Tree consists of ten globes of luminous splendor arranged in three… ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Tree of the Sephiroth#^hall-stota-pdf-373|PDF 373]])

Use [[Symbols & Concepts/Sephiroth|Sephiroth]] for that diagram. The only justified link from this hub to Hall’s tree is **editorial resemblance**: both use a tree as a map of divine life / emanation. That is not identity and not a citation.

Hall’s Golden Legend cross-wood discussion ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Cross and the Crucifixion#^hall-stota-pdf-602|PDF 602]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. ## From E. W. Bullinger, The Witness of the Stars (6 November 2026)

In Hercules he says the Mighty Man shall destroy the Old Serpent and "open the way again, not to fabled 'apples of gold,' but to the 'tree of life' itself." Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/07 Chapter III The Sign SCORPIO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00016|Hercules]]. Footnote 44 notes that Bayer placed an apple branch in the figure's hand as an emblem of the Hesperides apples.

- Tier: source-specific interpretation. Thin but explicit naming of the biblical tree of life, set against a Greek apple-story he treats as a later fable.
- Limit: This is not a sephiroth teaching and not the Golden Legend cross-wood story already distinguished above. Argo's recovered treasure is a different picture and was not used here.

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, footnote 38 (6 November 2026)

Footnote 38 (annotating "Before the Veil") says Akiba and Aher visited Paradise, that Aher "took branches from the Tree of Knowledge," and "See 2d Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter xii." Installed [[2 Corinthians 012#^kjv-2co-12-2|2 Corinthians 12:2]]-[[2 Corinthians 012#^kjv-2co-12-4|12:4]] describe an ascent to the third heaven and paradise; they do not mention the Tree of Knowledge.

- Tier: explicit citation of that chapter; the tree-branch detail is her overlay (or that of the midrash she is using).
- Limit: Do not treat Paul as narrating a theft from the Edenic tree, and do not merge Tree of Knowledge with this hub's other sources without their own locators.

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapters III–IV (6 November 2026)

On [[08 Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_85|page 85]] she says scientists now use their sceptre “like the cherubim and flaming sword of Eden, to keep the people away from the tree of immortal life.” That is rhetorical use of the Eden image, not a printed verse citation (see the review queue). Footnote 172 reports C. B. Warring identifying Peruvian bark with the paradisiacal Tree of Life; she does not adopt the identification.

Chapter IV quotes Genesis vi (including “mighty men which were of old; men of renown,” footnote 223: Genesis vi. 4) beside a Hindu descent of Brahmans from “sons of God” and a giant's daughter ([[09 Chapter IV Theories Respecting Psychic Phenomena#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_122|pages 122-123]]). Installed [[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-1|Genesis 6:1]]–[[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-4|6:4]] match those sentences. This is a comparative-myth use of the same chapter already checked against footnote 100, not a repeat of that overlay about the flood's purpose.

- Tier: `explicit citation` of Genesis 6:4; the flaming-sword sentence is editorial resemblance only.
- Limit: Do not treat Warring's cinchona identification as hers, and do not rewrite this hub as if she were commenting on Bullinger's Hercules “tree of life.”
