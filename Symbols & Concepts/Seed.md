# Seed

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

Something small that already holds what will grow from it. The sources split over what the seed is: for Gaskell and Hall it is a divine germ latent in the soul or in matter; for Bullinger it is one promised person, the "Seed of the woman" of Genesis 3:15, and secondarily Abraham's posterity. Blavatsky uses the plant sense for her own purposes and flatly denies the messianic reading. The serpent half of Genesis 3:15 is on [[Symbols & Concepts/Serpent|Serpent]].

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entries: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/S/SEED|SEED]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_672|page_672]]) and [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/S/SEED, GOOD|SEED, GOOD]] (page_672–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_673|page_673]]).

Short definitions (OCR exact):

> A symbol of the manifesting God, the source of all things.

> A symbol of the germs of the higher emotions arising from buddhi.

The SEED body is mostly quotation: Baur on the Docetae (OCR `Docetsae`) picturing God as a seed from which the world grew like a fig-tree, William Law on "a seed of Christ" in every man, and R. J. Campbell's oak-in-the-acorn image. SEED, GOOD glosses the sower parable ("The seed is the word of God", printed as LUKE viii. 12-15): the wayside hearers are qualities still ruled by the desire-nature, and Christ is the Archetypal Man who "dies in involution" and rises in evolution (OCR `cvolution`).

Neighbouring entries: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/S/SOWER OF SEED|SOWER OF SEED]] (the Higher Self planting germs of higher qualities), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/S/SPERMATIC WORDS|SPERMATIC WORDS]] (monads of life and form descending into matter), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/F/FIG-TREE|FIG-TREE]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/T/TREE OF LIFE|TREE OF LIFE]]. Gaskell quotes Genesis 3:15 under [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/H/HEEL|HEEL]], not under SEED, and reads it there as a conflict inside the soul; see [[Studies/Bible–Gaskell Cross References/Bridges/heel__genesis-3-15]].

## King James Bible

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 003#^kjv-gen-3-15|Genesis 3:15]] — "thy seed and her seed"; Bullinger's base text, quoted by Gaskell (HEEL) and twice by Blavatsky.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 015#^kjv-gen-15-5|Genesis 15:5]] and [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 022#^kjv-gen-22-17|Genesis 22:17]] — Abraham's seed as the stars; cited by Bullinger.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/04 Numbers/Numbers 024#^kjv-num-24-7|Numbers 24:7]] — "his seed shall be in many waters"; Bullinger, Aquarius.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/42 Luke/Luke 008#^kjv-luk-8-11|Luke 8:11]] — "The seed is the word of God"; the sentence Gaskell quotes in SEED, GOOD (his printed locator is viii. 12-15).
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/43 John/John 012#^kjv-jhn-12-24|John 12:24]] — the corn of wheat that dies and bears fruit; cited by Bullinger in Capricornus.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001#^kjv-gen-1-11|Genesis 1:11]] — "whose seed is in itself"; attached to a Gaskell entry by a bridge note (not opened for this hub).
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/48 Galatians/Galatians 003#^kjv-gal-3-29|Galatians 3:29]] — "Abraham's seed, and heirs"; likewise a Gaskell bridge, not opened here.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Theory and Practice of Alchemy Part One#^hall-stota-pdf-484|PDF 484]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Theory and Practice of Alchemy Part One#^hall-stota-pdf-485|485]] — alchemy is growth, not creation from nothing: "in every stone is the seed of man", and God "is the spiritual seed planted in the dark earth (the material universe)."
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Theory and Practice of Alchemy Part One#^hall-stota-pdf-486|PDF 486]] — the axiom "Within everything is the seed of everything."
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Flowers, Plants, Fruits,and Trees#^hall-stota-pdf-285|PDF 285]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Flowers, Plants, Fruits,and Trees#^hall-stota-pdf-286|286]] — the universe as a tree from a single seed sown in space; grain as an emblem "of the human spermatozoon"; the oak in the acorn.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Life and Teachings of Thoth Hermes Trismegistus#^hall-stota-pdf-105|PDF 105]] — in Hall's retelling of the Hermetic vision the Milky Way is "the seed-ground of souls".
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Cross and the Crucifixion#^hall-stota-pdf-602|PDF 602]] — from the *Golden Legend*: three seeds from the Tree of Life laid in dead Adam's mouth grow into the tree later used for the cross. Hall reports the legend.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/05 Chapter I The Sign VIRGO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00006|Virgo]] — Genesis 3:15 is "the commencement of all prophecy"; the whole zodiac unfolds it. The Coma section ([[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/05 Chapter I The Sign VIRGO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00007|here]]) makes the Seed "a child born, a son given".
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/06 Chapter II The Sign LIBRA#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00010|Libra]] — the Seed comes "as an atoning sacrifice".
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/07 Chapter III The Sign SCORPIO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00014|Scorpio]] — the enmity traced through attempts on "the seed of Abraham", "all the seed royal", and the babes of Bethlehem.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/12 Chapter III The Sign PISCES#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00030|Pisces]] and [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/17 Chapter III The Sign CANCER#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00048|Cancer]] — the collective sense: Abraham's multiplied seed, which "shall possess the gate of his enemies".

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/05 Chapter I The Church Where Is It#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_50|Vol. 2, p. 50]] — her own voice: in Genesis 3:15 "there is not the slightest allusion to a Redeemer".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/14 Chapter IX The Vedas and the Bible#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_446|Vol. 2, p. 446]] — her own voice: a plate of Christna crushing the serpent shows the source of "a later story extracted from an alleged prophecy".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/05 Chapter I The Church Where Is It#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_44|Vol. 2, p. 44]] — her account of the Mysteries: Ceres as bread means "regeneration of life from the seed".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/10 Chapter V The Ether or Astral Light#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_152|Vol. 1, p. 152]] — her own argument: bud and seed imply a pre-existing germ, so evolution presupposes one for the human race.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/09 Chapter V Mysteries of the Kabala#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_226|Vol. 2, p. 226]] — reporting Manu: the waters develop "a productive seed" that becomes the golden egg of Brahma.

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Direct disagreement | Bullinger builds everything on Genesis 3:15 as messianic prophecy; Blavatsky denies the verse says so | She is not answering Bullinger, whose book is later; her target is church exegesis generally |
| Same verse, different object | Gaskell also quotes Genesis 3:15 but reads an inner struggle of higher and lower nature | It sits under HEEL; his SEED entries do not use the verse |
| Editorial resemblance | Gaskell (quoting Campbell) and Hall both use the oak in the acorn for latent divinity | Hall does not cite Gaskell or Campbell; a common image, not a shared source |
| Individual versus collective | Bullinger keeps both a singular Seed (Virgo) and a multitude (Pisces, Cancer) | Gaskell's and Hall's "seed in every man" is a third thing; do not merge it with Abraham's posterity |
