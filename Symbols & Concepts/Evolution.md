# Evolution

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

In this vault the word seldom means Darwin's theory alone. For Gaskell it is the upward half of a two-part process: the emerging of what was first involved in matter (see [[Symbols & Concepts/Involution|Involution]]). Hall and Blavatsky likewise speak of a spiritual evolution and set it beside or against the physical one, though Blavatsky often uses “evolution” for the downward movement too. Bullinger uses the word once, to reject the idea that Bible truth evolved from older religions. The King James Bible does not use the word.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/E/EVOLUTION AND INVOLUTION|EVOLUTION AND INVOLUTION]] (`gaskell-1960-entry-evolution-and-involution`; [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_263|page_263]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_264|page_264]]). The entry is headed for both terms; there is no separate EVOLUTION headword.

Machine-selected short definition (OCR exact):

> Evolution is the gradual emerging into objective activity of qualities and faculties which have been previously involved to perfection in the matter of the lower planes.

Most of the body is quotation. It opens with Blavatsky's *Secret Doctrine* (Vol. II, p. 190; the OCR prints the name as `Buavarsxy`) on the Monad's descending and ascending arcs, with Spirit and Matter balanced in Man at the middle. Gaskell's own gloss follows: when the soul is fully involved, Spirit and Matter are equilibrised “in the Archetypal Man, the Adam (Kadmon) of Genesis i.” Then come R. J. Campbell (“You cannot have evolution without something to evolve”), Ueberweg on Erigena, Kingsford and Maitland, Ladd and Drummond.

The companion entry is [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/I/INVOLUTION TION|INVOLUTION TION]], which treats the two processes as descent and ascent of Spirit. Other relevant neighbours: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/I/INCARNATION OF SPIRIT|INCARNATION OF SPIRIT]] (“the point where evolution commences”), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CIRCLE OF EXISTENCE|CIRCLE OF EXISTENCE]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/A/ARCHETYPAL MAN|ARCHETYPAL MAN]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/A/ADAM (HIGHER ASPECT)|ADAM (HIGHER ASPECT)]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/F/FALL OF MAN|FALL OF MAN]].

## King James Bible

The word “evolution” does not occur in the King James Bible. The entry names “Genesis i.” and uses scriptural phrases but prints no chapter-and-verse locator, so no verses are listed here.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Atlantis and the Godsof Antiquity#^hall-stota-pdf-85|PDF 85]] — the sinking of Atlantis and the biblical fall of man both “signify spiritual involution – a prerequisite to conscious evolution.”
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Stones, Metals and Gems#^hall-stota-pdf-294|PDF 294]] — the Deucalion and Pyrrha story epitomises “the mystery of human evolution”: spirit, ensouling matter, raises mineral to plant, plant to animal, animal to man, and man “to the estate of the gods.”
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Human Body in Symbolism#^hall-stota-pdf-227|PDF 227]] — “According to the secret doctrine,” man is refining his vehicles and disentangling himself from matter; the trend of his evolutionary growth “is ever toward his own essential Selfhood.”
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Ancient Mysteries and Secret Societies Part Three#^hall-stota-pdf-79|PDF 79]] — in the Bacchic myth, smoke rising from the burned Titans signifies the soul's ascent; evolution is the soul rising “from the divinely consumed material mass.”

Hall does not cite Gaskell.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

One use, and it is a denial. It is “popularly believed that Bible truth is an evolution from, or development of, the ancient religions of the world,” but for Bullinger those religions are “a corruption and perversion of primitive truth” ([[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/03 Introduction#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00004|Introduction]]).

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

She speaks in her own voice throughout, attributing the doctrine to ancient philosophers and kabalists.

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/05 Before the Veil#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_xxx|Vol. 1, page xxx]] — her glossary entry: modern science holds “but to a one-sided physical evolution,” ignoring “the higher or spiritual evolution.” On [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/05 Before the Veil#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_xxxi|page xxxi]]: for the ancients evolution began “from pure spirit,” which descended until it became matter.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/10 Chapter V The Ether or Astral Light#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_154|Vol. 1, page 154]] — the pyramid shows that evolution “originally begun from above and proceeded downward, instead of the reverse, as taught in the Darwinian theory”.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/10 Chapter VI Esoteric Doctrines of Buddhism Parodied in Christianity#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_277|Vol. 2, page 277]] — the fall of Adam is not a personal transgression “but simply the law of the dual evolution”; the coats of skin are given by “the eternal law of Evolution or necessarianism.”

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Explicit citation | Gaskell's entry quotes Blavatsky by name on the descending and ascending arcs | The work quoted is *The Secret Doctrine*, not *Isis Unveiled*; the vault holds only the latter |
| Terminology | Gaskell and Hall (PDF 85) name the descent “involution” and keep “evolution” for the ascent | In *Isis Unveiled* Blavatsky usually calls both arcs evolution (“dual evolution”); she uses “involution” once |
| Editorial resemblance | Hall's mineral-to-god ladder and return to Selfhood resemble Gaskell's emerging of involved qualities | Hall does not cite Gaskell; resemblance is not influence |
| Contrast | Bullinger rejects an evolutionary account of religion and holds to a primitive revelation later corrupted | He is answering a different question and says nothing here about spirit and matter |
