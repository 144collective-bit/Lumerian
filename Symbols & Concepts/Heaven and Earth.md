# Heaven and Earth

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

The two terms used together as one symbol: a first duality, and whatever joins or separates its poles. Gaskell reads the pair as Spirit and Matter. Hall and Blavatsky also use it for two poles, and add images of a link between them (a string, a ladder, a chain, a pyramid's apex). Bullinger hardly uses the pair as a symbol at all; for him the words name the literal sky and ground. For each term alone see [[Symbols & Concepts/Heaven|Heaven]] and [[Symbols & Concepts/Earth|Earth]].

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/H/HEAVEN AND EARTH|HEAVEN AND EARTH]] (`page_353`–`page_354`).

Machine-selected short definition (OCR exact):

> Symbols of Spirit and Matter,— the life and the form sides of manifestation.

The body gathers parallels: Bruno on two substances, a Chinese note on heaven as Yang and earth as Yin, Varro on active and passive divinities. It then reads Genesis 1:1–2 as Spirit and Matter being differentiated from "that which is iteelf neither" (OCR for *itself*). The Chinese Pwan-koo and the opening of the Nihongi, where a chaotic egg parts into a lighter heaven and a heavier earth, are read the same way; "divine beings were produced between them". Other OCR slips include `onl two substances`.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Pythagorean Theory of Music and Color#^hall-stota-pdf-246|PDF 246]] — reporting Pythagoras: the universe is a monochord with one end fixed to absolute spirit and the other to absolute matter, "a cord stretched between heaven and earth".
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Fifteen Rosicrucian and Qabbalistic Diagrams#^hall-stota-pdf-454|PDF 454]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Fifteen Rosicrucian and Qabbalistic Diagrams#^hall-stota-pdf-455|455]] — describing alchemical plates: the First Day of Creation is "the separation of Schamayim and Ares" (upright and inverted triangles), and a globe on the creation diagram carries the words Heaven and Earth.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Mystery of the Apocalypse#^hall-stota-pdf-623|PDF 623]] — when the fiery element has consumed and purified the other three they come forth as "a new heaven and a new earth".
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Conclusion#^hall-stota-pdf-667|PDF 667]] — philosophy "connects heaven and earth like a mighty ladder"; Hall's own image.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

Bullinger has almost nothing on the pair as a symbol. The phrase occurs once in the passages gathered, in a hymn closing the book's last sign: "Bid heaven and earth Thy glory know" ([[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/18 Chapter IV The Sign LEO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00054|Leo]]). His working contrast is literal: heaven is where the signs are set and from where Christ returns; earth is what he comes to judge and restore. Those uses are on the [[Symbols & Concepts/Heaven|Heaven]] and [[Symbols & Concepts/Earth|Earth]] hubs.

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/05 Chapter I The Church Where Is It#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_36|vol. 2, p. 36]] — reporting Beausobre and Augustine, then giving "the esoteric interpretation": heaven and earth were not the first works but came through the Principle, the Logos or Wisdom.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/10 Chapter VI Esoteric Doctrines of Buddhism Parodied in Christianity#^blavatsky-isis-v2-hebrew9|vol. 2, ch. VI]] — her own exposition of the kabalistic cosmogony: "Heaven and Earth are personified by Adam Kadmon and the second Adam."
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/10 Chapter VI Esoteric Doctrines of Buddhism Parodied in Christianity#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_268|vol. 2, p. 268]] — her account of a "secret" Hindu triad: Nara, Father-Heaven; Nari, Mother-Earth; Viradj, the Son or Universe. Spirit, under the symbol of Heaven, fecundates Matter.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/14 Chapter IX The Vedas and the Bible#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_454|vol. 2, p. 454]] — she quotes a Christian phrase about the star "which joined heaven and earth" and prefers the axiom "as above, so below".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/10 Chapter V The Ether or Astral Light#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_154|vol. 1, p. 154]] — her own reading: the Pyramid's apex is "the mystic link between heaven and earth".

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Gaskell and Hall (PDF 246) | Both equate the pair with spirit and matter as two poles | Hall is reporting Pythagoras and does not cite Gaskell |
| Gaskell and Blavatsky (p. 268) | Both give a male heaven and female earth, with Spirit acting on Matter | Blavatsky's scheme is a triad with a Son; Gaskell's parallels are Chinese, Japanese and Roman. No mutual citation found |
| Genesis 1:1 | Gaskell reads it as the first differentiation; Blavatsky argues over "in the beginning" as the Principle | Different questions: what the pair is, and through whom it was made |
| Linking images | Hall's cord, ladder and chain and Blavatsky's apex put something between the poles; Gaskell's entry also has beings "between them" | The resemblance is editorial. Each image belongs to its own passage |
| Bullinger against the rest | The pair is literal sky and ground | He supplies no symbolic reading to compare |
