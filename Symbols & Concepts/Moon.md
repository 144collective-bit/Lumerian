# Moon

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

The moon is the dependent light: it shines by the sun, changes, and governs night, tides and months. Gaskell turns that into three separate inner meanings (buddhi, the astral plane, the personality). Hall and Blavatsky read it as the feminine or generative power and as a station of the soul. Bullinger reads it as a literal luminary that keeps time and bears witness. The split is between an inner symbol and a created body.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Gaskell has no single MOON entry. He gives three aspects and two period entries, and they do not add up to one definition.

- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/M/MOON (HIGHER ASPECT)|MOON (HIGHER ASPECT)]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_516|page_516]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_517|page_517]]). OCR exact: "A symbol of Buddhi as containing the transmuted results of the experience of the lower self." The body rests on an Upanishad passage in which the departing person passes wind, sun and moon in turn (OCR `mounte`).
- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/M/MOON (LOWER ASPECT)|MOON (LOWER ASPECT)]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_517|page_517]]). OCR exact: "A symbol of the astral plane, the plane of the desires by means of which the soul ascends." The body interprets a Manichaean account from the Disputation of Archelaus: sun and moon are "passage boats" for souls, and twelve urns revolve with the sphere. Gaskell reads waxing as desire growing and waning as mind taking control.
- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/M/MOON (SOLAR ASPECT)|MOON (SOLAR ASPECT)]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_517|page_517]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_518|page_518]]). OCR exact: "A symbol of the lower self—the Personality." The moon is the lower self drawn toward the sun, the Higher Self. He quotes a sermon: "We are not suns, we are moons". OCR has `aun` for "sun".
- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/M/MOON-WAXING PERIOD, OR THE PITRIYANA|MOON—WAXING PERIOD, OR THE PITRIYANA]] and [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/M/MOON-WANING PERIOD, OR THE DEVAYANA|MOON—WANING PERIOD, OR THE DEVAYANA]] apply the lower aspect to two periods of human evolution.

Neighbouring entries: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/L/LUNAR CYCLE OF MANIFESTATION|LUNAR CYCLE OF MANIFESTATION]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/S/SOMA, AS THE MOON|SOMA, AS THE MOON]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/S/SUN, MOON, AND STARS|SUN, MOON, AND STARS]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/U/URNS, THE TWELVE|URNS, THE TWELVE]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/D/DIANA, OR ARTEMIS, GODDESS OF LIGHT AND THE MOON|DIANA, OR ARTEMIS, GODDESS OF LIGHT AND THE MOON]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CROWN OF TWELVE STARS|CROWN OF TWELVE STARS]].

No Bible bridge is recorded for the five MOON entries themselves.

## King James Bible

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/66 Revelation/Revelation 012#^kjv-rev-12-1|Revelation 12:1]] — the woman with "the moon under her feet"; bridged from Gaskell's CROWN OF TWELVE STARS, and alluded to by Hall (PDF 129).
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/46 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 015#^kjv-1co-15-41|1 Corinthians 15:41]] — "another glory of the moon"; bridged from a Gaskell star entry.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 037#^kjv-gen-37-9|Genesis 37:9]] — Joseph's dream of sun, moon and eleven stars; cited in Bullinger's Introduction.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/19 Psalms/Psalms 136#^kjv-psa-136-9|Psalms 136:9]] — moon and stars "to rule by night"; cited in Bullinger's footnote 2.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Isis, the Virginof the World#^hall-stota-pdf-129|Isis, the Virgin of the World, PDF 129]] — Isis is lunar but her power is "due to the sun’s rays"; as the moon wears reflected light, so Isis, "like the virgin of Revelation", is clothed in solar glory.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Zodiac and Its Signs#^hall-stota-pdf-152|The Zodiac and Its Signs, PDF 152]] — Cancer is "the house of the Moon, the great Mother of all things"; he quotes Richard Payne Knight on the moon as ruler of moisture and generation.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/An Analysis of Tarot Cards#^hall-stota-pdf-406|An Analysis of Tarot Cards, PDF 406]] — the eighteenth trump, La Lune. He reports Court de Gébelin's Nile reading, then adds his own: the card shows the path of wisdom, with man emerging "from the pool of illusion".

Hall does not cite Gaskell.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

Bullinger gives the moon no hidden meaning. It is one of the created lights whose motions measure time.

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/20 For Signs and For Seasons#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00056|For Signs and For Seasons]] — Genesis 1:14 means the sun, moon and stars are for "signs (things to come)" and "cycles (appointed times)"; he then works through the cycles of sun and moon.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/22 Footnotes#^bullinger-wots-fn-2|Footnote 2]] — the vocabulary of Psalm 19 is tied to the ruling of sun and moon in Genesis 1:18 and Psalm 136.

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/13 Chapter VIII Some Mysteries of Nature#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_264|Vol. 1, page 264]] — her own voice: the moon "shines only by the reflected light of the sun", hence the myth of Diana as Jupiter's daughter. She treats the myth as encoded natural observation.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/10 Chapter VI Esoteric Doctrines of Buddhism Parodied in Christianity#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_284|Vol. 2, page 284]] — quoting Plutarch at length: earth gives the body, "the moon the soul, and the sun the understanding"; a second death takes place in the moon. The doctrine is Plutarch's; she presents it as ancient teaching.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/05 Chapter I The Church Where Is It#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_48|Vol. 2, page 48]] — her own inference: the Khaldi worshipped a Moon-god whose trinity was the three lunar phases, the waning being the god's death.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/05 Chapter I The Church Where Is It#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_49|Vol. 2, page 49]] — quoting Draper: the image of Isis "standing on the crescent moon" returned as the Madonna.

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Reflected light | Gaskell (solar aspect), Hall (PDF 129) and Blavatsky (page 264) all build on the moon borrowing the sun's light | They apply it differently: personality, Isis as Nature, the Diana myth. No mutual citation |
| The moon and the soul's journey | Gaskell's lower aspect (Manichaean boats) and higher aspect (Upanishad), and Blavatsky's Plutarch, each make the moon a stage for souls | Gaskell maps it to planes (astral, buddhi); Plutarch to the parts of man. The schemes do not match |
| Mother and generation | Hall (PDF 152) calls the moon Mother of all things; Gaskell's lower aspect speaks of egos nurtured by "their mother the moon" | Hall means Nature's life forces; Gaskell means the astral plane |
| Bullinger against the rest | For Bullinger the moon is a literal timekeeper under Genesis 1:14 | A difference of kind: he does not answer the symbolic readings |
