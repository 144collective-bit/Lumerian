# Serpent

This is a **shared research hub**, not a universal definition. Each reading stays attributed to its source. Gaskell dictionary text is `machine-extracted-unreviewed` OCR unless a note says otherwise. Cross-source comments below are **editorial comparison** unless labelled as an explicit citation. Shared wording or a similar symbol is not evidence that one author used or influenced the other.


## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

A dual or many-valued symbol in this vault: wisdom and desire, healer and accuser, brazen sign and cursed beast. Gaskell himself insists on higher and lower serpents. Do not treat every “serpent” or “dragon” hit as the same figure.

## From E. W. Bullinger, The Witness of the Stars (6 November 2026)

Genesis 3:15 is the plot of the book: the Seed bruised in the heel who bruises the serpent’s head ([[Genesis 003#^kjv-gen-3-15|Genesis 3:15]]). He restates that conflict in Serpens and Ophiuchus, Hercules (lifted heel, foot on the dragon), Draco ([[Revelation 012#^kjv-rev-12-9|Revelation 12:9]]), Hydra (“put away”), and Cetus bound. Source doors: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/07 Chapter III The Sign SCORPIO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00015|Serpens and Ophiuchus]], [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/08 Chapter IV The Sign SAGITTARIUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00020|Draco]], [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/18 Chapter IV The Sign LEO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00052|Hydra]], [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/19 Summary|Summary]] (he refuses a Marian foot as the crusher).

- Tier: explicit citation of Gen 3:15 and Rev 12:9 as he uses them; source-specific interpretation of the figures; contrast with Gaskell’s many serpent meanings and Hall’s initiatory serpent.
- Limit: Do not flatten those other entries into “serpent = Satan.”
- See [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

Footnote 43 records the Greek fable that names Ophiuchus Aesculapius, son of Apollo, with the serpent as the enduring symbol of the medical art; Bullinger calls that a perversion of the primitive truth that the Coming One, in overcoming the serpent, should become the great healer. That is an editorial comparison with Gaskell's `SERPENT ASSCULAPIUS` entry (Higher Self as divine healer), not a citation either way. He also calls Hydra the "female serpent," reads Cetus as Leviathan, and treats the variable stars Algol and Mira as emblems of an enemy who shifts appearance.

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter IV (6 November 2026)

She quotes the Catholic polemicist des Mousseaux: without the Devil's “speech which issued out of the mouth of the Serpent, *his medium*,” there would have been no Fall and the Cross would be “the most ridiculous of supernumeraries”; “The Devil is the chief pillar of Faith” ([[09 Chapter IV Theories Respecting Psychic Phenomena#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_103|page 103]]). She reports this as the church's echo, not as her own soteriology. The idiom she applies to that devil, “a liar and murderer from the beginning” (page 101), matches [[John 008#^kjv-jhn-8-44|John 8:44]] (no verse number printed).

- Tier: attributed quotation of a Catholic writer; editorial match for John 8:44. Contrast of frames with Bullinger's Genesis 3:15 Seed-versus-serpent plot already on this hub — different speakers and questions.
- Limit: Do not record this as her identification of the Eden serpent, and do not promote the still-queued Vedic *Sarpa-rajni*.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter IV Theories Respecting Psychic Phenomena - Analytical Note|Chapter IV analytical note]].
