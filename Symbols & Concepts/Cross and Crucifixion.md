# Cross and Crucifixion

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

A figure (the cross) and an event-pattern (crucifixion) that the vault’s sources treat both historically and cosmically. Do not collapse “the Latin cross,” “the Tau / crux ansata,” and “the crucifixion of Jesus” into one meaning.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CROSS|CROSS]] (`gaskell-1960-entry-cross`; [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_194|page_194]]).

Machine-selected short definition (OCR exact):

> A symbol of the manifested life of the Logos—the higher and lower natures, and the Divine Ray passing through the quaternary.

The same OCR body retells the Golden Legend tradition that the cross was of four woods and that its upright beam grew from a branch of the Tree of Life given to Seth. Gaskell internally See-references [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/T/TREE OF LIFE|TREE OF LIFE]].

## From E. W. Bullinger, The Witness of the Stars (6 November 2026)

In LIBRA’s decans he reads the southern constellation CRUX as a picture of Christ’s cross, immediately followed by Corona (the crown). Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/06 Chapter II The Sign LIBRA#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00011|CRUX]]. Plate 6 in the EPUB was visually inspected on this pass: four stars drawn as a Latin cross, labelled “VI. CRUX (the Cross).” He cites [[John 005#^kjv-jhn-5-24|John 5:24]] among related “finished” / life-from-death texts.

- Tier: source-specific interpretation (the plate is visually a drawn cross; the Gospel identification is his). Contrast / editorial comparison with Gaskell’s quaternary or Logos-ray cross and Hall’s pagan-antecedent / LVX discussion.
- Limit: His claim that the Cross was once visible at Jerusalem’s latitude, and the *Tau* etymology, stay his astronomical-historical claims.
- See [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

He glosses a name *Adom* as "cut off" ([[Daniel 009#^kjv-dan-9-26|Daniel 9:26]]) and cites [[John 019#^kjv-jhn-19-30|John 19:30]] ("It is finished"). He says that in earliest times Egypt used the cross as a sign and symbol of life, and that Auriga's Denderah sceptre ends in a cross. That Egyptian "cross = life" notice is an editorial resemblance (not identity) to Gaskell's `CROSS, ANKH (CRUX ANSATA), A SYMBOL OF LIFE`; the frames differ (historical passion and a constellation vs an interior symbol).
