# Jesus

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

Three of the four interpretive sources here separate "Jesus" from "Christ" and disagree about what is left on the Jesus side: for Gaskell a nearly perfected soul, for Hall a teacher confused with the principle he preached, for Blavatsky a reformer and adept overlaid with older myth. Bullinger refuses the separation: Jesus is the promised Seed in person. The principle is on [[Symbols & Concepts/Christ|Christ]]; this hub holds the name.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS OF THE GOSPELS|JESUS OF THE GOSPELS]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_414|page_414]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_415|page_415]]). Short definition, OCR exact:

> A symbol of the indwelling Higher Self (Christ), or Divine Spirit in man, evolving towards complete manifestation.

The body calls this "the Divinised Soul, or Christ-soul, not yet but almost perfected", and admits that the symbol "varies somewhat between the exalted Christ and the human, unperfected Christsoul" (OCR spelling). Pilate is read as the worldly mind questioning the higher nature.

**How this differs from Gaskell's CHRIST.** [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CHRIST|CHRIST]] opens "Christ, or the Higher Self": the Divine Life itself, proceeding from the Absolute. "Jesus" is that Life as it is found inside a soul still being perfected. The clearest statement is in [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS JESUS WEPT|JESUS JESUS WEPT]] (headword doubled by OCR): "the humanity in 'Jesus' was stirred", but "The Christ, however, did not weep." [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS, MORTAL|JESUS, MORTAL]] likewise defines Jesus as the "almost perfected personality, subject to the death of its lower nature".

Other entries: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS AS SON OF GOD|JESUS AS SON OF GOD]] (Higher Self on the buddhi-mental plane), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS AS SON OF MAN|JESUS AS SON OF MAN]] (Higher Self "as born or evolved in the mind"), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS AS THE LAMB|JESUS AS THE LAMB]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS, DESCRIBED AS SEVERE AND TURBULENT|JESUS, DESCRIBED AS SEVERE AND TURBULENT]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS THE PATTERN OR EXEMPLAR|JESUS THE PATTERN OR EXEMPLAR]] (a quotation from Stainton Moses only), and [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS JESUS THY SERVANT|JESUS JESUS THY SERVANT]], where Gaskell calls "Jesus" a generic name.

Neighbours: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/B/BIRTH OF JESUS|BIRTH OF JESUS]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/T/TEMPTATION OF JESUS|TEMPTATION OF JESUS]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CRUCIFIXION OF JESUS, ACCORDING TO THE CANONICAL GOSPELS|CRUCIFIXION OF JESUS]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/G/GOSPEL STORY OF CHRIST|GOSPEL STORY OF CHRIST]].

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-590|PDF 590]] — the "true story of the life of Jesus of Nazareth has never been unfolded" in the Gospels or the Apocrypha.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Mystic Christianity#^hall-stota-pdf-593|PDF 593]] — his own claim: "Jesus has been confused with the Christos, or god-man, whose wonders He preached."
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Fishes, Insects, Animals, Reptiles and Birds Part One#^hall-stota-pdf-257|PDF 257]] and [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Ancient Mysteries and Secret Societies Part Two#^hall-stota-pdf-60|PDF 60]] — reporting the Gnostics: Jesus a mortal man, Christos the Nous that used his body from the baptism and left it before the crucifixion.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Sun,A Universal Deity#^hall-stota-pdf-137|PDF 137]] — the chroniclers turned him into a solar deity and "the historical Jesus was forgotten".

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/05 Chapter I The Sign VIRGO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00007|Coma]] — he quotes Albumazar on the infant in the virgin's arms, "by some nations called IHESU".
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/06 Chapter II The Sign LIBRA#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00012|Lupus, the Victim]] — the slain animal prefigures "a silent, willing sacrifice—Christ Jesus".
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/06 Chapter II The Sign LIBRA#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00013|Corona]] — the crown that follows: "at the name of Jesus every knee should bow".
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/10 Chapter I The Sign CAPRICORNUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00022|Capricornus]] — the sun was in this sign, and the nights longest, "when Jesus was born".

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/08 Chapter IV Oriental Cosmogonies and Bible Records#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_186|Vol. 2, page 186]] — reporting the Ophite system, partly in quotation: "Christos entered into the *man* Jesus at the moment of his baptism" and left him on the cross.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/09 Chapter V Mysteries of the Kabala#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_239|Vol. 2, page 239]] — her own argument: she lines up New Testament texts that call Jesus "a man".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/11 Chapter VII Early Christian Heresies and Secret Societies#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_339|Vol. 2, page 339]] — her own voice: the "mythical birth and life of Jesus" copy Christna's, while his historical character as reformer is "the true type of Buddha".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/18 Chapter XI Comparative Results of Buddhism and Christianity#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_574|Vol. 2, page 574]] — her own reading of Paul: Christ is "not a person, but an embodied idea". She credits Wilder for the claim that Paul, not Jesus, founded Christianity.

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Jesus / Christ split | Gaskell, Hall and Blavatsky each distinguish the name from the principle | Gaskell's split is inside the soul (personality and Higher Self); Hall's and Blavatsky's is between a man and a doctrine about him |
| Gaskell and Hall | Both speak of a divine Self in every man that the Gospel figure represents | Hall posits a historical teacher behind the story; Gaskell's entries read every incident as soul-process. Hall does not cite Gaskell |
| Hall and Blavatsky on the Gnostics | Both report Christos entering Jesus at the baptism | Both are relaying Gnostic teaching (Hall through King's citation of Irenaeus), not stating it as their own creed |
| Bullinger against the three | For Bullinger Jesus is the Seed, the Lamb and the coming King in one person | His star-readings cannot be borrowed as support for an inner-Christ reading, or the reverse |
