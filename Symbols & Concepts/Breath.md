# Breath

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

Life or spirit pictured as air moving out from a source. Gaskell, Hall's Qabbalistic and Hermetic texts, and Blavatsky all treat breath as divine emanation, whether cosmic (the universe breathed out) or individual (the spark breathed into man). The split is over its worth: Gaskell's breath of life is immortal, one Hall passage speaks of a perishable "breath of form", and in the Bible verses Bullinger quotes breath is either human frailty or the fire of judgment.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Three entries share [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_133|page_133]]: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/B/BREATH|BREATH]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/B/BREATH OF LIFE|BREATH OF LIFE]] and [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/B/BREATH, DIVINE, OUTBREATHING AND INBREATHING|BREATH, DIVINE, OUTBREATHING AND INBREATHING]].

Short definitions (OCR exact; the stray space before the comma in the second is OCR):

> A symbol of the mental interpretation of Spirit, or the expression of the Divine on the mental plane.

> A symbol of the spiritual essence ,— the Divine Spark, atma-buddhi, which is immortal.

> Symbolic of spiritual action from the Supreme in the rhythmic forthpouring and withdrawing of the Divine Life of the Invisible and visible universe in successive spirations.

BREATH has no body beyond three See-references. BREATH OF LIFE quotes Genesis 2:7 and reads it as the Divine spark projected into the lower mind. BREATH, DIVINE quotes the Vendidad and two Upanishads ("Breath is the life of all beings") and identifies the Divine Breath with "the Holy Spirit of Wisdom"; its last sentences are broken by an intruding running head (`BRIARE US`).

Neighbouring entries: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/P/PRANA (BREATH OR LIFE)|PRANA (BREATH OR LIFE)]] (the Higher Self or Divine Life in all things), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/W/WIND, WORD, SPIRIT, MARUT, BREATH|WIND, WORD, SPIRIT, MARUT, BREATH]] ("Symbols of emanation"), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/H/HOLY GHOST, OR SPIRIT|HOLY GHOST, OR SPIRIT]] ("the Breath of the Atman"), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/I/INSPIRATION, DIVINE|INSPIRATION, DIVINE]], and [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/N/NOSE AND NOSTRILS BREATHING|NOSE AND NOSTRILS BREATHING]] (volition and freewill; the headword and definition are OCR-damaged).

## King James Bible

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 002#^kjv-gen-2-7|Genesis 2:7]] — "the breath of life"; quoted by Gaskell ([[Studies/Bible–Gaskell Cross References/Bridges/breath-of-life__genesis-2-7]]) and by Blavatsky.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/19 Psalms/Psalms 146#^kjv-psa-146-4|Psalms 146:4]] — "His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth"; a Gaskell bridge from another entry (not opened here).
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/19 Psalms/Psalms 062#^kjv-psa-62-9|Psalms 62:9]] — the KJV prints "vanity"; Bullinger quotes the Revised Version and glosses it "Heb. a breath".
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/23 Isaiah/Isaiah 030#^kjv-isa-30-28|Isaiah 30:28]] and [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/23 Isaiah/Isaiah 030#^kjv-isa-30-33|30:33]] — the LORD's breath as an overflowing stream and as brimstone; quoted by Bullinger.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/43 John/John 003#^kjv-jhn-3-8|John 3:8]] — wind and Spirit; reaches Gaskell's WIND entry through a quotation from Cruden. The verse says "wind", not "breath".

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Qabbalah, the Secret Doctrine of Israel#^hall-stota-pdf-353|PDF 353]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Qabbalah, the Secret Doctrine of Israel#^hall-stota-pdf-354|354]] — in the Sepher Yetzirah text Hall prints, the letter Aleph is "a reconciling breath" between mute water and hissing fire, and the twenty-two letters are "formed by the voice and impressed upon the air by the breath."
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Life and Teachings of Thoth Hermes Trismegistus#^hall-stota-pdf-101|PDF 101]] — in his retelling of the Hermetic vision: "The Word moving like a breath through space called forth the Fire".
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Qabbalistic Keys to the Creation of Man#^hall-stota-pdf-393|PDF 393]] — the cherubim tell the Adamic man that what was breathed into him "was the breath of form" and will flicker out like a flame. The speech is theirs; Adam's reply rejects the verdict.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Fishes, Insects, Animals, Reptiles and Birds Part One#^hall-stota-pdf-264|PDF 264]] — Hall quotes *Isis Unveiled* by name on the Spirit of God whose breath incubated cosmic matter into the serpent ring.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Fishes, Insects, Animals, Reptiles and Birds Part Two#^hall-stota-pdf-269|PDF 269]] — "Birds were used to signify the vital breath".

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

Bullinger has no symbolism of breath. The word comes in with scripture he quotes:

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/06 Chapter II The Sign LIBRA#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00010|Libra]] — men weighed in the balances are "vanity (Heb. a breath)"; breath here means weightlessness, the deficient price.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/15 Chapter I The Sign TAURUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00041|Eridanus]] — the river of fire from Orion's foot, matched with Isaiah 30: "The breath of the Lord, like a stream of brimstone, doth kindle it."

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/10 Chapter VI Esoteric Doctrines of Buddhism Parodied in Christianity#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_263|Vol. 2, p. 263]] — her own argument from Genesis 2:7: every atom of the dust contains God, and the life given to every creature is "a breath of His own Spirit".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/05 Before the Veil#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_xix|Vol. 1, p. xix]] — quoting the Manava-Dharma-Sastra: the Supreme drew from his own essence "the immortal breath".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/14 Chapter IX Cyclic Phenomena#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_302|Vol. 1, p. 302]] — expounding the Sohar: the first light is the "Infinite, Boundless, and Immortal breath" that infuses intelligent life.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/10 Chapter V The Ether or Astral Light#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_146|Vol. 1, p. 146]] — her own voice: the universal agent is "under one impulse" the breath of God "and under another—the venom of the devil."
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/11 Chapter VII Early Christian Heresies and Secret Societies#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_299|Vol. 2, p. 299]] — her etymology of IAO as "Breath of Life", leaning on Max Müller for *asu*, breath.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/16 Chapter X The Devil-Myth#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_489|Vol. 2, p. 489]] — the passage Hall quotes; she attributes it to "the explanations" of the initiates.

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Explicit citation | Hall (PDF 264) quotes Blavatsky (vol. 2, p. 489) by name on the incubating breath | One sentence about serpent-worship; it does not show Hall adopting her wider doctrine |
| Shared verse | Gaskell and Blavatsky both build on Genesis 2:7 as a divine element entering man | Gaskell limits it to the spark in the lower mind; Blavatsky extends "breath" to every animal and atom. Neither cites the other |
| Tension | Gaskell's breath of life "is immortal"; Hall's cherubim call Adam's a perishable "breath of form" | Hall puts the line in the mouth of guardians whom Adam answers; it is not his final word |
| Contrast with scripture as Bullinger uses it | His two passages make breath human vanity or divine wrath | He is quoting, not theorising; nothing here answers the emanation readings |
