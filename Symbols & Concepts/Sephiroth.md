# Sephiroth

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

The ten Qabbalistic numerations and their tree-diagram. This hub is about that diagram, not about the Edenic [[Symbols & Concepts/Tree of Life|Tree of Life]] unless a source itself joins them.

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter I (6 November 2026)

She calls "the fundamental geometrical figure of the Kabala" a figure "given by the Deity itself to Moses on Mount Sinai" ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_14|page 14]]; footnote 67 cites Exodus xxv. 40). Installed [[Exodus 025#^kjv-exo-25-40|Exodus 25:40]] speaks of making the furnishings "after their pattern, which was shewed thee in the mount."

- Tier: explicit citation of that verse; source-specific interpretation of the pattern as a kabalistic geometrical figure.
- Limit: Volume 2, Chapter V ("Mysteries of the Kabala") is unread. This is not a description of Hall's Tree of the Sephiroth, and it does not identify her "figure" with Gaskell's ten numerations.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter I Old Things with New Names - Analytical Note|Chapter I analytical note]].
