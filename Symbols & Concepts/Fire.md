# Fire

This is a **shared research hub**, not a universal definition. Each reading stays attributed to its source. Gaskell dictionary text is `machine-extracted-unreviewed` OCR unless a note says otherwise. Cross-source comments below are **editorial comparison** unless labelled as an explicit citation. Shared wording or a similar symbol is not evidence that one author used or influenced the other.


## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

Gaskell himself splits fire into higher and lower aspects (divine love versus passionate desire). Hall discusses elemental fire-beings. Those are neighbouring fire-traditions, not one meaning.

## From E. W. Bullinger, The Witness of the Stars (6 November 2026)

Ara is an inverted altar whose fires point down toward Tartarus - curse and everlasting fire, applied to [[Psalms 021#^kjv-psa-21-9|Psalm 21:9]]. Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/08 Chapter IV The Sign SAGITTARIUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00019|Ara]]. Eridanus is the "River of the Judge" / "River of Wrath," with Daniel's fiery stream ([[Daniel 007#^kjv-dan-7-10|Daniel 7:9-11]]), Psalm 97:3, and Isaiah 30:27-33 and 66:15, and he says it speaks of the lake of fire. Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/15 Chapter I The Sign TAURUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00041|Eridanus]].

- Tier: source-specific interpretation and explicit citation of those judgment texts. Differing interpretive frame from Gaskell's higher fire (divine love / purification) and from Hall's elemental fire-beings - not treated here as a direct contradiction of a shared proposition.
- Limit: Do not fold this judgment-fire into Aquarius's blessing-water or into baptismal fire. Gaskell's FIRE, DESTROYING entry remains a neighbouring lemma, not the same object.

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled (6 November 2026)

The glossary entry "Elemental Spirits" ([[05 Before the Veil#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_xxix|page xxix]]) names creatures of earth, air, fire, and water, "called by the kabalists gnomes, sylphs, salamanders, and undines," and adds that they "never become men."

- Tier: editorial resemblance of that fourfold list to Hall's report of Paracelsus's names in [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Elements and Their Inhabitants#^hall-stota-pdf-320|The Elements and Their Inhabitants, PDF 320]], and to Gaskell's See-reference to salamanders. Contrast on the added "never become men" clause until Hall's chapter is re-checked against that exact proposition.
- Limit: Volume 1, Chapter VII ("The Elements, Elementals, and Elementaries") is unread. Hall wrote later (1928); shared classification is not influence.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter I (6 November 2026)

Popular Greek tradition, as she reports it, alternately burns the world (Ecpyrosis) and deluges it ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_31|pages 30-31]]). Higgins is quoted: destruction *by fire* when the sun arrives at the equator (page 34).

- Tier: contrast of frames with Gaskell's higher/lower fire, Hall's elemental fire-beings, and Bullinger's judgment-fire (Ara / Eridanus) already on this hub — not treated as a direct contradiction of one shared proposition.
- Limit: She is describing cyclic world-conflagration, not commenting on those other passages.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter II (6 November 2026)

Crookes's sixth theory — an invisible earthly order of "gnomes, fairies, kobolds, elves, goblins, Puck" — is called "one of the claims of the kabalists" ([[07 Chapter II Phenomena and Forces#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_47|page 47]]). She later denies that human spirits can materialize *in propria persona*; solid forms are elementary "portrait statues" (pages 67-70), and talking spectre-animals are treated as elementals (pages 70-71).

- Tier: editorial resemblance of that class-list to Hall [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Elements and Their Inhabitants#^hall-stota-pdf-320|PDF 320]] and to her own glossary. The "never materialize in propria persona" clause is her proposition, not checked line-by-line against the rest of Hall's chapter in this pass.
- Limit: Volume 1, Chapter VII is still unread.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter III (6 November 2026)

The lotus is produced by fire (heat) and water; “the Eternal is the Spirit of Fire, which stirs up and fructifies and develops into a concrete form everything that is born of water” ([[08 Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_93|pages 92-93]]).

- Tier: `source-specific interpretation`. Neighbouring this hub's fire vocabulary; not Hall's elemental fire-beings and not the cyclic *ecpyrosis* already recorded from Chapter I.
- Limit: Volume 1, Chapter VII remains unread.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind - Analytical Note|Chapter III analytical note]].
