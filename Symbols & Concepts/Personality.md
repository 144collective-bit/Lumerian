# Personality

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

In Gaskell this is a technical term: the mortal, lower-mind self of one life-period, set against the immortal Individuality that puts it forth and takes it back. Hall and Blavatsky sometimes use the word in a similar perishable-versus-immortal contrast, but each also uses it in the plain sense of a person's character or identity, and Bullinger uses it only in that plain sense. The King James Bible does not use the word.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/P/PERSONALITY|PERSONALITY]] (`gaskell-1960-entry-personality`; [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_574|page_574]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_576|page_576]]).

Machine-selected short definition (OCR exact; typo `mumber` and a stray running head `PERSONALITY` preserved):

> A temporary aggregation of a limited mumber of soul-qualities PERSONALITY about a centre of consciousness during a life-period on the lower planes.

The body calls the personality “a limited manifestation of the immortal individuality which periodically outbreathes it and again inbreathes it,” seats it in the lower mind, and says “The personality (Lunar pitri) is mortal.” Adapa and Achilles are read as figures of it.

A second headword, [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/P/PERSONALITY, PHARISEES, SON OF MAN, FRANANGER WATERFALL|PERSONALITY, PHARISEES, SON OF MAN, FRANANGER WATERFALL]], is an import artefact. Its one-line body about the astral plane sits on `page_300`, far from the P pages, and most likely belongs to the waterfall headword alone.

Especially relevant neighbouring entries: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/D/DEATH OF THE PERSONALITY|DEATH OF THE PERSONALITY]] (“The personality is always said to be mortal.”), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/I/INDIVIDUALITY|INDIVIDUALITY]] (which glosses the moon as the personality), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/A/ADAM (LOWER ASPECT)|ADAM (LOWER ASPECT)]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CAUSAL-SELF|CAUSAL-SELF]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/A/ADAPA, THE SON OF EA, OR OF ERIDU|ADAPA]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/A/ACHILLES ACHILLES, SON OF PELEUS AND THETIS|ACHILLES]].

## King James Bible

The word “personality” does not occur in the King James Bible, and the PERSONALITY entry prints no verse locator. The neighbouring DEATH OF THE PERSONALITY entry does cite two passages (OCR locators `1 Cor. xv. 21, 22` and `Rev. xiv. 13`; study-layer bridges: [[Studies/Bible–Gaskell Cross References/Bridges/death-of-the-personality__1-corinthians-15-21|1 Corinthians 15:21]], [[Studies/Bible–Gaskell Cross References/Bridges/death-of-the-personality__revelation-14-13|Revelation 14:13]]):

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/46 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 015#^kjv-1co-15-21|1 Corinthians 15:21]]–[[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/46 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 015#^kjv-1co-15-22|15:22]] — Gaskell reads Adam here as “the lower mind or personality.”
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/66 Revelation/Revelation 014#^kjv-rev-14-13|Revelation 14:13]] — read as personalities perfected and absorbed into their individualities.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Human Body in Symbolism#^hall-stota-pdf-221|PDF 221]] — Hall quotes an unnamed “secret work”: “Personality is suspended upon a thread from the nature of Being.” Only man's bodies, he adds, pass through birth and death.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Fishes, Insects, Animals, Reptiles and Birds Part Two#^hall-stota-pdf-277|PDF 277]] — “Man’s lower personality is the Apis” in which Osiris incarnates.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Hiramic Legend#^hall-stota-pdf-230|PDF 230]] — the skull and cross bones on a Masonic apron recall that liberation comes only after “the philosophical death of man’s sensuous personality.”
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Fishes, Insects, Animals, Reptiles and Birds Part One#^hall-stota-pdf-257|PDF 257]] — Hall reports the Gnostic division of the Redeemer into the mortal Jesus and Christos, the divine nature being concealed “within the personality of the lowly Jesus.”

Elsewhere Hall uses the word in its everyday biographical sense. He does not cite Gaskell.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

One use, in the plain sense of identity: ancient authorities “differ as to the personality of Hercules” ([[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/07 Chapter III The Sign SCORPIO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00016|Hercules]]). He does not use the term in a doctrinal sense.

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/14 Chapter IX Cyclic Phenomena#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_315|Vol. 1, page 315]] — in her own voice, after reporting Neo-platonist and kabalist views of the spirit: the word, as usually understood, “is an absurdity, if applied literally to our immortal essence,” though that essence is still a distinct entity.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/14 Chapter IX Cyclic Phenomena#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_317|Vol. 1, page 317]] — in her own voice she speaks of “the possible death and obliteration of the human personality by the separation of the immortal part from the perishable,” then quotes the Swedenborgian minister Chauncey Giles in support.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/12 Chapter VII The Elements Elementals and Elementaries#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_206|Vol. 1, page 206]] — reporting Henry More, who held that “personality, memory, and conscience will surely continue in the future state.” This is More's view, not hers.
