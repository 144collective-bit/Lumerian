## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled (6 November 2026)

In the volume 1 preface she writes that they "stepped in spirit within the temple of Isis; to lift aside the veil of 'the one that is and was and shall be' at Saïs" ([[04 Preface#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_vi|preface, page vi]]–[[04 Preface#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_vii|vii]]). The Sais veil is the book's governing image of withheld-then-granted knowledge, not a passing mythic name.

- Tier: source-specific interpretation; editorial comparison with Gaskell's [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/V/VEIL OF ISIS|VEIL OF ISIS]] (same motif; Gaskell interiorises it as "the higher mind inaccessible to the lower consciousness"; she narrates a disclosure by living "sages"). Differing frames, not a demonstrated contradiction of one proposition.
- Limit: Numbered Egyptian chapters (especially volume 1, Chapter XIV) are unread. Bullinger's Isis/Apis aside remains queued, not promoted from this preface alone.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]] and [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Start Here|Isis Unveiled — Start Here]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter I (6 November 2026)

On [[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_16|page 16]] she writes that present-day philosophers "lift the Veil of Isis"—"for Isis is but the symbol of nature"—and see only physical forms, missing the soul.

- Tier: source-specific interpretation. This nature-symbol reading sits beside (not identical with) the preface's Sais-veil image of withheld wisdom already noted above.
- Limit: Volume 1, Chapter XIV remains unread. Gaskell's VEIL OF ISIS ("higher mind inaccessible...") is still a different interior frame, not a contradiction of one shared proposition.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter I Old Things with New Names - Analytical Note|Chapter I analytical note]].
