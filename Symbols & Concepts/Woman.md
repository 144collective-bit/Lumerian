# Woman

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

In this vault "woman" is read as a principle far more often than as a person, but the sources disagree about which principle. Gaskell alone gives her three values: the emotion-nature, Wisdom (buddhi), and matter. Hall moves between the Great Mother, Sophia and the lower mortal nature. Blavatsky sets woman with matter and the great deep, while naming Sophia as her highest prototype. Bullinger keeps to the woman of Genesis 3:15 and insists that the honour belongs to her Seed, not to her.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/W/WOMAN|WOMAN]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_828|page_828]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_829|page_829]]). Short definition, OCR exact:

> A symbol of the emotion-nature of the soul, which is to be transmuted from the astral to the buddhic state.

The body reads the rib of Genesis 2 as a higher sub-plane of mind on which the emotion-nature is evolved, and reads 1 Timothy 2 as a rule that emotion be "subject to the dictates of the reasoning mind (man)". Gaskell says that passage "has no erroneous reference to persons of different sexes".

Four qualified entries pull in different directions:

- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/W/WOMAN AS GODDESS|WOMAN AS GODDESS]] — "the Wisdom principle, Buddhi, from which the higher emotions proceed".
- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/W/WOMAN AS MATTER|WOMAN AS MATTER]] — "the veil of matter in which manifestation takes place".
- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/W/WOMAN TAKEN IN ADULTERY|WOMAN TAKEN IN ADULTERY]] — "the emotion-nature in its lower aspect allied to the sense-activities".
- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/W/WOMAN EMBODYING LOKE|WOMAN EMBODYING LOKE]] — the physical nature as used by the astral (the OCR definition ends on a comma).

Neighbouring entries: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/E/EVE, WIFE OF ADAM|EVE, WIFE OF ADAM]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/W/WIFE|WIFE]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/M/MOTHER, DIVINE|MOTHER, DIVINE]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/H/HARLOT, OR STRANGE WOMAN|HARLOT, OR STRANGE WOMAN]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CROWN OF TWELVE STARS|CROWN OF TWELVE STARS]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/V/VIRGIN BRINGING FORTH A SON|VIRGIN BRINGING FORTH A SON]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/A/ANDROMEDA, DAUGHTER OF CEPHEUS AND CASSIOPEA|ANDROMEDA]].

## King James Bible

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 002#^kjv-gen-2-22|Genesis 2:22]]–[[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 002#^kjv-gen-2-23|23]] — woman made from the rib; quoted at the head of Gaskell's WOMAN.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 003#^kjv-gen-3-15|Genesis 3:15]] — enmity between serpent and woman; the text Bullinger's whole book unfolds, and cited in Gaskell's HEEL.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 003#^kjv-gen-3-16|Genesis 3:16]] — cited in Gaskell's CONCEPTION OF CHILDREN.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/48 Galatians/Galatians 004#^kjv-gal-4-4|Galatians 4:4]] — "made of a woman"; quoted in WOMAN AS MATTER, and cited by Bullinger and Blavatsky.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/43 John/John 008#^kjv-jhn-8-3|John 8:3]] — quoted in WOMAN TAKEN IN ADULTERY.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/54 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 002#^kjv-1ti-2-11|1 Timothy 2:11]] — the opening of the passage Gaskell allegorises in WOMAN.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/66 Revelation/Revelation 012#^kjv-rev-12-1|Revelation 12:1]] — the woman clothed with the sun; quoted in CROWN OF TWELVE STARS and described by Hall.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Qabbalistic Keys to the Creation of Man#^hall-stota-pdf-392|PDF 392]] — two theories of the division of the sexes. Under the second, "woman is elevated from the position of being man's errant part to one of complete equality", and Hall says the deeper schools lean that way.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Qabbalistic Keys to the Creation of Man#^hall-stota-pdf-393|PDF 393]] — "in vindication of Eve" the allegory means only that man is tempted by his emotions away from reason.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Mystery of the Apocalypse#^hall-stota-pdf-623|PDF 623]] — the woman of Revelation 12 "represents the constellation of Virgo and also the Egyptian Isis" about to bear Horus.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Mystery of the Apocalypse#^hall-stota-pdf-626|PDF 626]] — the harlot of Babylon "may be an effort (probably interpolated)" to vilify the Great Mother; as an initiatory test she is "the temptress (the animal soul)".
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Isis, the Virginof the World#^hall-stota-pdf-130|PDF 130]] — the troubadours' "most beautiful woman in all the world" was Sophia, the Virgin of Wisdom.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/05 Chapter I The Sign VIRGO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00006|Virgo]] — the sign is the woman of Genesis 3:15, whom later prophecy defines as a virgin of Israel.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/12 Chapter III The Sign PISCES#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00032|Andromeda]] — the chained woman is "the captive daughter of Zion".
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/13 Chapter IV The Sign ARIES#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00035|Cassiopeia]] — "the same woman freed, delivered, and enthroned", preparing for her husband.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/19 Summary#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00055|Summary]] — he attacks the Vulgate reading *ipsa* in Genesis 3:15 as putting "the woman in the place of the woman's Seed".

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/14 Chapter IX The Vedas and the Bible#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_446|Vol. 2, page 446]] — her own voice: woman stands "in relation to 'matter' or the *great deep*, as the 'Virgin of the Sea,' who crushes the 'Dragon'", a title she then traces to the Virgin Mary, Dido and Venus.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/14 Chapter IX The Vedas and the Bible#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_449|Vol. 2, page 449]] — her own voice: the war "between the snake and the woman" is the war "between spirit and matter", and holds only in the mortal world.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/21 Footnotes#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Footnote_291|Vol. 2, footnote 291]] — her gloss on the Ophite scheme she is reporting: "Sophia is the highest prototype of woman—the first *spiritual* Eve".

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Internal Gaskell split | Woman is emotion, Wisdom and matter in separate entries | These are three lemmas. Do not average them into "the feminine" |
| Gaskell and Hall on Eve | Both read the temptation as emotion drawing mind or reason aside | Gaskell keeps emotion subordinate to mind; Hall argues for equality. Hall does not cite Gaskell |
| Gaskell and Blavatsky on matter | WOMAN AS MATTER and Blavatsky's page 449 both pair woman with matter against spirit | Blavatsky's snake is wisdom; Gaskell's Galatians reading concerns the Logos limited by matter. Different arguments |
| Revelation 12 | Gaskell reads the woman as buddhi; Hall as Virgo and Isis; Bullinger's Virgo is the mother of the Seed | Three readings of one star-woman. No mutual citation is claimed |
| Bullinger against Blavatsky | Bullinger denies the woman is the crusher; Blavatsky reports the Virgin who "crushes the 'Dragon'" | Blavatsky is describing a tradition, not defending Marian doctrine |
