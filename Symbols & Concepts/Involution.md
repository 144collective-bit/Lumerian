# Involution

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

The descent or infolding of spirit into matter, understood as the necessary first half of a process whose second half is [[Symbols & Concepts/Evolution|Evolution]]. The term is central to Gaskell and rare everywhere else in the vault: Hall and Blavatsky each use it once, Bullinger never, and the King James Bible not at all. The main split is in value: Gaskell calls the descent a Divine Sacrifice that makes things perfect in potential, while Hall and Blavatsky attach it to the fall of man and a loss of spirituality.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Both headwords are damaged in the import.

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/I/INVOLUTION TION|INVOLUTION TION]] (`gaskell-1960-entry-involution-tion`; [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_404|page_404]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_406|page_406]]). The fragment `AND EVOLUs INVOLUTION` stranded in the body suggests the printed heading was “Involution and Evolution” (an inference, not a checked reading). Machine-selected short definition (OCR exact):

> These processes of life are the descent of Spirit into Matter and the ascent of Spirit therefrom.

The next sentence assigns the halves: “The first is the Divine Sacrifice, or the limitation of Spirit in forms; and the second is the Resurrection from the Dead, or the liberation of Spirit from captivity in matter.” The body glosses Plotinus, Empedocles, the Sankhya and the *Corpus Hermeticum* as statements of this two-fold process, and quotes R. J. Campbell: “what is evolved in nature must first have been involved.”

Second entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/I/INVOLUTION-OF THE INCARNATE SELF OR ARCHETYPAL SOUL OF HUMANITY|INVOLUTION-OF THE INCARNATE SELF OR ARCHETYPAL SOUL OF HUMANITY]] (`page_406`–`page_407`). No short definition was auto-selected. Its opening says the Divine Self “descends IRIS as a spiritual germ to the astral plane” (`IRIS` is a stray running head) and culminates on the buddhic plane as the Archetypal Man.

The term is also defined from the other side in [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/E/EVOLUTION AND INVOLUTION|EVOLUTION AND INVOLUTION]], and [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/I/INCARNATION OF SPIRIT|INCARNATION OF SPIRIT]] says “Incarnation completes involution.” Other relevant neighbours: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/A/ARCHETYPAL MAN|ARCHETYPAL MAN]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CIRCLE OF EXISTENCE|CIRCLE OF EXISTENCE]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CREATION OR EMANATION OF THE SPIRITUAL AND MATERIAL UNIVERSES|CREATION OR EMANATION…]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CRUCIFIXION OF PURUSHA, OR THE SACRIFICE OF THE MAN|CRUCIFIXION OF PURUSHA]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/F/FALL OF MAN|FALL OF MAN]].

## King James Bible

The word “involution” does not occur in the King James Bible. Gaskell cites one verse in the primary entry (OCR locator `Eccuiss. iii. 11`, i.e. Eccles.; no bridge note exists for it yet):

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/21 Ecclesiastes/Ecclesiastes 003#^kjv-ecc-3-11|Ecclesiastes 3:11]] — Gaskell reads “He hath made everything beautiful in its time” as the period of involution, when the archetypal world is made perfect, and “eternity in their heart” as the means of immortality set latent in the causal-body.

Limit: Gaskell quotes a version reading “eternity in their heart”; the KJV text in this vault has “the world in their heart.”

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

One use. Reading Plato's Atlantis as an allegory, Hall says its sinking and the biblical fall of man both “signify spiritual involution – a prerequisite to conscious evolution” ([[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Atlantis and the Godsof Antiquity#^hall-stota-pdf-85|PDF 85]]). He does not cite Gaskell.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

Bullinger does not use the term.

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

One use ([[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/10 Chapter V The Ether or Astral Light#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_149|Vol. 1, page 149]]). She first reports what “the Chaldean Kabalists tell us”: primeval man was more spiritual than present man, became “*despiritualized* or tainted with matter,” and only then received a fleshly body. She then asks, in her own voice, what the “coats of skin” can mean except that the spiritual man “had reached, through the progress of involution, to that point where matter, predominating over and conquering spirit, had transformed him into the physical man.” Her verse is Genesis 3:21 ([[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/21 Footnotes#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Footnote_266|footnote 266]]; [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 003#^kjv-gen-3-21|KJV text]]).

Elsewhere in the book she describes the same descent as evolution begun “from above” ([[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/10 Chapter V The Ether or Astral Light#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_154|page 154]]), so the word is not yet fixed vocabulary for her here.
