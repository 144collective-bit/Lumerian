# Darkness

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

Darkness does two different jobs in this vault. In one it is what light is missing from: ignorance, matter, the enemy's power. In the other it is what comes before light: the unmanifest source that cannot be seen. Gaskell, Hall and Blavatsky each hold both senses; Bullinger uses only the first. It is the counterpart of [[Symbols & Concepts/Light|Light]], but only the first sense is light's simple opposite.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Gaskell gives two entries and keeps them apart.

[[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/D/DARKNESS-HIGHER ASPECT|DARKNESS—HIGHER ASPECT]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_206|page_206]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_207|page_207]]). Short definition (OCR exact):

> Symbolic of the inscrutable Source of the One and the All, under the terms, the Absolute, the Potential, the Unknowable, and the Unmanifest.

The body is a chain of quotations: Damascius on the Egyptians' "thrice unknown Darkness", the Rig-Veda, Manu, and Origen's remark that Scripture sometimes speaks of darkness in a good sense, with Psalm 18:11 as his example. The OCR is rough (`darknees`, `iteelf`).

[[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/D/DARKNESS-LOWER ASPECT|DARKNESS—LOWER ASPECT]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_207|page_207]]). Short definition (OCR exact):

> Symbolic of ignorance which is absence of the light of Truth.

The body cites Plotinus ("all matter is dark"), Swedenborg, 2 Samuel 22:29, and Gregory on Job, who reads darkness as ignorance. A running head (`200 DAVID`) has leaked into the OCR text.

Neighbouring entries: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/S/SHADOW OR DARKNESS|SHADOW OR DARKNESS]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/N/NIGHT, PRIMORDIAL|NIGHT, PRIMORDIAL]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CHAOS, OR THE DEEP|CHAOS, OR THE DEEP]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/B/BLACK|BLACK]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/I/IGNORANCE, AVIDYA|IGNORANCE, AVIDYA]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/L/LIGHT, PRIMORDIAL|LIGHT, PRIMORDIAL]], and [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/D/DAYLIGHT AND DARENESS|DAYLIGHT AND DARENESS]] (the headword is an OCR misreading of "DARKNESS").

## King James Bible

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001#^kjv-gen-1-2|Genesis 1:2]] — darkness on the face of the deep, before light. Blavatsky quotes it on her page 214.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001#^kjv-gen-1-4|Genesis 1:4]] — light divided from darkness; already on the Light hub.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001#^kjv-gen-1-5|Genesis 1:5]] — darkness named Night; cited under Gaskell's DAYS and MORNING entries.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/19 Psalms/Psalms 018#^kjv-psa-18-11|Psalms 18:11]] — "He made darkness his secret place"; the verse behind Gaskell's higher aspect (through Origen), bridged from his WATER (HIGHER ASPECT) entry.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/10 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 022#^kjv-2sa-22-29|2 Samuel 22:29]] — "the LORD will lighten my darkness"; cited in Gaskell's lower aspect.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/23 Isaiah/Isaiah 060#^kjv-isa-60-2|Isaiah 60:2]] — darkness over the earth, glory rising on Israel; quoted by Bullinger.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/42 Luke/Luke 022#^kjv-luk-22-53|Luke 22:53]] — "your hour, and the power of darkness"; quoted by Bullinger.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Life and Teachings of Thoth Hermes Trismegistus#^hall-stota-pdf-100|Thoth Hermes Trismegistus, PDF 100]] — retelling the Poimandres vision: a "great darkness" swallows the Light, and Hermes understands that the swirling darkness "represented material substance".
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Human Body in Symbolism#^hall-stota-pdf-223|The Human Body in Symbolism, PDF 223]] — Hall's own statement of the other sense. Light is "posterior to life"; what is anterior to light is darkness, which veils "abstract and undifferentiated Being". He calls associating darkness with evil a "false practice".
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Chemical Marriage#^hall-stota-pdf-546|The Chemical Marriage, PDF 546]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Chemical Marriage#^hall-stota-pdf-547|547]] — a caption to a figure from Fludd: the dark half of the top globe is the divine darkness that serves as the Deity's "hiding place"; a second dark globe is the "potential darkness" on the primordial deep.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Ancient Mysteries and Secret Societies Part Three#^hall-stota-pdf-73|Ancient Mysteries Part Three, PDF 73]] — in the Eleusinian reading, Persephone (the initiate's soul) ascends "from the darkness of his material nature into the light of understanding".

Hall does not cite Gaskell.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

Bullinger has no darkness symbol of his own. The word comes in with Scripture and means the enemy's power or the world's condition.

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/07 Chapter III The Sign SCORPIO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00014|Scorpio]] — the conflict of Genesis 3:15 renewed at Gethsemane; he quotes Luke 22:53.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/15 Chapter I The Sign TAURUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00040|Taurus (Orion)]] — Isaiah 60:1–3 set out as a structure; his [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/22 Footnotes#^bullinger-wots-fn-63|footnote 63]] labels the central lines "the darkness of the world".
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/13 Chapter IV The Sign ARIES#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00034|Aries]] — the literal "noon-day darkness" of the Crucifixion, with the sun near stars whose names he renders "the pierced" and "the bruised".

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/09 Chapter V Mysteries of the Kabala#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_214|Vol. 2, page 214]] — her own comparison: the kabalistic Sephira is the point of divine light "appearing from within the fathomless and boundless darkness", set beside Manu, Hermes and Genesis 1:2.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/09 Chapter V Mysteries of the Kabala#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_225|Vol. 2, page 225]] — her exposition of the Kabala and the Ophite system: "Darkness is *matter*"; light tempts it and it attracts light. She is describing those systems, with evident approval.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/05 Before the Veil#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_xxv|Vol. 1, page xxv]] — reporting the Rosicrucians: what seems light to matter "is darkness to *spirit*".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/05 Before the Veil#^blavatsky-isis-v1-BEFORE_THE_VEIL|Vol. 1, Before the Veil]] — the "night of Heathenism" dispelled by Christianity and science is a claim she reports ("as we are told") in order to dispute it.

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Darkness before light | Gaskell's higher aspect, Hall (PDF 223, 546–547) and Blavatsky (page 214) all describe a darkness that precedes and hides the source | No mutual citation. Hall's Fludd passage is a caption; Blavatsky is comparing cosmogonies, not defining a symbol |
| Darkness as lack | Gaskell's lower aspect says ignorance; Hall's Poimandres and Blavatsky's page 225 say matter | Ignorance and matter are not the same reading. Gaskell quotes Plotinus on matter but defines by ignorance |
| Bullinger against the rest | For Bullinger darkness is only the enemy's hour and the world's state | He does not discuss Psalm 18:11 or reject a higher sense; he simply never raises it |
| With the Light hub | Gaskell's lower aspect is defined by reference to "the light of Truth", matching his LIGHT entry | The higher aspect is not light's opposite; do not file it as "absence of light" |
