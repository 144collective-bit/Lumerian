# Heart

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

The inner centre of a person. Gaskell and Hall both make it the seat of spirit and not only of feeling, though each admits the "emotional" sense as a second meaning. In the King James verses the vault cites, the heart is as often the source of evil as of good. Bullinger has no heart symbolism of his own: in his book the word mostly marks where a star sits in a figure.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/H/HEART|HEART]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_351|page_351]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_352|page_352]]).

Short definition (OCR exact; `atmabuddhi` is printed without its hyphen):

> A symbol of the causal-body as a centre of being on the higher mental plane, and the receptacle of atmabuddhi, the spirit within.

The body adds at once that the heart "also signifies the love principle and the higher affections." It reads the Book of the Dead address "My heart, my mother!" as the causal-body, and the weighing of the heart as that body balanced against the personality. A quotation from Bunyan brings in Proverbs 23:26, Matthew 15:19 and Psalm 26:2, after which Gaskell notes that "soul" and "heart" both "have higher and lower meanings".

Neighbouring entries: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/A/AB, THE HEART|AB, THE HEART]] (the spiritual ego, which "cannot act wrongly"), [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CAUSAL-BODY, SARIRA|CAUSAL-BODY, SARIRA]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/P/PRANAS, FIVE LIFE-BREATHS|PRANAS, FIVE LIFE-BREATHS]], and the weighing scene at [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JUDGMENT JUDGMENT HALL OF OSIRIS, AND THE WEIGHING OF THE HEART|JUDGMENT HALL OF OSIRIS, AND THE WEIGHING OF THE HEART]] (the import doubled the first word of that headword).

## King James Bible

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/20 Proverbs/Proverbs 023#^kjv-pro-23-26|Proverbs 23:26]] — "give me thine heart"; Gaskell, through Bunyan.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/40 Matthew/Matthew 015#^kjv-mat-15-19|Matthew 15:19]] — evil thoughts proceed out of the heart; same Bunyan quotation.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/19 Psalms/Psalms 026#^kjv-psa-26-2|Psalms 26:2]] — "try my reins and my heart"; same quotation.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-5|Genesis 6:5]] — the thoughts of the heart "only evil continually"; a Gaskell bridge from another entry (not opened here).
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/24 Jeremiah/Jeremiah 017#^kjv-jer-17-10|Jeremiah 17:10]] — "I the LORD search the heart"; likewise a Gaskell bridge, not opened here.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/26 Ezekiel/Ezekiel 036#^kjv-eze-36-26|Ezekiel 36:26]] — "A new heart also will I give you"; cited by Bullinger in Aquarius.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/40 Matthew/Matthew 005#^kjv-mat-5-8|Matthew 5:8]] — "Blessed are the pure in heart"; echoed by Blavatsky.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Human Body in Symbolism#^hall-stota-pdf-220|PDF 220]] — the Mysteries knew the heart as "the center of spiritual consciousness" but outwardly used it for the emotional nature; putting the brain in its place "was one of these blinds."
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Sun,A Universal Deity#^hall-stota-pdf-140|PDF 140]] — brain, heart and generative system as three centres; Hall reads the three tabernacles of the Transfiguration this way, the heart being the largest.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Ancient Mysteries and Secret Societies Part Three#^hall-stota-pdf-79|PDF 79]] — in the Bacchic myth the Titans scatter the rational soul of the world but cannot scatter its heart, or source.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Fraternity of the Rose Cross#^hall-stota-pdf-432|PDF 432]] — "The rose symbolizes the heart", which Christians take as an emblem of love and compassion.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Conclusion#^hall-stota-pdf-674|PDF 674]] — "In the mystic this gate is the heart"; in the philosopher it is reason.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

Bullinger offers no doctrine of the heart. What he has:

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/07 Chapter III The Sign SCORPIO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00014|Scorpio]] — Antares marks "the scorpion's heart"; in the [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/07 Chapter III The Sign SCORPIO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00015|next section]] Ophiuchus treads on it. An anatomical position that he turns into a picture of the enemy crushed.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/18 Chapter IV The Sign LEO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00051|Leo]] — Regulus marks "the heart of the Lion"; he gives the name as "treading under foot".
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/11 Chapter II The Sign AQUARIUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00026|Aquarius]] — restored Israel will "possess a new heart and a new spirit" (Ezekiel 36).
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/19 Summary#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00055|Summary]] — his one pastoral use: "Knowledge of the Scriptures where the heart is not subject to Christ" is lifeless.

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/11 Chapter VII Early Christian Heresies and Secret Societies#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_318|Vol. 2, p. 318]] — her own voice: every true philosopher carried "in the silent sanctuary of his heart" the truth of the God within.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/12 Chapter VII The Elements Elementals and Elementaries#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_218|Vol. 1, p. 218]] — her own voice: only the pure in heart "see God" or exercise divine gifts; magical power is denied to the vicious.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/05 Chapter I The Church Where Is It#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_40|Vol. 2, p. 40]] — quoting the Sepher Jezireh on secrecy: close "thy heart, lest thou shouldst think aloud". Hall prints the same verse in another translation ("seal up your lips and guard your heart", [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Qabbalah, the Secret Doctrine of Israel#^hall-stota-pdf-352|PDF 352]]).
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/16 Chapter X The Devil-Myth#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_494|Vol. 2, p. 494]] — describing the Book of the Dead judgment scene, where the scales show "whether the heart of the deceased balances the symbol of truth"; she uses it as a key to Job.

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial resemblance | Gaskell and Hall each give the heart a higher (spiritual centre) and a lower or outward (emotional) sense | Hall does not cite Gaskell; Gaskell's "causal-body" is his own term and Hall's "blind" theory is his |
| Shared scene | Gaskell and Blavatsky both interpret the Egyptian weighing of the heart | Gaskell weighs causal-body against personality; Blavatsky reads an initiation drama behind Job. Neither cites the other |
| Shared text | Blavatsky and Hall both carry the Sepher Yetzirah verse on guarding the heart | Different translations; it concerns secrecy and silence, not the heart as divine seat |
| Contrast with the KJV | Several verses the vault cites make the heart a source of evil | Gaskell handles this only by saying the symbol has higher and lower meanings |
| Absence | Bullinger's "heart" is stellar anatomy plus ordinary devotional usage | Do not read Cor Scorpii or Cor Leonis as heart doctrine |
