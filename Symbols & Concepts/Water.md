## From E. W. Bullinger, The Witness of the Stars (6 November 2026)

Aquarius is read as the water-bearer pouring living waters for the redeemed. Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/11 Chapter II The Sign AQUARIUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00026|AQUARIUS]].

He applies [[Isaiah 044#^kjv-isa-44-3|Isaiah 44:3]] and Ezekiel 36, and refuses to read Pentecost or the Church into the sign: those waters are, in his frame, for Israel. The Church, he says, is nowhere seen in these signs.

- Tier: source-specific interpretation. Contrast with Gaskell’s higher/lower water (Truth vs illusion).
- Limit: Eridanus, in the same book, is a river of wrath — do not fold it into this blessing. This is not a baptism-rite claim.
- See [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter I (6 November 2026)

She describes a repeating "great year" in which, about every decimillennium or neros, a semi-universal deluge "like the legendary Noachian flood" occurs, and she cites Plato's *Timaeus* on several such deluges ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_31|pages 30-31]]). Footnote 100 claims the Bible sent the flood for the "special destruction" of giants; installed [[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-4|Genesis 6:4]] mentions giants, while [[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-5|6:5]]-[[Genesis 006#^kjv-gen-6-7|6:7]] give the reason as man's wickedness and announce destruction of man and animals.

- Tier: contrast of frames (periodic cataclysm vs a single Genesis flood as the narrative presents it). The giant-purpose clause is her overlay, not a wording match.
- Limit: Not Aquarius's blessing-water, and not unread Volume 2 comment on Genesis.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter III (6 November 2026)

Brahma, she says, is a secondary creating god and, like Jehovah, “a *mover of the waters*”; as *Narayana* he “moves on the eternal waters, with the spirit of God within himself” ([[08 Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_91|page 91]]). The lotus is “the emblem of the productive power of nature, through the agency of fire and water (spirit and matter)” and “the product of fire (heat) and water” (pages 91-93).

- Tier: `source-specific interpretation`. Editorial neighbourhood of this hub's water vocabulary, not a citation of Gaskell or of Bullinger's Aquarius blessing-water already on this page.
- Limit: Footnote 177 says she means a kabalistically explained “real” Jewish Scripture, not “the current or accepted Bible.” Do not collapse Jehovah into Narayana as a demonstrated identity.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind - Analytical Note|Chapter III analytical note]]; [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].
