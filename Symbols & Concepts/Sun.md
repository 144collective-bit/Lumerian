# Sun

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

In these sources the sun is both an astronomical body and a symbol of a central, life-giving divine principle. Gaskell and Hall both spiritualise it; they do not share a method or a bibliography here.

## From E. W. Bullinger, The Witness of the Stars (6 November 2026)

He frames the heavens with Psalm 19: the sun’s course and the heavens that “declare,” paired with the written law ([[Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-1|Psalm 19:1]], [[Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-7|Psalm 19:7]]). In “For Signs And For Seasons” he reads [Genesis 1:14](<Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001.md#^kjv-gen-1-14>) as appointing the luminaries for signs of what is to come and for appointed times. Source: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/03 Introduction|Introduction]] and [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/20 For Signs and For Seasons#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00056|For Signs and For Seasons]].

- Tier: explicit citation of those verses; contrast with Hall’s solar-cult reduction and with Gaskell’s interior “central source of Light and Life.”
- Limit: He treats the sun as a created witness and time-keeper, not as a deity. That is not Gaskell’s Higher Self.
- See [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

Further passages read on the same pass: in Capricornus he claims the sun stood in that sign when Christ was born, so that the figure pointed out when the "Sun of Righteousness" should arise and "the true light" appear ([[Galatians 004#^kjv-gal-4-4|Galatians 4:4]]; source [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/10 Chapter I The Sign CAPRICORNUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00022|CAPRICORNUS]]). In Aries he claims that on the 14th of Nisan, in the year of the Crucifixion, the sun stood at *El Nath* and *Al Sheratan* ([[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/13 Chapter IV The Sign ARIES#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00034|ARIES]]). Auriga and Lepus quote [[Malachi 004#^kjv-mal-4-2|Malachi 4:2]] ("Sun of Righteousness"), a verse this hub already holds from Gaskell's entry - a shared citation, not a shared interior meaning. Footnotes 2 and 10 discuss Psalm 19's sun-verbs and *chammah* as a name for the sun. The Capricornus and Aries solar positions remain his astronomical claims.

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter I (6 November 2026)

Via Drummond and Higgins she reads biblical *El*/*Al* as Mithra, the Sun ([[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_13|page 13]]). Footnote 66 adds that hierophants took the visible sun as an emblem of a central, invisible, spiritual Sun, and that retaining *Al* would have made "the *Jehovah* of Moses and the sun" seem identical to the uninitiated. On [[06 Chapter I Old Things with New Names#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_29|page 29]] she names the God she reveres the "Central Spiritual Sun."

- Tier: source-specific interpretation and source etymology (the linguistics are not independently verified). Contrast of frames with Bullinger's created sun as witness and time-keeper, already on this hub, and with [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/20 For Signs and For Seasons#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00056|For Signs and For Seasons]].
- Limit: Do not treat *El* = Jehovah = the visible sun as a demonstrated fact, nor fold this into Hall's solar-cult thesis.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter I Old Things with New Names - Analytical Note|Chapter I analytical note]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter III (6 November 2026)

Pimander, “the Power of the Thought Divine, of Hermes,” “represents the root of all the Egyptian Sun-gods” ([[08 Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_93|page 93]]). Bruno, as she quotes him, cites *Ecclesiastes*: “there is nothing new under the sun,” matching [[Ecclesiastes 001#^kjv-ecc-1-9|Ecclesiastes 1:9]].

- Tier: `source-specific interpretation` of Pimander; `explicit citation` (via Bruno) of the Ecclesiastes proverb.
- Limit: Not a restatement of Chapter I's *El*/*Al* etymology or “Central Spiritual Sun,” and not Bullinger's created sun as witness.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind - Analytical Note|Chapter III analytical note]].
