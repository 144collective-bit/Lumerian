# Zodiac

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

The circle of twelve signs along the sun's yearly path. The sources agree on what it looks like and disagree sharply about what it is for. Gaskell makes it the soul's inner cycle. Hall makes it the solar and astrological frame behind the religions. Bullinger makes it a primeval prophecy of Christ that paganism corrupted. Blavatsky makes it the key that shows Genesis to be derived. The last two are direct opposites on which came first, the Bible's story or the signs' pagan meaning.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/Z/ZODIAC OF TWELVE SIGNS|ZODIAC OF TWELVE SIGNS]] ([[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_847|page_847]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_848|page_848]]). Short definition (OCR exact):

> A symbol of the Cycle of Life through which the Soul’s development is accomplished.

The body says the Soul, as sun, moves in a spiral of twelve stages, each answering to a part of the Archetypal Man. It quotes Proverbs 8:27 with "circle" where the KJV has "compass", and Porphyry (through Max Müller) on Cancer as the gate by which souls descend and Capricorn as the gate by which they ascend. Gaskell reads these as involution and evolution. The OCR carries a running head into the text (`841 ZODIAC`).

## King James Bible

The word "zodiac" does not occur in the King James Bible. These are the verses the sources themselves bring to the subject.

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/20 Proverbs/Proverbs 008#^kjv-pro-8-27|Proverbs 8:27]] — "a compass upon the face of the depth"; Gaskell's cited verse for this entry.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/18 Job/Job 038#^kjv-job-38-32|Job 38:32]] — "Mazzaroth", left untranslated in the text; Bullinger rests on the marginal reading "the twelve signs".
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/12 2 Kings/2 Kings 023#^kjv-2ki-23-5|2 Kings 23:5]] — incense burned "to the planets"; Bullinger notes the Hebrew "Mazzaloth" and the margin "twelve signs".
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001#^kjv-gen-1-14|Genesis 1:14]] — the lights "for signs, and for seasons"; the text of Bullinger's closing chapter.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 037#^kjv-gen-37-9|Genesis 37:9]] — sun, moon and eleven stars; Bullinger's evidence that the signs were linked to Jacob's sons.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/18 Job/Job 026#^kjv-job-26-13|Job 26:13]] — "the crooked serpent"; Bullinger takes it as the constellation Draco.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/19 Psalms/Psalms 019#^kjv-psa-19-1|Psalms 19:1]] — "The heavens declare the glory of God"; the psalm Bullinger's Introduction opens on.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/07 Judges/Judges 005#^kjv-jdg-5-20|Judges 5:20]] — "the stars in their courses fought against Sisera"; quoted by Hall to show that the patriarchs believed in celestial influence.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

Chapter: [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Zodiac and Its Signs|The Zodiac and Its Signs]].

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Zodiac and Its Signs#^hall-stota-pdf-147|PDF 147]] — the pagans saw the stars as living things that influence "the destinies of individuals, nations, and races".
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Zodiac and Its Signs#^hall-stota-pdf-148|PDF 148]] — the signs were probably not drawn from the star groups. The creatures are "symbolic of the qualities and intensity of the sun’s power" in each part of the belt.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Zodiac and Its Signs#^hall-stota-pdf-152|PDF 152]] — "Lamb of God" is a title of the sun reborn in the Ram; Cancer is the Egyptian scarab and the house of the Moon.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Zodiac and Its Signs#^hall-stota-pdf-155|PDF 155]] — after surveying the signs: "the sun in its path controls whatever form of worship man offers to the Supreme Deity."
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Ancient Mysteries and Secret Societies#^hall-stota-pdf-54|The Ancient Mysteries, PDF 54]] — quoting Lundy on the cave of Mithras: Cancer and Capricorn are "the gates of souls", descent and ascent.

Hall does not cite Gaskell or Bullinger in these passages.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

The whole book is one reading of the zodiac: twelve signs, each with three constellations, in three "books".

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/03 Introduction#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00004|Introduction]] — the signs are named in Job as "Mazzaroth". The figures are arbitrary, so the pictures came first and carry a revelation. Greek mythology is a later misreading, and the ancient religions are "a corruption and perversion of primitive truth". The circle must be read from Virgo to [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/18 Chapter IV The Sign LEO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00051|Leo]], which he calls the riddle of the Sphinx.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/17 Chapter III The Sign CANCER#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00047|Cancer]] — "we have not got the original picture"; the Denderah zodiac shows a scarab.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/20 For Signs and For Seasons#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00056|For Signs and For Seasons]] — "signs" in Genesis 1:14 means things to come.

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

Both passages are in her own voice.

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/14 Chapter IX The Vedas and the Bible#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_457|Vol. 2, page 457]] — Libra is a later Greek sign expressing the balance point of evolution. From the added signs she argues that Genesis as we have it is later than Libra, with "the genealogies remodelled to fit the new Zodiac".
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/15 Chapter IX Additional Illustrations#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_460|Vol. 2, page 460]] — "the patriarchs are simply the signs of the Zodiac", emblems of the evolution of races and of divisions of time. On [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/15 Chapter IX Additional Illustrations#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_463|page 463]] Enoch, Hermes and Libra "are one".

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| What the zodiac is for | Gaskell: the soul's cycle. Hall: the sun's powers, shaping worship. Bullinger: prophecy of the Redeemer. Blavatsky: a cosmic and chronological key | Four different claims. None is a version of another, and none of these passages cites another of these authors |
| Bullinger against Blavatsky | Both tie the twelve signs to the patriarchs or tribes of Genesis | Opposite directions. Bullinger: a true revelation in the stars, later attached to Jacob's sons and corrupted by pagans. Blavatsky: the patriarchs are the signs, and Genesis was reshaped to fit |
| Bullinger against Hall | Both deny that the figures come from the look of the star groups, and both note the Cancer scarab | Hall concludes solar symbolism; Bullinger concludes divine revelation, and calls the pagan readings a perversion |
| Gates of Cancer and Capricorn | Gaskell (Porphyry) and Hall (Lundy) report the same doctrine of souls descending and ascending | Each has it second-hand from a different writer. Hall does not cite Gaskell |
