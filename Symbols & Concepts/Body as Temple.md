# Body as Temple

This is a **shared research hub**, not a universal definition. Each reading stays attributed to its source. Gaskell dictionary text is `machine-extracted-unreviewed` OCR unless a note says otherwise. Cross-source comments below are **editorial comparison** unless labelled as an explicit citation. Shared wording or a similar symbol is not evidence that one author used or influenced the other.


## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

Paul’s “ye are the temple of God”; John’s report that Jesus spoke of “the temple of his body”; Gaskell’s TEMPLE OF GOD as causal-body / higher mental condition; Hall’s teaching that mystery-temples were built like a human body and that the body is a house or prison of a divine principle. Related to, but not the same as, the wilderness [[Symbols & Concepts/Tabernacle|Tabernacle]].

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

Chapter: [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Human Body in Symbolism|The Human Body in Symbolism]].

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Human Body in Symbolism#^hall-stota-pdf-216|PDF 216]] — Hall distinguishes Macrocosm and Microcosm: man’s body as “the individual human universe.”
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Human Body in Symbolism#^hall-stota-pdf-220|PDF 220]] — “If possible, the temple itself was constructed in the form of the human body. The candidate entered between the feet and received the highest degree in the point corresponding to the brain.”
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Human Body in Symbolism#^hall-stota-pdf-221|PDF 221]] — “the body of man must not be considered as the individual but only as the house of the individual, in the same manner that the temple was the House of God.” In “grossness” it is “the tomb or prison of a divine principle”; in “unfoldment” it is “the House or Sanctuary of the Deity.”

Hall does **not** name Gaskell. Analytical note: [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Notes/The Human Body in Symbolism - Analytical Note|Human Body — analytical note]].
