# Heaven

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

Heaven taken alone; for the pair as one symbol see [[Symbols & Concepts/Heaven and Earth|Heaven and Earth]]. The sources divide on whether heaven is a state, a set of spheres, or the sky. Gaskell makes it a state of consciousness. Hall usually writes of "heavens" in the plural, the planetary spheres through which a soul rises. Bullinger means the starry heavens, which he reads as a written revelation, and the place from which Christ returns. Blavatsky compares systems of seven heavens and treats "Kingdom of Heaven" as one name among several for a final state.

Machine-selected short definition (OCR exact):

> A symbol of the state of consciousness on the buddhic plane which is above the mental plane.

The body rests on 1 Corinthians 15:47–9 (the quotation opens with the OCR error `'Phe first man`). Gaskell reads the "first man" as the personality, built up from below, and the "second man" as the individuality or Divine monad from above; the ego finally rises to union with the higher "in consciousness on the buddhic plane". Two sermon extracts follow, from Phillips Brooks and Henry Drummond. This Corinthians citation has no bridge note yet.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Pythagorean Theory of Music and Color#^hall-stota-pdf-248|PDF 248]] — the Greek initiates matched the seven planetary heavens to the seven vowels; together they sound a harmony before the Creator's throne (Hall refers to Irenæus).
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Fundamentals of Qabbalistic Cosmogony#^hall-stota-pdf-372|PDF 372]] — among the fifty gates, the "World of Spheres" lists ten heavens from the Moon to the Empyrean.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Mystery of the Apocalypse#^hall-stota-pdf-620|PDF 620]] — John ascends through the seven planetary orbits; related to "the Eastern system", the "door in heaven" is the point in the crown of the skull.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Sun,A Universal Deity#^hall-stota-pdf-136|PDF 136]] — earthly kingdoms were held to be copies of the kingdoms of Heaven, which were best figured by the sun and its planets.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/Fifteen Rosicrucian and Qabbalistic Diagrams#^hall-stota-pdf-462|PDF 462]] — describing a plate: God's outflow shows first in "the heaven of heavens – the Schamayim", beyond human understanding.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/03 Introduction#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00002|Introduction]] — Psalm 19 sets two revelations side by side, one "written in the Heavens" and one in the Word.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/20 For Signs and For Seasons#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00056|For Signs and For Seasons]] — Genesis 1:14 declares that the heavens hold a revelation of things to come and of appointed times.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/08 Chapter IV The Sign SAGITTARIUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00020|Draco]] — the first book of the signs closes with the Dragon "cast down from Heaven" (Revelation 12:9–10).
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/12 Chapter III The Sign PISCES#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00033|Cepheus]] — a crowned king "enthroned in the highest heaven", foot on the Pole Star: the Redeemer coming to rule.

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/08 Chapter IV Oriental Cosmogonies and Bible Records#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_187|vol. 2, p. 187]] — reporting a Gnostic theogony taken from Theodoret and King: Ilda-Baoth's seven angels made the seven heavens. In her own voice she adds that Christians held a plurality of heavens from the first, citing Paul.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/14 Chapter IX The Vedas and the Bible#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_417|vol. 2, p. 417]] — her own generalisation, backed by Julian and Lydus: every ancient people set the Demiurge over the seventh heaven.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/10 Chapter VI Esoteric Doctrines of Buddhism Parodied in Christianity#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Greekch4|vol. 2, ch. VI]] — her own claim: the Zohar's "Palace of Love", Moksha, the Pleroma, Nirvana and the Christian "Kingdom of Heaven" are names for one goal of the purified soul.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/09 Chapter V Mysteries of the Kabala#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_234|vol. 2, p. 234]] — her comparison of Exodus 24:10 with the sapphire-paved heaven of Brahma.

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Gaskell and Hall | Both describe an ascent: the ego to the buddhic plane, the soul through seven spheres to a door in the skull | Hall does not cite Gaskell. A plane of consciousness and a planetary heaven are different schemes |
| Gaskell and Blavatsky | Both make heaven a condition reached, not a locality | Blavatsky's point is polemical (Christianity renamed an older doctrine). No mutual citation found |
| Bullinger against the rest | The heavens are the visible stars, a second scripture, and the place of Christ's throne | A contrast. He reads the signs as prophecy, not as stages of the soul |
| Plural heavens | Hall's planetary heavens, Blavatsky's seven heavens and Paul's "third heaven" all count levels | Gaskell's entry is singular, and the counts differ (seven, ten) |
