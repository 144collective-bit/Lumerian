# Christ

This is a **shared research hub**, not a universal definition. Each reading stays attributed to its source. Gaskell dictionary text is `machine-extracted-unreviewed` OCR unless a note says otherwise. Cross-source comments below are **editorial comparison** unless labelled as an explicit citation. Shared wording or a similar symbol is not evidence that one author used or influenced the other.

aliases in vault: Christos, Higher Self (Gaskell’s usage), inner / solar god-man (Hall’s usage).

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

A title and principle that, in the sources already in this vault, is sometimes treated as more than the biographical Jesus. The sources do **not** use the word in the same way.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Primary entry: [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CHRIST|CHRIST]] (`gaskell-1960-entry-christ`; EPUB pages [[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_160|page_160]]–[[Supporting Material/Gaskell/Source Text/page_161|page_161]]).

> Christ, or the Higher Self, is the fandamental principle underlying the idea of personal Godhood ; and is the Divine Life which proceeds fortu from the Absolute, as the Son from the Father…

Related Gaskell entries (not collapsed into this one): [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CHRISTOS AND CHRESTOS|CHRISTOS AND CHRESTOS]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/C/CHRIST-PRINCIPLE|CHRIST-PRINCIPLE]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/J/JESUS OF THE GOSPELS|JESUS OF THE GOSPELS]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/A/ATONEMENT OF CHRIST|ATONEMENT OF CHRIST]], [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/B/BIRTH OF CHRIST|BIRTH OF CHRIST]].

## From E. W. Bullinger, The Witness of the Stars (6 November 2026)

Bullinger’s whole book is a Christian reading of the constellations as a picture of the promised Seed — the child of Virgo, later the coming Prince — identified with Jesus the Messiah. Primary source passages: [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/05 Chapter I The Sign VIRGO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00006|VIRGO]] and Coma (including the Albumazar IHESU / CHRISTOS report, which is a tradition he recounts, not a vault-verified manuscript). Verses he cites include [[Isaiah 007#^kjv-isa-7-14|Isaiah 7:14]], [[Isaiah 009#^kjv-isa-9-6|Isaiah 9:6]], and [[Matthew 001#^kjv-mat-1-23|Matthew 1:23]].

- Tier: explicit citation of those verses; source-specific interpretation of the figures; Albumazar remains cited tradition.
- Limit: This does not turn Gaskell’s interior Christ-principle, or Hall’s Christos language, into the same claim. Star-name etymologies remain his.
- See [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Evidence/Crossrefs/Supported Connections|supported connections]].

## From H. P. Blavatsky, Isis Unveiled — Volume 1, Chapter III (6 November 2026)

Reporting Berti's documents against Draper's atheist reading, she says Bruno held Jesus to have been a magician in Porphyry's and Cicero's sense: a man of *divina sapientia*, not a worker of breaches in natural law ([[08 Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_96|pages 93-98]]). That is Bruno's view as she reports it, not a vault doctrine about Jesus.

- Tier: `source-specific interpretation` of her Bruno material.
- Limit: Not a reading of unread Volume 2 chapters on Christianity, and not Bullinger's messianic Seed already on this hub.
- See [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Notes/Chapter III Blind Leaders of the Blind - Analytical Note|Chapter III analytical note]].
