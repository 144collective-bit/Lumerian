# Earth

## Working definition (editorial scaffolding)

Earth taken alone, apart from its pairing with heaven (for the pair see [[Symbols & Concepts/Heaven and Earth|Heaven and Earth]]). Gaskell gives the word four separate entries, and they do not agree in level: earth is primordial matter, the physical plane, the lower nature that must be tilled, and also a sustaining Mother he places on the buddhic plane. Hall mostly treats earth as an element and the lowest sphere; Blavatsky treats it as a living mother with her own cycle; Bullinger means the literal earth that Christ will judge and restore. The main split is low versus maternal inside Gaskell, and symbol versus planet between the sources.

## Gaskell (OCR; unreviewed)

Four entries, each with its own definition (OCR exact):

- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/E/EARTH ,-PRIMORDIAL|EARTH ,—PRIMORDIAL]] — "A symbol of matter which, when acted upon by Spirit, takes forms and qualities for manifestation." Reads Genesis 1:1–2 as Spirit and Matter differentiating before involution begins.
- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/E/EARTH (SPECIALISED)|EARTH (SPECIALISED)]] — "A symbol of the physical nature, or the physical plane, as the lowest plane of manifestation." Reads Genesis 1:22 as discipline of the higher emotions through the physical nature.
- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/E/EARTH, THE GREAT SUSTAINING MOTHER|EARTH, THE GREAT SUSTAINING MOTHER]] — "A symbol of the productive buddhic nature as the Divine expression upon the astral and physical planes." Supported from the Homa Yasht, the Satapatha Brahmana, a Homeric hymn and a work cited as `Apoc. Rev.`
- [[Reference/Gaskell/Entries/E/EARTH|EARTH]] — "A symbol of the lower nature of the soul, which requires ‘‘ tilling ”’ and ‘‘sowing’’ to produce good « fruit.”" (quotation marks mangled by OCR; the machine-selected definition then runs on into the Genesis quotation). The verse is printed `GEN. ili. 23`, an OCR error for iii. 23.

Two cautions on the last entry. Its page files (`page_337`–`page_338`) sit far from the other three (`page_244`–`page_245`), so the headword is probably truncated; that is an inference, not checked against the scan. And its bridge note, [[Studies/Bible–Gaskell Cross References/Bridges/earth__genesis-3-23|EARTH → Genesis 3:23]], was first resolved to Genesis 50:23 because the OCR prints the chapter as `ili`; it was corrected to Genesis 3:23 on 12 November 2026, which is the verse the entry quotes.

## King James Bible

- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001#^kjv-gen-1-2|Genesis 1:2]] — earth "without form, and void"; Gaskell's text for primordial earth (his bridge points to the whole chapter).
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 001#^kjv-gen-1-22|Genesis 1:22]] — cited under EARTH (SPECIALISED); [[Studies/Bible–Gaskell Cross References/Bridges/earth-specialised__genesis-1-22|bridge]].
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 003#^kjv-gen-3-17|Genesis 3:17]] — "cursed is the ground"; Blavatsky quotes the curse (vol. 2, p. 420) without a printed reference.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/01 Genesis/Genesis 003#^kjv-gen-3-23|Genesis 3:23]] — Adam sent "to till the ground"; the verse actually quoted in Gaskell's EARTH entry.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/19 Psalms/Psalms 096#^kjv-psa-96-13|Psalms 96:13]] — "he cometh to judge the earth"; Bullinger's text for Boötes.
- [[Books/King James Bible (Project Gutenberg 10)/45 Romans/Romans 010#^kjv-rom-10-18|Romans 10:18]] — "their sound went into all the earth"; Bullinger's Introduction.

## Hall — *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*

- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Elements and Their Inhabitants#^hall-stota-pdf-320|PDF 320]] — reporting Paracelsus: each element is twofold, and earth has a fixed lower part and a "rarefied, mobile, and virtual" higher part.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Life and Philosophy of Pythagoras#^hall-stota-pdf-199|PDF 199]]–[[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Life and Philosophy of Pythagoras#^hall-stota-pdf-200|200]] — Pythagoras denied the earth the centre of the universe and put a sphere of divine fire there.
- [[Books/The Secret Teachings of All Ages (Manly P. Hall)/Text/The Theory and Practice of Alchemy Part Two#^hall-stota-pdf-498|PDF 498]] — a rendering of the Emerald Table that Hall reproduces makes the nurse of the One Thing "a spirituous earth", to be separated from the dense.

## Bullinger — *The Witness of the Stars*

- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/15 Chapter I The Sign TAURUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00039|Taurus]] — the sign foretells Messiah as "the coming Judge of all the Earth".
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/05 Chapter I The Sign VIRGO#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00009|Boötes]] — the Coming One, read through Psalm 96:13.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/11 Chapter II The Sign AQUARIUS#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00026|Aquarius]] — Christ will pour out literal waters, remove the curse and fill the whole earth with blessing; he insists the physical promises are not exhausted by a spiritual sense.
- [[Books/The Witness of the Stars (E. W. Bullinger)/Text/03 Introduction#^bullinger-wots-pgepubid00004|Introduction]] — in Psalm 19 he distinguishes "the earth (as created)" from "the world (as inhabited)".

## Blavatsky — *Isis Unveiled*

- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 1 Science/Text/13 Chapter VIII Some Mysteries of Nature#^blavatsky-isis-v1-Page_255|vol. 1, p. 255]] — reporting "the magians", with evident agreement: the "fecund mother Earth" is born, brings forth, and dies like her children, her spirit returning to its source.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/14 Chapter IX The Vedas and the Bible#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_420|vol. 2, p. 420]] — ascribed to "the ancients" and then stated as her own: each world returns to a *spiritual* earth, and our planet too had its "Fall" when matter defied spirit.
- [[Books/Isis Unveiled (H. P. Blavatsky)/Volume 2 Theology/Text/16 Chapter X The Devil-Myth#^blavatsky-isis-v2-Page_490|vol. 2, p. 490]] — citing "the oldest philosophers" and Sanchoniathon: the earth, serpent-like, casts its skin after each pralaya.

## Cross-source comparison (editorial)

| Relation | Supported observation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Gaskell with himself | Four entries place earth at four levels, from formless matter to buddhic Mother | Do not pick one as "the" meaning. All four are unreviewed OCR, and one headword is doubtful |
| Gaskell's Mother and Blavatsky's mother Earth | Both use maternal language | Gaskell means a principle in consciousness; Blavatsky means the planet and its spirit. No citation either way |
| Gaskell's lowest plane and Hall's element | Both set earth at the bottom of a scale | Hall, reporting Paracelsus, also gives earth a spiritual half. Hall does not cite Gaskell |
| Bullinger against the rest | Earth is the literal scene of judgment and restoration | A contrast. He rejects spiritualising the physical promises |
| Citation status | Genesis 1:22 is a clean bridge | The Genesis 3:23 bridge rests on a corrected OCR numeral (`ili` read as `iii`), not on a page-image check |
