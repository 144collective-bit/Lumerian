# Concept hub candidates — 12 November 2026

Ranked proposals for new shared hubs in `Symbols & Concepts/`. Nothing here is built yet.

**How the ranking works.** A term is a Gaskell headword (its part before the first comma or bracket). *Entries pointing to it* counts the Gaskell entries whose See-references name it. The other columns count the chapters of each source whose text contains the word or its plural. A term must appear in at least two of the four other sources. Terms that already have a hub are left out, and so are everyday words whose text matches are mostly not the symbol (for example *Will*, *Set*, *Name*) and the broad terms *God*, *Soul*, *Spirit*, *Life*, *Death*, *Mind*, *Body*, *World*, which touch nearly every entry. Built by `_lumerian_build_logs/rank_hub_candidates.py`.

**Limits.** The counts are word matches, not judgements that the sources mean the same thing by the word. Common words (for example *Man*, *Earth*) score high partly because they are common.
