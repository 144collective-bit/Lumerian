## Strongest new connections (examples)

- **Baptism of water, print 91:** OCR `Mart. xxviii. 19` is printed **MAT. xxviii. 19**. New correction note: [[Studies/Bible–Gaskell Cross References/Citation Corrections/baptism-of-water__matthew-28-19-image-validated]]. Gaskell’s English (“make disciples… into the name…”) does not copy the installed KJV (“teach… in the name…”).
- **Baptism of fire, print 92:** opening printed **Christhood** (OCR `Christhoo`); quotation matches Matthew 3:11. MAT/Mar abbreviation after the lemma-quote is noted, not treated as Mark 3:11.
- **Veil:** image-checked Veil of Isis + Proverbs 8:23, set beside the sanctuary veil (Exodus 26; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3; 10:19–20) and Hall’s veiled Isis (PDF 124), with the differences labelled.
- **Body as temple:** Gaskell TEMPLE OF GOD → 1 Corinthians 3:16 beside John 2:19–21 and Hall’s body-shaped temple (PDF 220–221).
- **Wisdom / Sophia / Isis:** Proverbs 8 bridges plus Hall’s Plutarch etymology (PDF 120) and “Sophia, the Virgin of Wisdom” (PDF 130), without collapsing Gaskell’s split entries.
- **Quaternary / Cross:** Gaskell’s image-checked CROSS opening (“Divine Ray passing through the quaternary”) beside Hall’s tetrad/tetractys and four-armed elemental cross — not equated.
- **Serpent / tree:** John 3:14 (already print-validated) pointed back to Numbers 21:8–9 as the Gospel’s own reference; the tree of life remains a separate symbol.
